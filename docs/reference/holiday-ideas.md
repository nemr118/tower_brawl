# Holiday ideas for later (kept 2026-10-25)

The user, after the Halloween build: "Note the other ideas for the other seasons and holidays." Halloween got an arena (THE HAUNTED HOUSE), power-ups that fit it (TRICK OR TREAT, PUMPKIN BOMBS, BAT SWARM), a friendly thing (the black cat), a costume you turn into (GHOSTFACE), and the ghosts. Each holiday below gets the same set, so they feel just as full. The research these grew from: [[dynamic-ideas]].

## What Halloween taught us (rules for every holiday)
- **Rare and special.** The cat comes in a quarter of rounds; GHOSTFACE in about 1 trick-or-treat box in 3. A holiday thing should be a treat, not noise.
- **You can always get away.** A warning first (the bats screech, the lightning counts down), and a dodge or a shield helps.
- **Easy to read at 90 px.** One strong shape and colour each: the pumpkin, the bat cloud, the white mask on the black robe.
- **The bots can use it.** Bots seek every orb and play GHOSTFACE, so a kid playing with bots sees everything too.
- **The same rules on the phone.** Anything that moves your own fighter (wind, ice, low jumps) must run in `predict.gd` too.
- **Switch on by date.** A THEME button in the lobby: AUTO (the server's date), OFF, or pick any holiday. The server sends it, so all phones match.

## Thanksgiving (Nov 2-30)
- **Arena: THE HARVEST BARN.** Hay bales to stand on, a loft, falling leaves (the rain effect, slowed), a big moon over the fields.
- **Power-ups:** PIE BOMBS (the bomb arrow as a pie that splats a slowing cream puddle); CORN POP (a burst of popcorn arrows); LEFTOVERS (eat to get your last power-up back).
- **Friendly thing:** a turkey that runs about the floor. Catch it (touch it) for a random power-up; it gobbles and runs off.
- **Costume:** THE SCARECROW: crows circle you and peck arrows away, but you are slow.

## Christmas and winter (Dec 1-30)
- **Arena: THE SNOWY BRIDGES.** Snow on the bridges, icy ledges (slippery: needs the phone's prediction), a big tree with lights.
- **Power-ups:** SNOWBALL ARROWS (freeze whoever they hit for 2 s, no kill); PRESENT (like TRICK OR TREAT: open it for a gift or a lump of coal, which makes you slow); SLEIGH BELLS (the next orb drops by your feet).
- **Friendly thing:** a snowman. Stand by it 2 s and it gives you a snow shield. Arrows knock its head off.
- **Costume:** THE YETI: big, a stomp that shakes the floor, and you can only be hurt from behind.
- **Orb drop:** Santa's sleigh flies over and drops the orbs instead of the lightning.

## New Year (Dec 31 - Jan 2)
- **Arena:** the city roof at midnight, fireworks in the sky.
- **Power-ups:** FIREWORK ARROWS (they fly up and burst into stars); CONFETTI (a cloud that hides you, like smoke but sparkly).
- **Special:** a countdown voice before the match point round: "3, 2, 1, HAPPY NEW YEAR!"

## Valentine's Day (Feb 7-14)
- **Arena:** a garden with pink lanterns and a fountain; hearts instead of dust.
- **Power-ups:** CUPID ARROWS (they tie two players together for 3 s, no kill); LOVE SHIELD (a shield for you and the nearest player).
- **Friendly thing:** a bunch of balloons. Grab it and float up slowly for a few seconds.
- **Mode:** PAIRS (teams of 2) with heart badges.

## Easter and spring (the week up to Easter Sunday)
- **Arena: THE MEADOW.** A sunny keep with flowers, a burrow under the floor.
- **Power-ups:** EGG BOMBS (crack open into a random small effect); BUNNY HOP (a double jump for a round: the bots need routes for it first).
- **Friendly thing:** a rabbit hops out of the burrow; follow it to an egg with a power-up.
- **Mode:** EGG HUNT: eggs hide around the arena, the first to 3 eggs wins the round.

## Summer and the Fourth of July (Jul 1-5)
- **Arena:** a boardwalk over the sea at sunset, fireworks over the water.
- **Power-ups:** FIRECRACKER ARROWS (a string of small pops); ROMAN CANDLE (3 small arrows in a fan); WATER BALLOON (a splash that pushes everyone away, no kill).
- **Friendly thing:** a beach ball that bounces about; hit it with an arrow and it bounces far.
- **Costume:** star capes for the winner.

## Birthdays (any day: the host page picks whose)
- Party hats on everyone, balloons in the sky, the birthday person's name on the lobby.
- **Power-up:** CAKE (a shield made of cake that comes back once).
- **Special:** the birthday person's fighter wears a crown, and the winner screen sings (when the game has sound).

## Costumes you turn into (like GHOSTFACE)
The family liked turning into a character. Ideas for other times (each: no bow, one special move, two hits to end):
- **The witch** (Halloween, another year): flies on a broom, drops potion bottles.
- **The mummy** (Halloween): slow, wraps a nearby player in bandages for 2 s.
- **The snowman** (Christmas): rolls into a big snowball that knocks players over.
- **The robot** (any time): a short laser in front, sparks when hit.

The repo is public, so a character from a film or a book is the user's call each time (GHOSTFACE was: "Make sure the likeness is very obvious").
