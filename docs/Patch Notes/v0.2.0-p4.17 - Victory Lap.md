# v0.2.0-p4.17 - Victory Lap

**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Phase:** the look for the next play night. The user asked: "Any more work we can do on generating graphics for the next play night? Let's make it look great." They picked all four jobs (big round banners, the winner's screen backdrop, painted winner portraits, award pictures in the lobby) and added: "Look at any UI elements that may make sense to have comfyui make." From the BEFORE/AFTER pictures they said "yes" to all five pieces. · **Harness:** the proofs below

> The round's big words now look like the lobby's title: a colour fade, a dark edge, a glow, and a pop when they come up, with a painted ribbon for the second line. The winner's screen has a painted terrace with fireworks (a spooky one in October) and the winner's painted hooded archer, in their colour, behind the podium. In the lobby, each card shows its award's picture, and the buttons have pictures too.

## Changes
- **The round banners** (`client/hud_banner.gd`, polish `banners2`): ROUND N (the arena's name on the ribbon, GET READY under it), FIGHT!, a round's winner (the name in its colour, WINS THE ROUND on the ribbon), DRAW, and in the replay the match's winner (WINS THE MATCH on the ribbon) and MATCH POINT.
  - The lobby title's look: its heavy font, a colour fade with the light that sweeps across, a dark edge, a coloured rim, a glow, a drop shadow; a pop when a banner comes up.
  - The ribbon is a ComfyUI picture; its flat middle stretches to fit the words.
  - The letters are drawn at three sizes only and scaled. A font keeps a 2 MB sheet of letters for every size and outline it draws (p4.15's iPhone lesson).
- **The winner's screen** (`client/winner_view.gd`):
  - polish `winner_backdrop`: a painted castle terrace with fireworks fills the window. From 1 October to 2 November it is the spooky one, with the moon and jack-o'-lanterns (the lobby's dress);
  - polish `winner_portrait`: the winner's painted hooded archer, in their colour, rises up behind a wider podium in place of the fighter; the words sit a little higher;
  - the pictures load when the screen first comes up, and only the winner's hero is kept (the iPhones' memory).
- **The lobby:**
  - polish `award_badges`: after a match, each seat card shows its award's picture in a round gold badge beside the fighter (`client/lobby_cards.gd`);
  - polish `ui_icons`: pictures on the buttons: a lightning bolt on POWER-UPS, a robot on BOTS, a speed gauge on BOT LEVEL, a door on LEAVE, a paint palette on LOOK, a game controller on CONTROLS (`client/lobby_art.gd`, the button's `pic`); and a painted crown on the winner's podium.
- **The ComfyUI pictures** (`tools/comfy/`): `run_winner.py` (4 castle and 4 spooky backdrops; the hooded archer in the 8 seat colours, one seed a try, 4 tries) and `run_ui.py` (the icons, a crown, a ribbon): 72 pictures. `make_assets.py` makes the game's files from the picks. It cuts the heroes off their background with ISNet (anime) on the CPU, with no GPU.
- **Test tools:** the page prints `🏁 [Round]`, `🎬 [Replay]` and `🏆 [Winner]` lines. `tools/v2_shot.py --burst SECS` keeps every frame the page draws (the browser's screencast); `--until "TEXT@D1,D2"` shoots D1 and D2 seconds after a console line with TEXT, and `"TEXT@cast:SECS"` keeps the frames after it.
