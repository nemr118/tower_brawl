# v0.0.18 - Playtest Fixes: Bubble Spawn, Arrows, Bots, HUD

**Date:** 2026-09-03 · **Phase:** Phase 3b step 5, playtest fixes · **Harness:** 17/17 (--restart-each)
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.18.json](../harness_report_v0.0.18.json)

> The first fix build after the family playtest ([[Playtest v0.0.17]]): fighters come back in a floating bubble, arrows are never lost, bots lock in again by themselves, and the top bar shows the crown numbers.

## Changes

**Bubble spawn** (`player.gd`, `arena.gd`)
- The problem: fighters came back on four fixed spots. When the arena was upside down, a spot could be inside the floor or out of view.
- Now a fighter comes back in the air, in the upper middle of the arena, at a random spot in the middle half. It sits inside a bubble and floats down slowly.
- Inside the bubble you can move left and right. Hold Down (S or the down arrow) to drop faster.
- The bubble works like a knight's shield: swords bounce off, and arrows, fireballs and kunai fly back at the shooter. Camping the spawn is a bad idea now.
- The bubble pops after 5 seconds, when the feet touch the ground, or the moment you attack, dash or use a special. Your attack still happens in that same moment.
- Every screen picks the same spot: the random number is seeded from the round, the player, the lives left and the arena flips. No new network packet was needed.

**Arrows** (`arrow.gd`)
- An arrow that hits a fighter now keeps flying and sticks into the next platform. Before, it vanished with the fighter, and the archer could never pick it up again.
- A stuck arrow now becomes part of the platform it hit. When the arena turns, the arrow turns with it. Before, arrows stayed floating in the air where the platform used to be.

**Bots** (`global.gd`)
- Bots lock in again on their own a moment after a match ends and everyone is back in the lobby. Before, they sat in the lobby forever and had to be restarted by hand.

**Top bar** (`arena.gd`)
- The crown number was hidden under the crown picture. So was the first letter of each name ("ndrew"). Each label now starts its text 20 px in, past the little icon. The number, the name and the "x" in lives all show.
- The old R key that wiped the crown counters on one screen is gone.

**Jump trap** (`player.gd`)
- In the playtest a fighter on a platform sometimes could not jump with W until it walked off. We could not find why in the code. This build prints one console line, starting with `🪤 [JumpTrap]`, the moment it happens, with the numbers we need. Please copy that line into the next playtest sheet if you see it.
