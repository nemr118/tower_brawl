# v0.1.4 - Twin Sticks

**Date:** 2026-09-08 · **Phase:** twin-stick phone controls, and the duck fix · **Harness:** 24/25: smoke failed on `timeout.assign-id` because two leftover screenshot bots still held slots when the suite started (the test setup, not the build); the other 24 clean, no Minor; counted as a pass (user, 2026-09-08)
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.1.4.json](../harness_report_v0.1.4.json) · the sheet: [[Playtest — Master Validation Suite v0.1]] §7

> The phone has two layouts now. The arc of buttons plus one stick from v0.1.1 stays. New: twin sticks. The left one walks, jumps (tilt up) and dashes (push past the rim); the right one aims and shoots (push past its rim); SPEC sits in the corner; a SWAP button under the top bar switches and remembers the choice. Building it found a v0.1.3 bug: the duck flickered on and off every frame, so the look-down never came and the small hitbox was only there half the time. Fixed with a short floor grace.

## The user's picture (2026-09-08)
Left stick: one big circle; inside it the fighter walks where the stick points, past the edge a dash that way, above 45 degrees a jump. Right stick: aim, a fairly large circle, past its edge a shot. A special button lower right. A small button upper left switches between the arc layout and the twin sticks, and the choice survives a reload. Duck and look must stay reachable: the left stick straight down ducks, the right stick straight up held looks up.

## Changes
- **Two layouts in one script** (`scripts/touch_controls.gd`, `layout` = `arc` or `twin`). More than half the file is shared (the show-on-touch logic, the player prefix, the press and release helpers, the axis simulation, the button styling, the drawing), so it stays one script. The scene file is untouched: the twin layout hides three of the four Buttons and moves the special; the SWAP button is made in code.
- **The left stick** (`MOVE_RADIUS` 80, floating under the thumb in the left half). Past the dead zone (8 percent) the unit direction goes out on the p<N> move actions; the x part walks. Within `DOWN_DEG` 30 of straight down a clean (0, 1) goes out: the duck, and after 1.5 s the look-down (`player.gd`, as before). Above `JUMP_DEG` 45 from horizontal, either side, one jump press when the stick enters the wedge; it re-arms under `JUMP_REARM_DEG` 35 or back in the dead zone, so a wobble on the line does not double-jump. `RIM_PAD` 10 px past the rim: one dash press, direction = the stick (`_start_dash` reads the move axes); re-armed inside `RIM_REARM` 85 percent, so walking at full tilt does not chain dashes. In the down sector the rim sends a jump press instead of a dash: down + jump = the drop-through on a ledge. The trade: no straight-down air dash on this layout (the down diagonals still dash).
- **The right stick** (`AIM_RADIUS` 70, floating in the right half). Any tilt past the dead zone sets `Global.touch_aim`, a full circle, and it stays when the thumb lifts. The left stick never touches the aim, so walking left while shooting right works (the thing section 1 of the sheet said was missing). Straight up held 1.5 s = the look-up (`player.gd`, as before). 10 px past the rim: one attack press, re-armed inside 85 percent. One flick, one shot; holding at the rim does not keep firing.
- **The taps** (`_tap`). A rim or wedge press is `Input.action_press` now and `action_release` `TAP_FRAMES` 2 physics frames later, so `player.gd`'s `is_action_just_pressed` sees it once whatever the node order. A tap never releases an action a thumb still holds.
- **SPEC** at (612, 322), radius 22, the corner. A touch there wins over the aim stick.
- **SWAP** at (26, 66), radius 14, under the top bar (it ends at y 44). It releases every held action, swaps, redraws and saves `towerbrawl_layout` = `arc` / `twin` through `Global._storage_set` (localStorage on the web). Read in `_ready`, so a reload keeps it; the console prints `🎮 [Layout] twin saved=true`. Default `arc`.
- **The duck fix** (`scripts/player.gd`, `DUCK_GRACE_S` 0.1). The cause: a collision shape change costs one `is_on_floor()` frame when the body is at exact rest on a floor, whatever the change (a new shape resource, the same one resized in place, a bottom-half polygon with the origin unchanged, with or without `apply_floor_snap()`: all tested in headless Godot, all lose the frame). v0.1.3 ducked only while `is_on_floor()`, so the duck went off the frame after it went on, on again the frame after that, for ever: the look-down timer reset every frame, the small hitbox was there half the time, the fighter wobbled a third of a pixel. Now a ducked fighter keeps the duck through 0.1 s without a floor. Nothing else changed: the same 14 x 12 shape at (0, 2), the same `FLAG_DUCK` bit.
- **Two tools.** `tools/webshot.py`: Chromium headless over CDP, a phone user agent, touch emulation, the layout in localStorage, the full-screen sheet removed, the page joins as a player, a named sequence of touches (`idle`, `sticks`, `moves`, `duck`, `keys`) with a screenshot after each step. `tools/duck_probe.gd`: a fighter on a floor with `p1_down` held, per-frame prints, a pass line when the duck holds 40 frames.

## Seen on the PC
- **The twin layout in Chromium headless** (`webshot.py`, the page as a real player against a headless turtle): SWAP under the top bar, SPEC in the corner, both circles at rest; the jump wedge lit gold with the thumb in it and the fighter in the air 150 ms later; the dash trail 100 ms after the rim; an arrow spent and flying after the aim rim; the duck squash 80 ms after the stick went straight down; the aim line staying where the thumb left it.
- **The duck bug, before the fix:** the same run held the stick down 2.8 s and the view never moved. A debug print showed `[DuckDbg] on=true floor=true frame=1898`, `on=false floor=false frame=1899`, and so on, every frame. The keyboard's S key did the same, so v0.1.3's duck and look-down were broken on every device. `duck_probe.gd` reproduced it in headless Godot in 10 s per try; four shape variants later the grace was the fix. After it, the web run shows the view 200 px lower at 2.8 s and back after the release.
- **The arc layout** still draws as before, with SWAP added (the `keys` run).
