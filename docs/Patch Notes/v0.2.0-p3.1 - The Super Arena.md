# v0.2.0-p3.1 - The Super Arena

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Phase:** P3 = G5, the super arena ([[v0.2-transformation-plan]] G5, the user's go before the sheet had answers) · **Harness:** the G5 proofs on the PC (below); **not deployed**
**Report:** `bench/tools/arena_probe.gd` (the rules, the routes, the bots' rounds, a map picture) and the server's report lines (`v2_server.jsonl`, now with the arena)

> Two arenas built for 8, our own designs: THE KEEP, a castle courtyard with walls to jump off and cover, and THE BRIDGES, open air with floating islands, long bridges and a pit in the middle. The lobby's ARENA button picks MIX (the two take turns each round) or one of them. A new tool, the arena probe, checks the clever-ledges rules on any layout. No spawn spot has a clear shot at another within 600 px. Every floor and ledge can be reached from every other and left two ways. The wrap is a way round. Left and right are balanced but not a mirror. It also plays bot rounds on the layout. The bench arena stays for the tests. Not on the laptop yet: the play night tests p2.1 first (the user's call).

## Changes
- **1. The arena probe** (`bench/tools/arena_probe.gd`). *Spawn fairness:* from each spot's bow to five points on every other body, through each wrap copy: a block stops the line, a ledge only from above (as an arrow sticks); no clear line under 600 px may stay. Each spot stands with room for the body, and none sits one drop above another (input_probe rule 27's test). *The routes:* a surface is a run of places to stand on one row. From every second place (and both ends) the sim's own movement runs 83 moves: walks off the edge (steering on or letting go), tapped and held jumps with steering at 0, 12 or 24 ticks, run-up jumps, drops through a ledge, wall jumps (up to 4, steering back to climb), ledge grabs, dodges in 7 ways from the air and 5 from the ground, a dodge jump. Where each lands is an edge. Every surface must be joined to every other, each must have 2 ways out, and the wrap must carry routes right, left and down. It lists the routes only a wall jump or a dodge makes. *Balance:* as many spots left as right, the blocks and ledges per half within 10%, some cells not mirrored. `--rounds N`: N rounds of 8 bots (one life, the walls at 25 s): length, draws, causes, where bots got stuck. `--png DIR`: a flat map. The bench arena is reported, never a fail.
- **2. Two arenas** in `sim/level.gd`, as text maps: 36 lines of 64 characters (`#` a block, `=` a ledge, a digit a spawn spot, a..h a torch). Layout 0 is the bench arena, built from its pieces as before (the same grid, checked by its sha256) and still the default for the tests and the benchmark page. **THE KEEP** (1): the keep, a roof on two short legs with merlons, over the floor; the watchtower block above it; side blocks with steps; pillars hanging from the ceiling; two gaps in the floor; one passage in each side wall; 8 spawn spots. **THE BRIDGES** (2): floating islands and long bridges, one wide pit in the middle of the floor, two passages in each side wall with a ledge under each; 9 spawn spots. `LevelScript.PLAY = [1, 2]` is the lobby's list. `stands()` and `one_drop()` moved into `level.gd`; rule 27 checks every arena. Chest spots wait for the treasure (G10).
- **3. The arena in the lobby and the match.** The ARENA button (beside LOOK; the seat rows are 52 px now): MIX, THE KEEP, THE BRIDGES, for everyone, like BOTS. The server: `arena_pick`, `--arena mix|keep|bridges|bench|N`, the JSON `arena {n}`, `seats {arena, arena_now}`. Each round sets the sim's arena; MIX goes on across matches; the lobby shows the arena that comes next. The snapshot header carries the round's arena (one byte after the walls). The page swaps its tiles and torches to the arena of the snapshot it draws (`world.set_arena`). The prediction runs on the arena of each `T_OWN`'s snapshot, and the arrows' copy follows the newest snapshot. The ROUND banner names the arena, and so do the `[ROUND] N start on THE KEEP` log line and the report.
- **4. Test tools.** The page option `?arena=N`; `play_probe.gd --arena=N` (its move proof's local copy runs on the round's arena, and it now picks a run a wall does not stop short); `v2_synth.py` reads the new byte; `v2_shot.py --round-wait` (and its LOOK tap moved with the button).
- **5. The bots.** No brain change (that is G6). The dumb brain got stuck in pockets under platforms on a wall: it jumps into the underside while it chases someone above. So the arenas changed. The keep's side platforms reach the floor, and the bridges' lower passage shelves are ledges you jump up through.

## Found on the way (reported, not fixed)
- **A match with 8 runs long.** The 7-bot match took 12 rounds (first to 3 points, the points spread over 8), about 6 minutes. D2's open question (points to win with 5 to 8 players) and the sheet's rounds line.
- **The bot brain gets stuck under a platform on a wall with a low gap beneath.** Designed out of these two arenas. A brain that finds routes is G6; the probe's route map could feed it.
- **play_probe misses a lobby the fake players leave within a tick** (`v2_synth.py --start` presses START at once), so "lobby back" fails in an 8-socket run with `--goal 1`. A test-tool limit.
- **Headless Chromium draws the page at 7 to 9 fps,** so `v2_shot.py`'s round shot lands on FIGHT unless `--round-wait` is shorter (3.2 s here).
