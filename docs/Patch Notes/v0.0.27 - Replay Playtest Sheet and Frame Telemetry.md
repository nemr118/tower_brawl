# v0.0.27 - Replay Playtest Sheet and Frame Telemetry

**Date:** 2026-09-04 · **Phase:** v0.1.0 list, step 1: the replay playtest sheet and the fps numbers · **Harness:** 21/21, no Minor (second full run; the first run had a one-off `fleet.client-silent`, kept as `harness_report_v0.0.27_run1.json`, backlog 12 in [[PASSDOWN]])
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.27.json](../harness_report_v0.0.27.json)

> The test sheet for the replay playtest on phone and PC, and one new field at the end of every `📈 [NetStats]` line that says how fast the screen runs: `fps draw=59.8 phys=60.0 worst=21ms hitches=0`. No game rule changed. The replay itself is untouched.

## Changes
- **New sheet [[Playtest v0.0.26]]**, same shape as [[Playtest v0.0.17]]. Seven parts: the device table, the 6.5 s gap, the slow-motion window (1 s before the kill, 1 s after, half speed), reading the caption and the labels on a phone, the paused world (keys do nothing, clean new round, no leftovers, top bar still, spectators, platforms), the odd cases (hidden tab, a kill while turning, a kill on the seam, two kills in one second, a reload during the replay, a late joiner), and a numbers table to copy the `NetStats` and `📼 [Replay]` lines into. The verdict block names the three numbers the playtest may change (`REPLAY_ROUND_DELAY`, the K-30 / K+30 slow-motion frames, the caption size) and the bug builds that follow.
- **Frame-time telemetry in `scripts/global.gd`.** `Global` is an autoload that runs in every scene, so it counts its own `_process` calls (one drawn picture) and a new `_physics_process` (one physics tick) between two `NetStats` reports. `_ft_sample(delta)` also keeps the slowest frame in ms and counts frames over `FRAME_HITCH_MS = 50.0`. `_report_net_stats` adds `"fps": {draw, phys, worst_ms, hitches}` to the `net_stats_updated` dictionary and prints ` | fps draw=%.1f phys=%.1f worst=%.0fms hitches=%d` at the end of the line, then `_ft_reset()`. The count runs before the `--no-net` early return, so the compile-check client counts too. Nothing reads the field yet; the harness regex for `puppets=` ends at `pn=` and is not touched.
- **Why:** the replay draws 211 tape frames in about 5 s and the `📼 [Replay]` line already says `drawn` versus `frames`, but nothing said what the screen does the rest of the time. A phone playtest now gives one number for a normal round and one for a replay, and that number feeds the optimisation plan (step A of the plan in [[PASSDOWN]]).
- **Docs:** `obsidian_notes.md` section 6 explains the field.

## Metrics
| Measure | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Fields at the end of a `NetStats` line | `... dips=0 pn=0` | `... dips=0 pn=0 \| fps draw=143.2 phys=60.0 worst=83ms hitches=1` |
| Headless client on the PC, first 5 s (scene load) | - | `draw=143.2 phys=60.0 worst=83ms hitches=1` |
| Headless client on the PC, steady | - | `draw=145.0 phys=60.0 worst=7ms hitches=0` |
| Game rules, packets, server | unchanged | unchanged |

A headless client has no vsync, so `draw` sits well above 60. A browser tab is capped by the screen, so a PC should read about 60 and a phone 60 or 120. The first interval always carries one hitch from the scene load.
