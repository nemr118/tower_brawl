# v0.2.0-p4.16 - Crisp Phones

**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Phase:** after the second gathering night's logs ([[mobile-controls]] has them). The user asked: "Anything else we can do to make mobile controls as crisp as possible? Maybe benchmark brawlhalla and other platforms games." Then, about the night: "Another issue was a bit of warping. It wasn't bad, but sometimes characters would warp." From the logs and the research the user picked four changes ("iPhone speed, small control fixes, bow only on a pull, BUTTONS layout") and the ComfyUI pictures ("I like the ones you recommend"). · **Harness:** the proofs below

> iPhones run faster: no HIGH look on them (it ran at 16 fps, LOW at 60), and the arena is drawn a little smaller there. Phones get crisper controls: a quick DODGE tap is never lost, a shot goes where you aimed just before you lifted your thumb, and a thumb resting on the right side no longer freezes you. There is a new BUTTONS layout (pick it in the lobby: JUMP and DODGE buttons, the stick only runs). Other fighters no longer freeze and jump when the Wi-Fi stalls. And new pictures: the arena picker, the candy tricks' badges, a painted castle sky, and the loading picture changes with the season.

## What the night's logs showed
- iPhones ran slowly: a median 15 to 22 fps on HIGH, 22 to 30 on LOW for the bigger iPhones; one iPhone 60 fps on LOW but 16 on HIGH (picked by hand, so AUTO never stepped it down). The Androids 36 and 43 fps.
- Every phone saw snapshot gaps over 133 ms now and then (the worst 734 ms): the others froze, then jumped. The ROG's own Wi-Fi power save was already off; the gathering's Wi-Fi had pings of 47 ms (7 to 17 at home).
- Kids let go inside the ring on 20% to 37% of their right-thumb touches (the user 4%).

## Changes
- **The warping** (`client/net.gd`): when snapshots are late, the others go on at their last speed (6 ticks at most) instead of standing still, and a catch-up eases out over 0.1 s instead of a jump (a bed or a locker still jumps). The phone report carries `late_pct`, `eased`, `eased_px`.
- **iPhone speed** (`client/game_client.gd`): on iPhones and iPads the LOOK button offers AUTO, LOW and NORMAL (a HIGH kept from before becomes NORMAL); the arena is drawn at 0.75 of 1920 x 1080 there and scaled up (`?res=1` draws it full size). The phone report carries `res`.
- **The small control fixes:** a quick DODGE tap lasts 2 ticks (never lost between two ticks); a let-go takes the aim the thumb held 60 ms before, unless it moved a lot since (the let-go wobble); the page blocks the long-press popup, the grey tap flash and pull-to-refresh; a dodge pressed up to 6 ticks early (the cooldown, a ledge hang, a knock) goes when it can; the jump buffer is TowerFall's 6 ticks (was 5). The bots' route map rebuilt.
- **The bow only on a pull** (`client/touch_sticks.gd`): the bow comes up once the thumb has pulled about 2 mm, so a resting thumb or a tap roots nobody. The phone report counts those touches (`aim_taps`).
- **The BUTTONS layout** (the lobby's CONTROLS under LOOK, phones only, kept on the device; `?controls=buttons`): the stick only runs and its middle follows the thumb past the rim; JUMP in the bottom-right corner (held: a higher jump; with the stick down: drop through a ledge; its touch area runs to the screen's edges); DODGE up and to the left of it (the stick's way; a dark sweep shows its cooldown). CLASSIC is as before. The phone report carries `controls`.
- **The ComfyUI pictures the user picked:** the ARENA button shows the pick's painting and opens a picker of painted cards (`client/lobby_arenas.gd`); a candy's trick shows its own badge (PUMPKIN HEAD, BACKWARDS, TINY, GHOSTFACE); a painted pixel-style night behind THE KEEP and THE BRIDGES; the loading picture is the Halloween one from 1 October to 2 November and the castle at sunset the rest of the year.
- **Test knobs and tools:** the server's `--candy-trick N`; `v2_shot.py --lobby-tap X,Y`; `play_probe.gd --warps --stall=MS --stall-every=MS`; `pit_probe.gd` part 3 (BUTTONS). The night report names THE BOILER ROOM, a furnace and GHOSTFACE's knife. `input_probe`'s arrow check leaves out arrows that met a fighter (p4.10's arrows stay in the body they hit).
