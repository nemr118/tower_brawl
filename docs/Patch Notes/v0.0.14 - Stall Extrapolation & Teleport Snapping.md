# v0.0.14 - Stall Extrapolation & Teleport Snapping

**Date:** 2026-09-03 · **Phase:** Phase 3b, step 3 of 5 · **Harness:** three fleets PASS (see Metrics)
**Report:** before/after at heavy jitter below; step 2 numbers in [[v0.0.13 - Snapshot Interpolation]]

> When packets run late the puppet keeps going along its last velocity for up to 200 ms instead of stopping dead, and a blink or respawn is drawn as a step instead of a 50 ms glide. Heavy-jitter puppet jitter drops by another 2.5×.

## Changes
### Client (`scripts/player.gd`, `scripts/global.gd`)
- **Extrapolation on stall.** When the render clock passes the newest sample, the puppet follows the velocity of the last motion segment (the last two samples, only if they are 1–2 ticks apart and not a teleport) for at most `EXTRAP_MAX_TICKS = 4` (200 ms), then freezes at that point until a fresh sample arrives. The extrapolated position goes through the same unwrap / wrap as interpolation, so a puppet extrapolating across a seam wraps correctly. An idle gap is never extrapolated: the sender was standing still.
- **Teleport snapping.** A displacement over `TELEPORT_PX = 80` in one tick (mage blink 95 px, respawn, an idle hold ending in a jump) renders as an immediate step to the new sample instead of a glide across the segment. Such a segment also contributes no extrapolation velocity.
- **Telemetry:** new `extrap=<pct>%` field in `puppets=` (share of puppet-frames rendered past the newest sample, extrapolated or frozen). `stall=` keeps measuring starvation itself (no sample for 150 ms while the sender was moving), so it does not change with rendering: it says how bad the network was, `extrap` says how much of it was covered.
- **`--jitter-ms` no longer reorders.** The v0.0.12 model floored each packet's due time on the wall clock, but SceneTree timers count down by frame delta, so two timers could cross by one frame; the harness oracle caught it (`oracle.movement-out-of-order`, a Godot client's tick 164 after 165) in the first 2 + 2 loss run. The floor now comes from the previous timer's own remaining time. TCP never reorders, so the model must not either.

### Harness
- The fleet note reads `extrap` alongside `stall`. `--loss` (protocol bots dropping sends) works as a gap generator for the Godot clients' puppets: gaps of 1–2 ticks interpolate, longer ones hold-then-step.
