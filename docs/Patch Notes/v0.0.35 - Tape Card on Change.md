# v0.0.35 - Tape Card on Change

**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Phase:** optimisation Step C, build 2: the tape card goes out on change, at most once per 10 s while recording · **Harness:** 23/23 with `--restart-each`, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.35.json](../harness_report_v0.0.35.json) · fleet before: `fleet_before.json` and after: `fleet_after.json` (scratch, numbers below are the record)

> A screen no longer sends its `history_status` card (about 380 B) for every kill. A kill stamp marks the card dirty and `arena.gd` sends it at most once per 10 s while the tape records; the freeze and the replay start, skip and end still send at once. Two files changed: `scripts/arena.gd` and `tools/chaos_bots.py`. The game, the packets and the server are unchanged.

## The premise, corrected
Step B ([[optimisation_step_b_profiles]]) said the card went out "every 2.6 s whether or not the tape changed". There is no timer. `arena.gd` sent `tape.status_card()` in five places: on every kill stamp in `_on_net_player_died`, on the freeze in `_record_tape_frame`, and at replay start, skip and end. The 0.38 packets a second was the kill rate of that four-brain fleet. The per-kill send is the cost: every screen sends a card for every kill, and that card only feeds the deck's `● 5.0s ·2` stamp count. The server logs nothing for it and the harness never reads it; the freeze card carries the same `last` and `stamps` a second later.

## Changes
- **`scripts/arena.gd`.** New `TAPE_CARD_MIN_S = 10.0`, `_tape_card_dirty`, `_tape_card_sent_msec`. `_tape_report(send_now)` always prints the `📼 [Tape]` line (the harness reads it); with `send_now = false` (a kill stamp) it only sets the dirty flag. `_record_tape_frame` sends the dirty card once the floor has passed. `_send_tape_card()` is the one place that sends and it resets the flag and the clock; the freeze, `_start_replay`, `_skip_replay` and `_on_replay_finished` call it directly, so those cards are never held back.
- **What a deck watcher sees.** While a round records, the `Tape` column's stamp count (`·N`) refreshes at most every 10 s instead of at every kill. Frozen, playing, played and skipped states show as before.
- **`tools/chaos_bots.py`.** `GodotClient.stats()` counts `history_status` packets and bytes from the `out:` type lists; the fleet note prints `N tape cards sent` per client, so every report records the rate.

## Harness
- **Full suite:** 23/23 with `--restart-each`, no Minor. The `tape` and `replay` scenarios read `status.json` only after the freeze and the replay, and those cards still send at once.
- **Fleet before and after** (`--godot 4 --ai chaser,sniper,turtle,rusher --duration 90 --latency-ms 120 --jitter-ms 40`, both runs PASS): the table.
