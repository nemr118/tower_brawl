# v0.0.32 - Free-Fall Arena Shift

**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Phase:** v0.1.0 list, bug build 5: backlog 9 closed (option A, the free-fall arena shift) · **Harness:** 23/23 with --restart-each, no Minor · heavy fleet seed 13: 0 `🧗 [TopEdge]` lines, fails `fleet.bot-fall-loop` (see below)
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.32.json](../harness_report_v0.0.32.json) · heavy fleet run: [seed 13](../harness_report_v0.0.32_fleet_seed13.json)

> The platforms are not solid while the stage turns. A fighter standing on one drops through the turning stage and lands on the new layout when it locks. The ride out of the top of the screen (backlog 9) is gone. One file changed, `scripts/arena.gd`, 25 lines.

## The design call (backlog 9, option A)
- **The bug, in short.** The arena shift is a half turn of `Platforms` around `(320, 180)` over 2.5 s. The ground slabs and ledges sit 209 to 216 px from the pivot, half the screen height is 180 px, so they pass 29 to 36 px above the top edge mid-turn. A fighter standing on one rode it out of view for about a second. The bottom wrap to `y = -10` could also drop a fighter onto a slab passing there. The whole story with numbers: [[v0.0.31 - Top Edge Probe]].
- **The three options were:** A) the platforms are not solid during the turn, B) wrap a fighter carried above `y = -60` to the bottom, C) leave it. The user picked A: the smallest sure fix, and both ways in (the ride and the bottom wrap onto a passing slab) go away at once, because there is nothing solid up there any more.
- **Why `collision_layer` and not `collision_mask`.** A `StaticBody2D` never moves by physics, so its mask does nothing for it. The layer is what a fighter's `move_and_slide()` looks for. Zeroing the layer makes the platform invisible to every body that collides with it; the mask stays as it was, so nothing else changes.

## Changes
- **`scripts/arena.gd` `_set_platforms_solid(solid)` (new).** Loops over every `StaticBody2D` under `Platforms`. With `false` it stashes each body's `collision_layer` in the new `platform_layers` dictionary and sets the layer to 0. With `true` it gives each body its stashed layer back and clears the dictionary. The layer numbers are never written in code; whatever the scene says is what comes back.
- **`_activate_rotation`** calls `_set_platforms_solid(false)` right after `is_arena_rotating = true`, before the spin tween starts. When the 2.5 s timer ends and the spin is still ours, it calls `_set_platforms_solid(true)` next to `is_arena_rotating = false`.
- **`_finish_spin_now`** (a round start snapping the platforms into place, v0.0.20) also calls `_set_platforms_solid(true)`. Both ways a turn can end restore the platforms; a turn can never leave them soft.
- **What a player sees.** The shift banner comes up, the stage turns, and every fighter that was standing on a platform falls. Fighters that were already in the air keep falling. Everyone lands on the new layout when it locks, or falls out of the bottom and wraps to the top like any other fall. Puppets are drawn from the sender's packets as before, so other screens show the same thing.
- **The probe stays in.** `player.gd` `_probe_top_edge` and the `🧗 [TopEdge]` line are unchanged, so the fleet notes still say when a fighter touches something above the top edge. Zero lines is the pass mark for this build.
- **Nothing else changed:** packets, server, `bot_brain.gd`, the tape and the replay are as they were.

## Harness
- **Full suite:** 23/23 with `--restart-each`, no Minor, no top-edge notes. Report `harness_report_v0.0.32.json`.
- **Heavy fleet, seed 13** (`--godot 4 --ai chaser,sniper,turtle,rusher --duration 120 --latency-ms 120 --jitter-ms 150 --restart-server`, the seed that gave two lines in v0.0.31): **zero `🧗 [TopEdge]` lines** in all four client logs and no top-edge note. Four shifts happened (the `🧭 [Spawn]` lines show `flips=` 0, 1, 2, 4). Backlog 9 is closed.
- **The run still reports FAILED, on `fleet.bot-fall-loop`:** `Godot1 (chaser): 4 bottom wraps without landing` and `Godot2 (sniper): 4 bottom wraps without landing`. This is the free fall itself, not a fault in the change. The numbers: a fighter wrapped to `y = -10` falls at `FALL_GRAVITY = 1600` and reaches the bottom wrap at `y = 376` in 0.65 s. With every platform soft for 2.5 s, a fighter standing when the shift starts drops off its platform in about 0.45 s and then wraps three more times before the stage locks: 4 bottom wraps, no landing in between, which is exactly the gate's threshold. The turtle brain, which hardly moves (52 moves in 120 s), went from 6 wraps and a 0.98 s landing average in the v0.0.31 seed 13 run to 9 wraps and a 2.62 s landing average here: the turn length. The gate was written for a brain stuck falling through the seam columns (backlog 8); it cannot tell that fall from this one.
- **What that means for the game: a fighter tumbles for the whole shift.** It falls out of the bottom and comes back in at the top three or four times, then lands on the new layout. Whether that is the feel we want, or the gate should ignore wraps while `arena.is_arena_rotating` (a `bot_brain.gd` change in `_track_wraps`), is the new design call, backlog 23. Nothing was changed for it in this build.
