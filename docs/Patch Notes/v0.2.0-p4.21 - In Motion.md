# v0.2.0-p4.21 - In Motion

**Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Phase:** menu motion. The user, after p4.20: "Anything else able to be polished? Add animation to menus, character select, etc." · **Harness:** the proofs below

> The menus move. Every panel grows in with a soft bounce and shrinks away; its rows come in one after another. A button squeezes under the finger and springs back. Changed words bump. The seat cards react to more: someone leaving, a bot taking a seat, a team flag dropping in, a tap on your own card. One polish switch, "motion": off, and everything is as in p4.20.

What already moved before this build (a still picture cannot show it, and nobody had seen the game run since p4.17): the lobby's parts rise in, the fighters on the seat cards breathe, hop and aim, a person who joins pops in with sparks, the title bobs and a light sweeps over it, START breathes, the countdown punches in, the winner's screen rises and its awards slide in.

## The rule that keeps a phone's memory flat
Only sizes, places and see-through change (transforms and `modulate`). No new text size (each is its own sheet of letters, about 2 MB), and no box in a new shade (`lobby_art.gd box` keeps every box it makes). A panel fades as one piece, so the stepped fades that panels used are not needed with the polish on. Measured on an iPhone-style page, SETTINGS, HOW TO PLAY and PICK AN ARENA opened and closed 8 times each: 137.1 MB at every report with the polish on, and 137.1 MB with it off (`?polish_off=motion`).

## A way to SEE motion on the test PC
The test page draws 2 to 8 frames a second (no graphics card), so a recording stutters. `tools/v2_motion_clip.py`: the page runs N times slower (`?slowmo=N`, a test knob: `Engine.time_scale`), every frame is kept with its time, and the clip plays them back N times faster. At `--slowmo 14` a 15 s clip has 26 frames a second and takes 4 minutes to film. The BEFORE/AFTER film the user was sent: the `lobby` and `teams` scripts, each filmed with the polish off and on, stacked with ffmpeg (BEFORE on top); a white ring marks each tap.

## Found on the way, not fixed
1. The panels' letter sheets: opening SETTINGS, HOW TO PLAY and PICK AN ARENA for the first time adds 27 MB of textures on an iPhone-style page (109.8 to 137.1 MB) and they stay for the visit. It is the same in p4.20 (the polish off gives the same numbers): each text size a panel uses is its own sheet. Fewer sizes in the panels would give some of it back.
2. On the test PC, AUTO picks the HIGH look (it has no phone to recognise), and the lobby then draws 2 to 3 frames a second there. Not a fault on a real device; it is why a film needs the slow-motion knob.
3. Left from p4.20: a page under 8 frames a second plays the replay slow and cuts the winner's screen short; among bots THE BIG TURKEY's wearer wins 45% of 8-fighter rounds.
