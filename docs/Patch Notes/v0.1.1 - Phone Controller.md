# v0.1.1 - Phone Controller

**Date:** 2026-09-08 · **Phase:** v0.1.1 build 1: the phone controller revamp, the kill cam circle fix, the speed probe · **Harness:** 24/24 with --restart-each, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.1.1.json](../harness_report_v0.1.1.json) · the sheet: [[Playtest — Master Validation Suite v0.1]] · the decision: [[release-v0.1.0]] §5

> The first match on two phones (2026-09-07, 23:32 to 23:36 on the laptop, an S25 Ultra against a Pixel) said three things: the buttons miss ("I kept trying to attack and hitting jump"), the kill cam circled the winner twice, and a fighter "went into super speed for a second or two" once. This build answers all three on the client. The phones' own numbers said the web build is playable (60 physics ticks all match, no hitch in play, ping about 20 ms), so the Android app stays an idea and cut 1 proper is build 2.

## Changes
- **The phone controller, rewritten** (`scripts/touch_controls.gd`; the scene file is untouched, the buttons are moved, resized and rounded from `_ready()`).
  - *Why the buttons missed:* four 55 px squares in a plus shape, the hit test grew each by 20 px, then asked them in a fixed order with jump first. The grown squares overlapped, so the lower right part of the attack button, and the whole gap, counted as jump.
  - *Now:* four round buttons on an arc under the right thumb, which pivots from the bottom right corner: ATK (radius 30) where the thumb rests, JUMP (24) next along the arc toward the bottom, SPEC (22) above, DASH (22) at the far end. A touch goes to the nearest centre inside its circle plus 14 px, never to the first match. A held thumb keeps its button until it is 12 px further out and another is nearer, so a wobble on an edge does not re-press anything.
  - *Why the stick felt like eight directions:* the thumb offset became four action strengths, each axis cut below 0.18, so a tilt near an axis lost its small component and snapped to the axis. And any tilt walked the fighter: no aiming while standing still.
  - *Now:* one vector per touch, radial, radius 60 (was 45). Inside the aim ring (the inner 40 %) the fighter aims and does not walk; past it, it walks and aims the same way, the walk strength ramping from the ring edge. The aim is the raw direction at any tilt above 8 %, a full circle, kept after the thumb lifts (`Global.touch_aim`, read by `player.gd` `_update_aim` on a phone). Movement still goes through the `p<N>_left/right/up/down` actions, so the bubble's first-press pop, the bots and the keyboard see nothing new.
  - The stick is drawn (base, the yellow aim ring, the thumb, the aim line); the thumb turns yellow inside the ring; a held button lights up.
- **The kill cam circles the kill that ended the round** (`scripts/history_ring.gd` `closing_stamp()` / `report_stamp()`, used by the tape card, the `📼 [Tape]` line, `replay_player.gd` and the `📼 [Replay]` line). The story: the winner's own kunai came back and killed them inside the one-second tail after `round_end`; that death was stamped after the closing kill, so `last_stamp()` named the winner as the victim, the red circle sat on the winner and the server line said "no closing kill" (rounds 2 and 7 of the match). Once a round is closed, the closing stamp is the one reported; before that, the newest, as before.
- **A speed probe for backlog 25** (`scripts/player.gd` `_probe_speed`): the real sideways distance per tick; one `🏃 [Speed]` line a second while it is over 300 px/s outside a dash (run speed is 220, a dash 550 for 0.14 s then 0.35 s to settle), with the peak, the tick count, `dashes(2s)=`, the flags, velocity, position, aim and round. Seam wraps (400+ px in a tick) and spawns are skipped.
