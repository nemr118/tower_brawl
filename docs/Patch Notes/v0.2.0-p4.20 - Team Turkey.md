# v0.2.0-p4.20 - Team Turkey

**Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Phase:** more polish and mechanics before Thanksgiving. The user, after p4.19: "Let's keep pushing polish and mechanics. What else can we improve?", then picked from a list: silly rounds, TEAMS, tonight's champions, a match menu, more juice, the five loose ends, "ACE-Step songs on the GPU", and asked: "Can you scope out adding additional classes? ... Archer ammo is a concern. I love the pick up ammo mechanic, if we add a melee character, how does that impact ammo recovery?" · **Harness:** the proofs below

> Two teams can play: TEAM TURKEY against TEAM PIE, with no friendly fire, so a grandparent can pair with a small kid. Every third round is a silly round for everyone (PIE FIGHT, TURKEY TROT...). The game has music: a tune for the lobby, one for the match and a winner's song. A match has a MENU, the lobby shows TONIGHT'S CHAMPIONS, and a +1 flies to the round winner's card. A phone that loses the Wi-Fi comes back to its seat by itself. And a study of classes, with numbers.

Two helpers worked in their own copies (TEAMS; the loose ends). Both were cut off twice by a server error near their ends; their committed work was merged and their last proofs were run here.

## TEAMS (a helper; `bench/tools/teams_probe.gd`)
- **A MODE for everyone** in SETTINGS: FREE FOR ALL (the default, unchanged) or TEAMS (`--mode ffa|teams`; kept with the lobby's settings).
- **Two teams,** named by the season: TEAM TURKEY and TEAM PIE (Halloween: GHOST and PUMPKIN; else GOLD and SILVER). A tap on your own card in the lobby changes sides. New people go to the smaller team; the bots are spread so the teams are as even as they can be. A match needs a fighter on both.
- **No friendly fire** (`sim.gd friends`): a teammate's arrow passes through you, a stomp bounces, and a teammate's blast, fire, poison, pie cream, peck, spikes, cats, FREEZE, RAIN, bats and BOO leave you alone. A teammate can pick up your arrows.
- **A round ends when one team is left.** That team gets the point. Every living winner is safe (the PHEW rule). The banner says TEAM PIE WINS THE ROUND; the winner's screen names the team, its members, and shows its best player's hero.
- **Seeing the teams** (`client/teams_view.gd`): a flag over each fighter and on each card: an orange pennant with a letter, a teal banner with a letter.
- **Proofs:** 29 of 29 rule checks. 4 against 4 bots over 1 200 rounds: 48% to 52%. 2 against 2: 52% to 48%. 1 against 3: the one wins 9% (uneven teams are lopsided, as they should be). A predicted TEAMS match: corrections 0 px on 99.4 to 99.9%. A 15-minute chaos soak with 100 side changes and 42 mode changes: found nothing; 935 knockouts in TEAMS rounds, none by a teammate. FREE FOR ALL draws its random numbers exactly as before (`bench/tools/ffa_hash.gd`: 84 fixed-seed lines the same).

## Silly rounds
Every third round of a match the same surprise for every fighter, from rules the game already has (`proto.gd SILLY_*`, `sim.gd silly_round`, `game_server.gd _pick_silly`):

| The round | What everyone gets |
|---|---|
| PIE FIGHT! | pie arrows |
| TURKEY TROT! | everyone is THE BIG TURKEY: no bows |
| PEA-SIZED! | everyone is tiny |
| EVERYBODY FLIES! | wings |
| ARROW STORM! | big arrows and a quiver that never empties |

The ROUND banner says it, a small pill keeps its name up, a ta-da plays. Nothing of it is kept for the next round. Off with POWER-UPS OFF (or `--silly-every 0`). Bots: 12 rounds of each kind end; EVERYBODY FLIES rounds are the long ones (a median 25 s: bots hover until the walls come).

## Music
- Six takes made by ACE-Step in ComfyUI on the desktop card, on the user's go: 4.5 minutes, the card quiet before (2.4 GB in use) and after (`tools/comfy/run_music.py` through `batch.sh` with `TB_MEM_MAX=44G`).
- A lobby tune and a match tune (a harvest hoedown, nobody sings) and a winner's song: "Winner, winner, turkey dinner!". `tools/comfy/make_music.py` cuts the picked takes to whole bars and one loudness.
- `client/music.gd`: its own MUSIC switch in SETTINGS, **off everywhere until picked** (nobody had heard the songs when this was written; take 1 of each is in the game). Quieter than the sound effects. The winner's song plays in the fanfare's place.
- Listen and pick: `mpv ~/.cache/tower-brawl-comfy/music/lobby_s1.flac` (and `_s2`; `match_s1`, `match_s2`; `winner_s1`, `winner_s2`), then `python3 tools/comfy/make_music.py --pick match=2`.

## Polish
- **A match's MENU:** a small button at the bottom on a phone, Esc or M on a PC: LOOK, CONTROLS, SOUND, MUSIC, BACK TO THE GAME, LEAVE THE MATCH. The match goes on while it is open.
- **TONIGHT'S CHAMPIONS:** the lobby's TONIGHT button opens a board of who won how many matches tonight (rounds and knockouts beside); THE BIG SCREEN's lobby shows the list.
- **More juice** (polish "juice"): a +1 flies from the round's banner to the winner's card and the gold pip pops in; a card one point from the goal blinks gold; the match's leader wears a small crown in the arena.
- SETTINGS has rows for MUSIC and MODE and fits six.

## The loose ends (a helper; p4.19's "found, not fixed")
| What | Now |
|---|---|
| One slow server tick in the first round on each arena | The bots' route maps are built at the server's start (55 to 59 ms, once). 102 first rounds: the worst tick 17.5 ms before, 4.2 ms after |
| A phone that walks out of the Wi-Fi saw a frozen match | The page has its own watchdog (`client/net.gd`): an open socket with nothing on it for 5 s is given up, and the page joins again by itself. A new socket that will not open gives way to a fresh one every 4 s |
| A person who sat down on a bot's seat in a round got that bot's win | The bot plays the round out under its own name; the person plays from the next round |
| Bots' points stayed behind when seats shifted | A bot keeps its seat, its name and its points for the whole match |
| Names kept direction characters and markup | Names are cleaned: letters of any language, digits, single spaces and a few marks |

The watchdog's proofs, on the real page: the server stopped for 10 s: the page closes its socket 5.3 s in and is back in its own seat 0.2 s after the server runs again. Every packet thrown away for 12 s and for 35 s: back in its own seat 1.1 s and 0.6 s after the link returns. No false alarm in any phase: pages sat in the lobby (13 minutes), the countdown (410 s), a match (300 s: a seated page, a watcher, THE BIG SCREEN) and the winner's screen (6 minutes), and the longest an open socket was ever quiet was 7 ms (5 000 closes it).
