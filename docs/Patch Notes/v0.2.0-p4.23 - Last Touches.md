# v0.2.0-p4.23 - Last Touches

**Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Phase:** the last touches before the deploy. The user picked three fixes from the found list (KRAMPUS and THE BIG TURKEY left as they are), listened to the Christmas songs and picked the lobby's second take. · **Harness:** the proofs below

> A Christmas tune for the lobby, award lines that say "1 time", not "1 times", whole names on TONIGHT'S CHAMPIONS, and earmuffs you can see on a phone.

## Changes
- **The Christmas lobby song** (ACE-Step, take 2 of 2: a jolly toy-workshop tune with sleigh bells, 67 s, looped): `bench/assets/music/lobby_xmas.wav`. It plays in the lobby in the Christmas season when MUSIC is on. The user turned down both match takes and both winner takes: in December the match plays the year-round match song, and the winner gets the fanfare as before. `tools/comfy/make_music.py --pick NAME=0` now means "no take" (the file goes, the game falls back).
- **Award lines with one:** "opened 1 presents", "opened 1 dishes", "opened 1 candies", "packed 1 player into snowmen" now read "opened 1 present", "1 dish", "1 candy", "into a snowman". LUMP OF COAL says "got coal N times" (it said "N tricks out of presents"). Every award line was checked with a count of 1 (`game_server.gd _award_line`).
- **TONIGHT'S CHAMPIONS:** a name too long for its room is squeezed sideways (the same letters, no new size), not cut. "FRUITCAKE (a bot", "CORNBREAD (a bo" and "CRANBERRY (a bo" are whole now, on the board and on THE BIG SCREEN (`lobby_champs.gd rows`, its new `bxf`: the caller's own transform).
- **The earmuffs** (the weakest hat on a phone): a dark band that shows on any hood, and a big fluffy muff in the seat's colour with a shine. They were a cream puff on a band in a lighter shade, and on the yellow seat the band vanished into the hood.
