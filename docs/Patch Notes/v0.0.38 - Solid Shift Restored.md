# v0.0.38 - Solid Shift Restored

**Date:** 2026-09-07 · **Phase:** user decision: the platforms stay solid during the arena shift again (v0.0.32 free fall reverted); the v0.1.0 release candidate · **Harness:** 24/24 with `--restart-each`, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.38.json](../harness_report_v0.0.38.json) · sheet: [[Playtest — Master Validation Suite]] section 4 · release: [[release-v0.1.0]]

> v0.0.32 made every platform soft while the arena shift turns the stage, so a standing fighter fell through and tumbled out of the bottom three or four times before the new layout locked. That closed backlog 9 (the ride out of the top edge), but in play it felt terrible. The user's call on 2026-09-07: go back to the v0.0.31 shift. The platforms stay solid, a fighter rides its platform through the turn, and backlog 9 is open again for the arena configurations. One file changed, `scripts/arena.gd`, 8 lines; the softening code stays behind a constant.

## Changes
- **`scripts/arena.gd`.** New `const SHIFT_SOFT_PLATFORMS: bool = false`. `_activate_rotation` only calls `_set_platforms_solid(false)` when it is true. The two restore calls (`_set_platforms_solid(true)` at the timer end and in `_finish_spin_now`) stay: with nothing stashed they do nothing, and if a future arena configuration turns the switch on per layout they still put the platforms back. `platform_layers` and `_set_platforms_solid` are unchanged.
- **What a player sees.** The shift banner comes up, the stage turns, every fighter stays on its platform. A fighter on an outer ledge or slab passes about 30 px above the top of the screen for up to a second mid-turn (backlog 9, the reason v0.0.32 existed). Fighters in the air land on the turning platforms, which are solid.
- **Backlog 9 reopened** in [[PASSDOWN]]: solve it with the arena configurations; the candidate is option B from [[v0.0.31 - Top Edge Probe]], wrap a fighter carried above the top edge to the bottom during a turn. Backlog 23 (the bot gate ignores shift wraps, [[v0.0.33 - Shift Tumble Kept]]) stays closed: `bot_brain.gd` still counts a wrap during a turn apart, which is harmless with solid platforms.
- **The `🧗 [TopEdge]` probe** in `player.gd` is unchanged and prints again when a fighter touches something above the top edge. It is a note in the fleet run, not a gate.
- **Docs.** Master sheet section 4 rewritten from the tumble check to the ride check (does the old shift feel right, how bad is the ride), with a verdict that either ships v0.1.0 with it or asks for option B first. The release candidate in [[release-v0.1.0]], PASSDOWN and the sheet moves from v0.0.37 to v0.0.38. Nothing else changed: packets, server, `player.gd`, `bot_brain.gd`, the tape and the replay are as they were.

## Harness
- **Full suite:** 24/24 with `--restart-each`, no Minor, no `🧗 [TopEdge]` note in the run. The suite's own fleet scenario is short; the heavy fleet run (seed 13, 120 s, 120 ± 150 ms) that showed the ride in v0.0.31 was not repeated for this build, because the ride coming back is the expected result and section 4 of the sheet judges its feel.
