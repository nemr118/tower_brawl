# v0.2.0-p4.7 - Night Log

**Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Phase:** P2's last part, before the next play night. The user: "Would setting up logging or anything help collect data for you to look for bugs?" Tonight's two bugs (the look stepping down, the invisible power-arrow effects) lived only on the phones, where the server's log could not see them. The user picked phone reports and page errors, without a bug button. · **Harness:** the proofs below
**Report:** `tools/v2_report_check.py`, `tools/v2_night_report.py`, `tools/pull_rog_logs.sh`

> Each phone now tells the server how it is doing: every 30 seconds, and at once when its look changes or the page hits an error. The server writes it into its log, and the host page can show each phone's health. After a night, one command pulls the logs from the ROG and another turns them into a plain report, with a list of what to look at.

## Changes
- **1. The phone report** (`client/game_client.gd`, the JSON `report`): the device (android, ios, pc) and its browser's name, the screen, the look picked and in use, the frame rate in matches with the share of frames slower than 30 fps and the longest frame, the ping, reconnects, step-downs, the shots let go (armed past the ring, let go inside it, with the mouse; `touch_sticks.gd` counts them), and page errors. It goes every 30 s (the first 3 s after the socket opens), at once on a look change (picked or stepped down), and within 5 s of a page error.
- **2. Page errors**: the page keeps what `console.error` prints (Godot's own errors use it), script errors and failed promises, up to 10 per report.
- **3. The server** (`server/game_server.gd`, `_on_report`): cleans each report (known fields only, numbers clamped, text cut), writes it as a `client` line in `v2_server.jsonl` with who sent it (client, seat, name, address), and keeps the last one per phone: the host port's `/state` has `seats[].phone` and `watching[].phone` (the device, the look, the frame rate, slow frames, the ping, reconnects, step-downs, errors, the report's age). A step-down or a page error also makes a `[PHONE]` line in the journal and the host page's events.
- **4. `tools/pull_rog_logs.sh`**: the ROG's `bench_results/` and the new game's journal into `playtest_logs/<date>_rog/`.
- **5. `tools/v2_night_report.py`**: the night in plain words: the server (its starts, ticks, stalls, memory, late inputs), the matches and rounds (arenas, deaths by cause), the people (rounds played and won), each phone (the look, the frame rate, the ping, reconnects, shots let go against the server's count, page errors), and a list of what to look at.
- **6. `tools/v2_report_check.py`**: the check below.
