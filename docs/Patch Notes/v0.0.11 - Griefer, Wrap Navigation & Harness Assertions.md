# v0.0.11 - Griefer, Wrap Navigation & Harness Assertions

**Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Phase:** AI bot brains (steps 5–7 of 7, milestone signed off, tagged `v0.0.11`) · **Harness:** 17/17
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.11.json](../harness_report_v0.0.11.json) (full suite 17/17; the `fleet.no-kills` / 150 s round-end gating landed after that run and only affects `--ai` fleets)

> The bots now treat the arena's seams as routes instead of hazards, the griefer persona hunts edge cases on purpose, and the fleet scenario asserts that every brain fought, nobody fall-looped and a round ended.

## Changes
### Client (`scripts/bot_brain.gd`)
- **Wrap-aware navigation (design correction from the user: the voids are a mechanic).** The v0.0.10 void blacklist is gone. `_platform_below` tells a bot whether a column ends in the bottom seam; `_landing_move` picks the platform to come down on after the seam (the one nearest the persona's goal among those air control can reach in the fall time, else the highest reachable, else the nearest column so the next pass converges) and air-steers under it. `_steer_to` routes a rise beyond one jump (80 px; the ledge steps are 86–90 px) through the arena's tools: a jump with an upward dash at the apex when the dash is ready and the target is close, otherwise a walk to the nearest drop column and a fall through the seam to come out of the ceiling above the target.
- **griefer** (druid): moods every 2–5 s: `wrap` (drop through the seam onto the highest platform), `seam` (hop across the vertical seam ledge to ledge with a jump and a mid-air dash), `camp` (fall through the powerup at the top centre), `harass` (chase with thorns or bear slashes). Always: dashes *into* an inbound projectile (dash frames are invulnerable), spams the 1.5 s air shield, toggles bear form on the floor.
- **Wrap telemetry** in the status line: `wraps` (bottom seam passes), `drops` (deliberate ones), `seams` (horizontal crossings), `land_avg` (seconds from a wrap to the next landing), `max_loop` (longest chain of wraps without landing).

### Client (`scripts/global.gd`, `scripts/player.gd`)
- The three P1 key-bind copies (`assign_id`, `player_joined`, snapshot) require `my_player_id >= 2`; id 0 (still a spectator) produced `InputMap action "p0_left" doesn't exist` whenever another client joined during the connect window. This was the intermittent red on the default fleet scenario since v0.0.8.

### Harness (`tools/chaos_bots.py`)
- The fleet scenario parses each brain's status line. New findings: `fleet.bot-missing` (`--ai` given, no brain line), `fleet.bot-idle` (no attack, no special or no movement), `fleet.bot-fall-loop` (4+ wraps without landing), `fleet.no-kills` (brains in the match, 30 s or more, no `player_died` reached any Godot client), `fleet.no-round-end` (brains in the match, 150 s or more, no `round_end`: a round ends after 9–11 kills and four bots score about 7 a minute, so 60 s runs end a round only sometimes). Brainless headless clients never die, so the plain fleet is exempt from both. Notes carry per-persona counts, kills, rounds and the wrap stats. `--ai-difficulty` and `--latency-ms` now reach the Godot clients.

## Fixed on the way (approved by the user)
- **No screen wrap while shielding or in egg form** (`scripts/player.gd`): the `is_egg` and `is_shielding` early returns called `move_and_slide()` without `_check_screen_wrap()`, so a druid air-shielding while falling dropped far below the arena (the griefer bot was logged at y = 513) until the shield ended. Both branches now wrap. Found by the griefer persona; shipped in the same version by re-exporting v0.0.11 before any browser had loaded it. The fix exposed a second thing: a shielded fall has no air control (the shield branch ignores input), so the griefer no longer air-shields while over an empty column, or it chained seven wraps.
