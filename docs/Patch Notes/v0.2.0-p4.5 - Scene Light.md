# v0.2.0-p4.5 - Scene Light

**Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Phase:** P2's last part, the look from the night. The user: "The scene lighting looked way better in the demo. Need to add some additional polish for the lighting." The demo was the benchmark page; what the user liked there: glow and bloom, moving lights, fire and explosions (not the storm and rain). Fire and explosions come with the power arrows, now a lobby switch. · **Harness:** the proofs below
**Report:** `tools/v2_shot.py` pictures; `tools/v2_host_check.py`; `bench/tools/play_probe.gd`

> The arena glows now. Every fighter carries a soft light in its colour, flying arrows glow, a death bursts in a flash of its colour, and spirit lights drift through the air. On HIGH the torches and flashes bloom, two spirit lights and the newest four arrows are real lights that light the walls, and a death lights the walls too. A new POWER button in the lobby turns on the demo's arrows: fire, bombs, poison and smoke, with all their fire and explosion light.

## What the demo had
The benchmark page plays with power arrows, so its bombs and fire throw moving light everywhere; its best looks (T6, T7) add bloom, 16 spirit lights (all real lights), a storm, rain, a third fog layer and a screen warp. In the benchmark, T6 ran 44 fps on the Galaxy S25 Ultra (T3: 59): real lights were the main cost ([[bench-8p-results]]). So the glow comes mostly from sprites (cheap), with few real lights, and only on HIGH.

## Changes
- **1. POWER ARROWS in the lobby** (`lobby.gd`, `game_client.gd`, `game_server.gd`): a button beside ARENA, off by default, for everyone, in the lobby (the JSON `power {n}`, `seats {power}`, `[LOBBY] power arrows ON, picked by seat N`); the host port's `/power {n}` and `state.power_arrows`. On: every shot is a random arrow of the benchmark's mix (fire, bomb, poison, smoke), with their fire, explosions, smoke and light.
- **2. A death bursts in light** (`fx.gd`): a flash in the fighter's colour on every look (LOW too: `flash(..., always)`), and on NORMAL and HIGH a real light that lights the walls for half a second.
- **3. Moving lights** (`world.gd`): a soft glow in each fighter's colour around it (every look); a glow along each flying arrow in its kind's colour (every look); on HIGH the newest four flying arrows are real lights too (`arrow_lights`); spirit lights drift: 8 glows on NORMAL, 12 on HIGH with 2 of them real lights (`wisps`, `wisp_lights`; `_set_wisps(n, lit)`).
- **4. Bloom on HIGH** (the post shader's bloom, until now only in the benchmark's T6 and up).
- **5. The phone's help line** in the lobby now says how the right thumb works since p4.4 (pull back past the ring, let go).

## What each look adds (the cost)
| Look | Real lights added | Sprites added | Full-screen work |
|---|---|---|---|
| LOW | none | a fighter glow each, an arrow glow each, the death flash | none |
| NORMAL | a short light at each death | as LOW, and 8 spirit glows | none |
| HIGH | a death's light, 2 spirit lights, up to 4 arrow lights | as NORMAL, 12 spirit glows | bloom in the post pass it already had |

A headless browser draws in software (7 to 8 fps on every look), so it cannot time the change; the frame cost is judged on a phone. A phone that cannot keep up steps its look down by itself, as before.
