# v0.2.0-p4.18 - Harvest Feast

**Date:** 2026-11-24 · **Phase:** the Thanksgiving update. The user: "Do a review of the game. It's almost Thanksgiving and I want to do an update for the Holiday. Look at ways to makes some fun thanksgiving themed power-ups and maps. Review the game overall, look for way to improve it". The review is [[game-review-2026-11]]. From it the user picked every Thanksgiving piece (the base set, the turkey and THE HARVEST BARN, PIE and THE BIG TURKEY, THE BIG TABLE) and every fix offered (catch-up help, HOW TO PLAY, THE BIG SCREEN, tap your name), said "Yes, go ahead" to a ComfyUI batch, and "Thursday morning" for the ROG. · **Harness:** the proofs below

> The game has seasons now, and it is the Harvest Feast. A turkey runs through the arena: catch it for a power-up. Candies are covered dishes with new tricks, a pie splats cream over your screen, and you can turn into THE BIG TURKEY. Two new arenas: THE HARVEST BARN and THE BIG TABLE. A player who falls behind starts with a shield. New players get a HOW TO PLAY card, kids can tap their name, and a TV can show the match.

## Why (what the review found)
- Inside a match it was still Halloween: candies, TRICK OR TREAT and GHOSTFACE dropped on every arena after 2 November.
- The catch-up power-ups mostly missed: 111 BIG orbs came on 25 October, 44 were taken, 18 of those by bots. Nothing said whose an orb was.
- GHOSTFACE's knife got 4 knockouts in 18 turns.
- Half the players never typed a name. A new player had one line of help.
- The black cat came 6 to 12 s into a round; by 9 s a third of rounds were over.

## Changes
- **The seasons.** The server is in a season: by its own date (Halloween 1 October to 2 November, the Harvest 3 to 30 November, else none), or by the lobby's new SEASON button (AUTO, OFF, HALLOWEEN, HARVEST; `--season`; the host port's `season`). It sends the season with the seats, so every phone matches. Out of a season the game is as before.
- **The Harvest's pictures on rules that already worked** (the bots and the phone's prediction needed nothing new):

| Before | In the Harvest season |
|---|---|
| A candy: TRICK OR TREAT | A covered dish: a treat or a trick |
| PUMPKIN HEAD, BACKWARDS, TINY | TURKEY TIME (a hopping turkey, no shots), TOO MUCH PIE (left is right), PEA-SIZED |
| PUMPKIN BOMBS | POPCORN ARROWS (on the season's two arenas) |
| BAT SWARM; the bats' sweep | CROWS; a gust of leaves on THE HARVEST BARN |
| GHOSTFACE | THE BIG TURKEY (no bow, a peck, two hits to end) |
| The black cat | The turkey |

- **Catch the turkey** (`sim.gd` `_update_turkey`): it comes 2 to 4 s into a round (9 rounds in 10 on the barn, about half elsewhere), walks its floor, and runs from whoever is near: off the floor's end, over a low wall. Touch it: a power-up for you, feathers, and it flies off. The bots do not chase it.
- **PIE** (a new arrow kind, `PU_PIE`): the pie splats where it lands. A small blast, and everyone within 230 px is covered in cream for 3 s: they see nobody (the smoke rule), and their own screen gets big cream dollops that slide down, never over their own fighter.
- **Two arenas** (each built by a helper in its own copy, then merged):
  - **THE HARVEST BARN** (layout 5): a red barn cut open, a loft with a hatch, hay to climb, a wagon, a silo to wall-jump, a sunset over the fields (`client/barn_view.gd`).
  - **THE BIG TABLE** (layout 6): you are tiny on the dinner table. The roast, two candlesticks, a gravy boat, a pie on its stand, and forks, knives and spoons for ledges (`client/table_view.gd`; its still parts are two pictures from `tools/make_table_art.py`).
  - In the Harvest season MIX is the keep, the bridges, the barn and the table. THE KEEP and THE BRIDGES get an autumn sky and falling leaves. Every arena can still be picked by hand; the picker shows seven cards.
- **The look around the match** (ComfyUI, 96 pictures; `tools/comfy/run_harvest.py`, `make_assets.py harvest`): a harvest loading picture, the lobby's HARVEST FEAST dress with a pumpkin pie for the O of TOWER, the winner's screen at a feast, two arena cards, an autumn sky, eight badges, the two turkeys as sprites.
- **Seven Harvest awards:** GOBBLE GOBBLE, PIE FACE, STUFFED, LEFTOVERS, GOBBLER, PECKING ORDER, TURKEY DINNER.
- **Catch-up help that cannot miss:**
  - a person 2 or more points behind the leader starts the round with a SHIELD (`sim.gd` `catch_up`; a HEAD START tag, a line on their screen);
  - a BIG orb that came for you says FOR YOU, with gold arrows from your fighter to it;
  - the black cat comes 2 to 5 s in;
  - a costume's swing comes with a short lunge;
  - the server's log line for a costume from a candy no longer fails (18 script errors on 25 October).
- **HOW TO PLAY** (`client/lobby_howto.gd`): three big moving pictures (move, shoot, dodge) for the controls in use. It opens the first time a device takes a seat, and from a lobby button.
- **WHO ARE YOU?** (`client/lobby_names.gd`): JOIN with an empty name box shows the names the server has seen as big buttons, and SOMEONE NEW. The server keeps the names in `bench_results/known_names.json` (the first time, it reads the old nights' files).
- **THE BIG SCREEN** (`?tv` on the page): a page for a TV. No seat; the match full screen; in the lobby a big QR code and the address to join (the server makes the code with `qrencode`). The ROG's links page names it.
- **Test knobs and tools:** the server's `--season`, `--pet-always`, `--start-power N`; the page's `?tv`, `?cream`, `?nohowto`; `v2_shot.py --no-join`, `--host-start PORT`, a numbered `--until`; `bench/tools/harvest_probe.gd`. The ComfyUI batch tool tries a blank picture again and can redo only the bad ones (`TB_REDO=1`). The night report knows the season and the new arenas.
