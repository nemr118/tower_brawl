# v0.2.0-p1.3 - Walls and Spawns

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Phase:** P1, before the first play night (the user's picks) · **Harness:** the P1 proofs on the PC (below); the relay harness does not apply to the new server
**Report:** the server's report lines, `bench_results/<date>/v2_server.jsonl`

> On the laptop since 2026-10-10 19:35, beside the old game. The night still had not happened (nobody had played since the 16:15 deploy), so the user picked the two open notes to go first, then P2. **The purple walls** (TowerFall's miasma): 25 s after FIGHT they close in from the left and right, and they kill 2 s after they appear. By about 48 s everyone left is dead, and they melt as soon as one fighter is left. Before, 45% of the sim's rounds ended as the 60 s draw, with the last two fighters stuck on either side of a wall. Now 2% do. **The spawn spots** moved, so none sits one drop above another. p1.2 had 7 such pairs.

## Changes
- **1. The purple walls** (`bench/sim/sim.gd`; D2 A, the placeholder P1 plays). `walls_start` turns them on: 25 s in the game, never in the benchmark. The server option `--walls-s N` moves the start, and 0 turns them off. `play_t` counts the ticks since FIGHT. `_update_walls()` follows `WALLS_STEPS`, TowerFall's timings: a wall reaches a third of the way to the middle in 9 s, pulls back to 15% in 3 s, then closes all the way (105%) in 11 s. Each wall's reach is a share of half the arena (960 px). They kill from 2 s after they appear (`WALLS_DEADLY`), and the deadly part stops 22 px inside the drawn edge. When one fighter or none is left, they melt at once. TowerFall melts them only after a kill that leaves one; ours melt on any end, so the winner never dies in the 1.5 s before the point. A death in the walls is cause 6: the `[KILL]` line says "died (the walls)". The 60 s draw stays as a backstop that the walls never reach.
- **2. Bots keep away from the walls** (`_brain`). From the moment the walls show, a bot within 240 px of a wall's edge heads for the middle (`WALLS_KEEP_AWAY`).
- **3. The snapshot carries the walls.** The header has a new `u16 walls` after `winner`: each wall's width in px, plus `0x8000` when they kill. `net.gd` reads it, and `sample()` smooths it between snapshots. `tools/v2_synth.py` reads the new offsets.
- **4. The page draws the walls.** `bench/shaders/walls.gdshader` (new) draws boiling purple pixel noise with a ragged, glowing inner edge. For the 2 s before the walls kill, they are see-through and their edge pulses; after that they are solid. `world.gd` (`_render_walls`) draws two sprites on the threats layer, which is never darkened. A death in the walls gets the purple burst (`fx.gd`).
- **5. The spawn spots** (`bench/sim/level.gd`). The 10 spots moved so that none sits one drop above another (dropping through your own ledge and steering up to 150 px sideways): (240, 1020), (690, 780), (360, 540), (210, 180), their mirrors, (960, 180) and (960, 660). The two low ledge spots sit at the ledge ends, under the pillars. A first try with them in the ledges' middles still let a fighter run off the side ledge onto them (the stress line below).
- **6. Three probe rules** (`input_probe.gd`, now 27): 25, the walls come at 25 s, kill at the edge from 27 s and melt when one is left; 26, the walls close on the last ones in the middle by 48 s; 27, no spawn spot sits one drop above another.

## Found on the way (reported, not fixed)
- **The bots shoot into walls.** Before the walls came, 75% of their shots had a wall between the bot and its target. Shooting only with a clear line would also end stand-offs, but it would make the bots deadlier for the kids. That belongs with the bots' difficulty (G6).
- **The edge torches draw over the walls.** The torch flames are on the glow layer, above the walls. It reads like a torch behind fog.
- **The spawn shuffle with 2 fighters.** TowerFall gives the second player the spot opposite the first. Ours shuffles, so two fighters can start on neighbouring spots (only with bots at 0).
