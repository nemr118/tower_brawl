# v0.2.0-p4.12 - Trick or Treat

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Phase:** the second gathering night. The user: "The trick or treat can be an additional way to get a random power up or a little trick. I also like the pumpkin bomb... Bat swarms sound good... I like the cat you can pet for a shield, let's not have it happen too much though." Then: "My daughter's favorite Halloween character is Ghostface from scream. As part the of trick or treat boxes, add a power up that turns you into Ghostface. Make sure the likeness is very obvious." And, after a picture: "We want to use graphics or small stylized text that disrupts the game as little as possible while conveying the meaning of the power up." · **Harness:** the proofs below
**Report:** `bench/tools/halloween_probe.gd`, `bench/tools/power_probe.gd`

> Halloween treats. Sometimes a black cat walks a floor: pet it for a shield. A new orb, TRICK OR TREAT, gives a power-up, a silly trick, or turns you into GHOSTFACE: a knife instead of a bow, and it takes two hits to stop. On THE HAUNTED HOUSE the bombs are pumpkins that burst into seeds, and swarms of bats sweep across. Power-ups now show as small pictures with one word, not sentences.

## How the bats work (the user asked)
- **The swarm (THE HAUNTED HOUSE only):** 20 s after FIGHT, then every 20 to 30 s, bats screech at one edge for 1 s (zigzags and a bat picture there). Then a cloud of bats, 520 px wide and 170 px tall, flies across the arena in about 3 s.
- An arrow flying into the cloud drops, as if the bats knocked it out of the air. Anyone inside the cloud is hidden from the others for half a second (like smoke). The bats hurt nobody.
- **BAT SWARM** (a BIG power-up, for someone behind): bats circle you for 8 s. They knock away any arrow that comes within 85 px of you, and they hide you (the others see only the circling bats).

## GHOSTFACE (the user's daughter's favourite)
- From TRICK OR TREAT, 3 times in 10. On THE HAUNTED HOUSE the TRICK OR TREAT orb comes more often.
- The look (`client/ghostface.gd`, drawn in code): a black hooded robe that flows (the ragged hem trails behind a run and billows in a fall), the white ghost mask (a long face, big black eyes that droop at the outer corners, the long open mouth), a black glove and a hunting knife.
- No bow for the rest of the round. Hold shoot: the knife comes up. Let go: it swings down in front (a white swoosh). It reaches about two body widths in front, about head to feet. A dodge or a shield is safe from it.
- Any hit (an arrow, a stomp, a blast, fire...) knocks GHOSTFACE down on its back for 2 s: a red ring counts down, and a target mark shows the others it can be finished. Then it gets back up. A second hit while it is down ends it. Only the closing purple walls end it standing.
- Bots can be GHOSTFACE too: they run at the nearest player and swing.
- New awards: SLASHER (knife knockouts), SCARY MOVIE STAR (turned into GHOSTFACE), GHOST BUSTER (finished off a GHOSTFACE).
- The repo is public: the likeness is a fan drawing in a free family game, as the user asked.

## Changes
- **The black cat:** it comes in about a quarter of rounds (4 in 10 on THE HAUNTED HOUSE), 6 to 12 s after FIGHT, on a long floor away from everyone. It walks its floor, turning at edges and walls, with a small PET ME tag. The first to touch it gets a SHIELD (hearts float up), and it runs off. It leaves by itself after 10 s. Only with POWER-UPS on.
- **TRICK OR TREAT** (a new regular orb, a wrapped candy): GHOSTFACE 30%, a treat 45% (a random power-up; 1 in 6 a BIG one), a trick 25%: PUMPKIN HEAD (3 s: you are a hopping jack-o'-lantern and cannot shoot), BACKWARDS (3 s: left and right are swapped), TINY (6 s: you look tiny; nothing else changes).
- **PUMPKIN BOMBS:** on THE HAUNTED HOUSE the BOMB orb is PUMPKIN BOMBS. A pumpkin blast is three quarters of a bomb's; then 5 glowing seeds fly out (away from the wall it stuck in, and up) and each pops in a small blast.
- **Power-ups on screen (the user's call):**
  - over a player: a round badge with the power-up's picture and one small word (FIRE, SHIELD, GHOSTFACE...), which rises and fades. No sentences.
  - on your own screen: a small card under the player cards (picture, short name, a tip of 2 or 3 words) for 2.6 s; it was a big card in the middle.
  - the warning before a drop: its picture and the count (3, 2, 1), no name; a BIG one shows a gold ring and "for <name>" in small letters.
  - orbs: the picture only (no name under it, no "BIG" word: the spinning gold arcs say BIG).
  - the cat, the bats, GHOSTFACE down, the tricks: pictures (hearts, a bat badge, a target mark, a candy badge) in place of "MEOW? pet the cat for a shield", "hit again now!" and the like. In smoke: a closed eye over your head.
- **Bots:** they take TRICK OR TREAT orbs, play GHOSTFACE (close in and swing), steer by sight while tricked, and never see someone hidden by bats.
- **Fixed on the way (found by the new probe and the pictures):**
  - pumpkin seeds started inside the wall the pumpkin stuck in and all popped at once: they now start outside it;
  - the cat could appear right on a player (an instant pet nobody saw);
  - GHOSTFACE's robe outline could cross itself at high speed (the page could not fill it);
  - power-ups numbered after SUPER SPEED (TRICK OR TREAT, PUMPKIN BOMBS) counted as BIG in the server's log and the awards: `proto.gd is_big()`.
- **Snapshot:** each power-up row has one more byte (GHOSTFACE, down, swinging, knife up); after the ghosts come the black cat and a bat swarm. The whole fighter (`write_fighter_state`) carries the trick and GHOSTFACE's knife and fall, so the phone predicts them.
- **Test knob:** the server's `--hw-gallery` puts every Halloween look on screen at FIGHT (two GHOSTFACE bots, one knocked down, the three tricks, BAT SWARM, PUMPKIN BOMBS, the cat, a bat swarm held in the middle).
- **Notes:** [[holiday-ideas]] keeps the ideas for the other seasons and holidays (an arena, power-ups, a friendly thing and a costume for each).
