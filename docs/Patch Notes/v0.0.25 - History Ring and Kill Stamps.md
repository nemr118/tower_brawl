# v0.0.25 - History Ring and Kill Stamps

**Date:** 2026-09-04 · **Phase:** Phase 3c: the history ring and kill stamps, groundwork for the v0.1.0 replay · **Harness:** 20/20 with `--restart-each`, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.25.json](../harness_report_v0.0.25.json)

> Every screen now keeps a tape of the last six seconds of the fight, as that screen drew it. When the server says somebody died, the tape is stamped with the kill. When the round ends, the tape records one more second and freezes. The v0.1.0 replay will play that frozen tape back. No playback yet in this build.

## What a tape is
- **New file `scripts/history_ring.gd`.** One frame per physics tick (60 a second). The ring holds 360 frames: 5 s before a kill (`BEFORE_S`) plus a 1 s tail after it (`TAIL_S`). The 360 slots are made once in `_init` and reused, so recording does not make new memory every tick.
- **One frame holds:** `seq`, `msec`, `round`, `rot` (the platform spin right now), `flips`, `present` (a bit per seated fighter), `fighters` (`{pid: [x, y, aim_x, aim_y, flags]}` as reused `PackedFloat32Array`), `n_proj` + `projectiles` (`[weapon_id, x, y, rot, stuck, shooter]`, weapon id in `Global.BIN_WEAPONS` order: arrow 0, firebolt 1, kunai 2, thorn 3) and `powerup` (`[x, y, present]`).
- **Flags** are the six `sync_pos` bits (`FLAG_FACING` 1, `FLAG_DASH` 2, `FLAG_SHIELD` 4, `FLAG_BEAR` 8, `FLAG_EGG` 16, `FLAG_FLOOR` 32) plus two that live only on the tape: `FLAG_BUBBLE` 64 (inside the spawn bubble) and `FLAG_DEAD` 128 (dead or hidden). The wire format did not change.
- **Where it lives:** the client only. `arena.gd` makes the ring in `_ready` (`HistoryRingScript.new()`, no scene edit) and records in a new `_physics_process` with `process_physics_priority = 10`, so it samples after every fighter has moved this tick. This is sim recording, not network polling: the stamps and the freeze come from the server's signals. Spectators record too.
- **Why not on the server:** the server would have to decode the movement packets, re-time them and ship about 5 KB per client after each round, and the client would still have to re-simulate the arrows. Every screen already has everything it drew.

## How a kill stamps a frame
- **The server's word.** `player_died` from the server now carries `"weapon"` (`serve_game.py`, the first 16 letters). `global.gd` passes it on: the signal is `net_player_died(killer_id, victim_id, stock, weapon)`. `arena.gd` `_on_net_player_died` calls `tape.stamp(killer, victim, weapon)`: the newest frame gets a stamp `{seq, round, killer, victim, weapon, closing, fighters}`, where `fighters` is where everyone stood on that frame. The stamp lands 50 to 100 ms after the drawn hit, inside the 5 s window. Stamps whose frame has left the ring are dropped (`_drop_old_stamps`); at most 32 are kept.
- **The round end.** `_on_round_end_sync` calls `tape.close_round()`: the newest stamp of this round is marked `closing` and `closing_seq` is set; the ring records 60 more frames (`TAIL_FRAMES`) and then freezes (`recording = false`, `frozen = true`). A round that ends with no kill (a forfeit) freezes with `closing_seq = -1`. `_start_round` calls `tape.clear(round)` for every round, so a tape never spans two rounds. A frozen tape stays until the next round starts.
- **Numbers on a frozen tape:** 300 frames before the closing kill, 60 after, about 5 990 ms on tape, 59.9 to 60.0 frames a second on the headless clients.

## What the tape tells the world
- **The `📼 [Tape]` log line**, printed by `arena.gd` on every stamp and on every freeze (`history_ring.gd` `status_line`): `📼 [Tape] round=1 frozen=1 closing=1 killer=3 victim=4 weapon=Firebolt seq=695 before=300 after=60 span_ms=5989 fps=59.9 stamps=9 frames=360`. A weapon name with a space prints with `_` (`Goomba_Stomp`).
- **The `history_status` card** (client to server, same moments, never relayed, like `bot_status`): `{"type": "history_status", "schema": 1, "frames", "span_ms", "fps", "recording", "frozen", "round", "stamps", "closing_seq", "last": {"seq", "round", "killer", "victim", "weapon", "closing", "before", "after", "fighters": {"1": [x, y], ...}}}`. The server keeps the last card per seat in `player_tapes` and writes it to `status.json` as `seats[].tape` (dropped when the seat is freed, cleared at match start; a card over 1 024 B is dropped). Only seated clients get a card in `status.json`; spectators record but are not shown.
- **The `[TAPE]` server log line** on a freeze: `[TAPE] P3's screen froze the round 1 tape: P3 killed P4 with 'Firebolt', 300 frames before, 60 after` (or `no closing kill, 360 frames`). Stamps log at debug level only. Colour `\033[95m` on a terminal.

## The deck (`tbdash`)
- **`Tape` column in SEATS**, drawn only when at least one seat sent a card: `● 5.0s ·2` while recording (seconds on the tape, kills stamped this round), `■ R3 ✓` frozen for round 3 with the closing kill (`■ R3` without one), `-` no card. In `--plain` too.
- **Inspect line.** Click the column where a round ended and the line gains `· tape frozen on 2 screens (P3 P4), 300 frames before the kill / 60 after` (or `no screen has a frozen tape for round 3`).
- **`[TAPE]` events** in the log with the filter key `0` (`FILTER_TAGS` has ten tags now, `FILTER_KEYS = "1234567890"`; the key hint reads `0-9 filter`).

## The harness
- **New scenario `tape`** (20 in the suite). Two headless Godot clients that stand still (no brain) and two protocol bots with `shoot = False` (a new `Bot` field: no random `spawn_projectile`; a stray bot arrow once killed a wanderer and ended the round two reports early). After 7 s of play the bots burn their three stocks each (2 s apart), then Bot1 reports Godot1 killed by Godot2 with a `Firebolt` three times, 2 s apart, so the round ends on a known kill. Checks on both logs: `tape.missing` (no frozen line for round 1), `tape.wrong-kill`, `tape.short` (under 280 frames before), `tape.no-tail` (under 30 after), `tape.fps` (outside 50 to 70), `tape.stamp-count` (not 9 = 6 bot deaths + 3 reports), `tape.no-card` (`status.json` `seats[].tape` not frozen with a closing kill), `tape.script-error`, plus `tape.clients-not-joined`, `tape.no-round-end`, `tape.wrong-winner`, `tape.no-status`. About 24 s.
- **`tape_gate`** on every `fleet` client and the `lag` observer: `<scen>.tape-missing` when a client saw N `round_end` packets but froze fewer than N - 1 tapes (the last round end can land in the run's final second), `<scen>.tape-fps` when a frozen tape with 120+ frames ran outside 50 to 70 fps. A note per client with the last frozen tape.
- **Schema:** `player_died` now requires `weapon` (a string). `history_status` is not in the schema on purpose: if the server ever relayed it, `schema.unknown-type` would fail the run.
- **`GodotClient`** gained `tapes()` (every `📼 [Tape]` line as a dict) and `slot()` (the seat from the `Assigned Player ID` line). The `tape` scenario waits for both seat lines before it starts, because the log lags the packet.

## Fixed on the way
- `bool(node.get("is_stuck"))` on a projectile with no `is_stuck` raised `Invalid call. Nonexistent 'bool' constructor` 84 times in the first run (a `null` cannot go through `bool()` in GDScript 4). Now `node.get("is_stuck") == true`.

## Not in this build (v0.1.0)
- Playback of the frozen tape, the VHS look, and whether the 2.6 s round gap must grow to fit a replay.
- Tapes of spectators on the deck; a "tape missing" alert; the kill picture drawn from the stamp's `fighters`.
