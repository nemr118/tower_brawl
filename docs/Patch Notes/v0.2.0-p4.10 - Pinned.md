# v0.2.0-p4.10 - Pinned

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Phase:** the morning before the second gathering night. The user: "Big arrows and smoke should be by area. Big areas should also effect the explosive arrows. Big arrows, infinite ammo, flying, etc let's limit those to 1 round. Let make deaths more interesting. The killing arrows should embed into the body and have momentum, carry the body a bit. If the dead body hits a wall, pin the body to the wall with the arrow. Bots should be able to utilize all power ups and actively seek them out." And: "a super + space app to launch the server, and pull up the ignition dashboard". · **Harness:** the proofs below

> A killing arrow now stays in the body and carries it a little way. If the body hits a wall on the way, it hangs there, pinned by the arrow. Bots go after power-ups hard, and they can use all of them, including FLYING and the BIG ones. BIG ARROWS, INFINITE, FLYING and SHIELD last one round. Smoke and BIG ARROWS grow by area. On the ROG, Super+Space "Tower Brawl Server" starts everything and shows the links phones need.

## Changes
- **By area (the user's calls):**
  - smoke covers 25% more area than before p4.9 (257 px, was 230);
  - BIG ARROWS' blasts (bombs too), fire and clouds cover exactly 3 times the area (1.73 times as wide).
- **One round:** BIG ARROWS, INFINITE ARROWS, FLYING and SHIELD end with the round. The arrow kinds (fire, bomb, poison, smoke) still last the whole match, as first asked.
- **Pinned deaths:**
  - A killing arrow (plain, fire or smoke) stays in the body. The body takes 70% of the arrow's speed, slows a little and falls slowly for a third of a second: it goes about 170 px, then falls on.
  - If the carried body meets a wall, it hangs there. The arrow sticks in the wall through it, and the body shows its hit pose with a thunk, a flash and a shake.
  - A living player can take the arrow out of the wall (the body falls) or out of a body on the floor, so arrows are not lost.
  - Bombs still blow up, and poison still bursts into its cloud.
  - The page draws an arrow in a body on the body it draws.
- **Bots and power-ups:**
  - Bots go for orbs much harder: EASY 70%, NORMAL 90%, HARD every time. They wait 170 px beside a warning and dash in after the strike.
  - They can take FLYING, with a new flying brain: it heads for the orb, else a spot beside its target to shoot from, else somewhere to roam, going over or under walls and away from warnings.
  - They can take a BIG one when they are behind the leader. A BIG one still lands near the person furthest behind; only when no person is behind does it land near a bot.
  - Flying, sped up or slowed bots steer by sight, since the route map is made for walking at normal speed.
  - A warning near a bot now comes first, even in the middle of a move or in the air (a wall in the way: it jumps). Lightning deaths dropped from 25 to 16 in 60 bot rounds.
- **The ROG's Super+Space "Tower Brawl Server"** (`tools/rog_server.sh`, `tools/rog_links.py`):
  - it starts whatever is not running (the pages and the old game, the new game with its host port);
  - it opens the host page, plus a links page with the link phones open on each network and a QR code to scan;
  - it shows the phone link in a notification.
- **Test knob:** the server's `--pin-demo` pins seat 7 to a wall and carries seat 6 at each FIGHT, for pictures.
