# v0.2.0-p4.11 - Ghosts and Awards

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Phase:** the day of the second gathering night. The user: "I like the emote time / awards idea. Create awards for a wide variety of things, many of them should be silly. That way the kids get awards even if they didn't perform well. I like the ghosts idea, go ahead and implement that." And: "the contrast between platform, bridge, solid ground and background is too little... Keep a high quality look"; then, after a first try with drawn lines: "Is there a way we could both increase the visibility and add detail?" · **Harness:** the proofs below

> When a person is out, their ghost rises and keeps playing: it floats through walls, and letting go of shoot does a BOO dash that shoves someone (an arrow pops the ghost for 3 s). Every match ends with awards, many of them silly, and everyone gets one. The arenas' shapes now stand out from the backdrop through depth and light, and the tiles keep their detail.

## Changes
- **Ghosts (people only, so 7 bot ghosts never swarm the last two):**
  - 1.25 s after a person dies, their ghost rises from the body, a see-through sheet ghost in their colour.
  - The stick floats it anywhere, through walls.
  - Letting go of shoot does a BOO dash the way you aim. The first living fighter it meets is shoved (knocked back, never killed; a dodge is safe). Then it waits 2.5 s.
  - An arrow through a ghost pops it for 3 s.
  - Your own ghost has a white outline, and a ring when the BOO is ready. A line tells you how to play it.
- **Awards at every match's end:**
  - The server counts each player's match: kills by kind, deaths by cause, stomps, catches, jumps, dodges, shots, empty-bow shots, pickups, power-ups, pins, BOOs, first out, time alive, time in the air, flying, invisible or a ghost, steps run, rounds won.
  - 34 awards, many silly: CAT FOOD, PANCAKE, BUTTERFINGERS, COUCH POTATO, NAP TIME, OOPS!, WALL DECORATION, EXTRA CRISPY, LIGHTNING ROD...
  - Everyone gets one: the rarest award they lead, or a silly one (FASHION ICON, BEST WIGGLE, CERTIFIED SILLY...). Up to 3 more are added.
  - They show under the winner's banner after the montage, and on a "LAST MATCH AWARDS" card beside the lobby until the next match starts. `[AWARD]` lines go in the log.
- **The arenas' shapes stand out, and keep their detail:**
  - Nothing is drawn over the tiles. Behind them, a soft dark halo surrounds every block, with a shadow under every ledge (only the fringe shows, on the backdrop).
  - On the light layer, a faint warm light falls on each walkable top, a cool light on the walls, and a warm glint on the ledges and bridges.
  - The backdrop is a step dimmer and cooler.
