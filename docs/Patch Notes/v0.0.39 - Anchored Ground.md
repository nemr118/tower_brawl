# v0.0.39 - Anchored Ground

**Date:** 2026-09-07 · **Phase:** user decision: the ground slabs stay put, the rest of the stage turns above them; the v0.1.0 release candidate · **Harness:** 24/24 with `--restart-each`, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.39.json](../harness_report_v0.0.39.json) · sheet: [[Playtest — Master Validation Suite]] section 4 · release: [[release-v0.1.0]]

> The arena shift used to turn the whole stage, floor included, half a turn around the middle of the screen. The two ground slabs sit 209 px from that middle, half the screen height is 180 px, so they swung past the top edge mid-turn and ended up at the top edge after an odd number of turns (backlog 9 and 22). Now the floor is anchored: when the arena loads, `GroundLeft` and `GroundRight` are moved out of the spinning `Platforms` group into a sibling node, `Ground`, at the same place. The four ledges, the tower and the apex still turn as a group. The floor is always the floor. Two files changed, `scripts/arena.gd` and `scripts/bot_brain.gd`; the scene file is untouched.

## The design call
- The v0.0.32 free fall fixed the ride but felt terrible (v0.0.38 reverted it). Pinning the floor keeps the platforms solid, removes the flipped floor at the top edge (backlog 22 closed), and gives a fighter knocked off a turning piece a floor to land on instead of a tumble out of the bottom.
- The outer ledges (216 px from the pivot, 110 px wide) still swing about 30 px past the top edge mid-turn, so backlog 9 stays open, on the ledges only. And the turning ledges sweep through the outer corners of the anchored slabs mid-turn, so a fighter standing at the very end of a slab can be shoved or clipped for a moment. That is new backlog 24, accepted for the v0.1.0 playtest; the arena configurations fix both (outer ledges inside 165 px of the pivot, or soft turning pieces through `SHIFT_SOFT_PLATFORMS`).
- Ideas weighed and set aside, for the record: rotating every piece in place (a half turn of a symmetric rectangle changes nothing; a quarter turn makes walls, which is a new layout type for the configurations list), zooming out during the turn (no camera in the arena yet), shrinking the group while it turns.

## Changes
- **`scripts/arena.gd`.** `const ANCHORED_PLATFORMS = ["GroundLeft", "GroundRight"]`, `var ground_node`. New `_anchor_ground()`, the first thing `_ready` does: makes a `Node2D` named `Ground` at `Platforms`' position and calls `reparent()` on each anchored slab. `Platforms` is still unturned at that moment (rotation 0 from the scene), so the global transform is kept and the local positions stay `(±140, 155)`; the rejoin rotation later in `_ready` then turns only what is left under `Platforms`. `_platform_tops` (the round-start spots) walks both nodes: the turning pieces mirror on an odd flip count as before, the anchored slabs never mirror. `_set_platforms_solid`, the spin tween, the tape's `rot` and the replay's use of `Platforms` are unchanged and never touch the floor.
- **`scripts/bot_brain.gd`.** The brain's platform map reads `arena.platforms_node` and `arena.ground_node`, so the floor stays on it.
- **What a player sees.** The banner comes up, the ledges, tower and apex turn upside down above the floor, the floor does not move. Fighters on the floor stay put (except at the outer slab corners, backlog 24). Fighters on a turning piece ride it; the outer ledges pass above the top edge for up to a second (backlog 9).
- **Docs.** Master sheet section 4 rewritten for the anchored floor (floor, ledge and mid-air cases, the two known costs, a four-way verdict). [[release-v0.1.0]], PASSDOWN and the sheet name v0.0.39 as the release candidate. Backlog 22 moved to closed, 24 opened.

## Harness
- **Full suite:** 24/24 with `--restart-each`, no Minor, no `🧗 [TopEdge]` note in the run (0 finding kinds, 0 warning kinds on every scenario). The heavy 120 s fleet run (seed 13) was not repeated: the ledge ride is the expected result and section 4 of the sheet judges it.
- **Live checks before the bump (headless clients on the v0.0.38 server):** two brains for 75 s: three shifts (`flips=` 0, 2, 3), no script error in either log, one `🧗 [TopEdge]` line (the ledge ride, expected). Four brains for 40 s: the round 1 `🧭 [Spawn]` line lists the floor spot `(180, 302)` next to the ledge and apex spots, so the anchored slabs are on the spot picker's list.
