# v0.2.0-p4.9 - Power-Ups

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Phase:** before the second gathering night. The user: "The power arrows were really cool, but the randomness makes them hard to use. Let's create some powerups that drop... Use the bolt of lightning from our bench test... If anyone is standing in the area it spawns when the lightning hits, kill them and have electricity course through their dead body"; a BIG power-up near the players behind ("a blue turtle shell mechanic from mario kart"); "make sure the power ups have clear animations"; the user's daughter wanted "something with sending a cat out at other people"; "a fun halloween specific arena". · **Harness:** the proofs below
**Report:** `bench/tools/power_probe.gd`, `tools/v2_power_shots.py`

> Power-up orbs now drop during play. A crackling warning with a 3-2-1 countdown shows where the next one lands. Then the lightning strikes and kills anyone standing there. The orb waits to be grabbed. Arrow power-ups and the other regular ones last the whole match. BIG power-ups appear only for the person furthest behind: ghost cats, rain, invisibility, freeze, a spiked shield or super speed. Smoke is bigger and blinds whoever stands in it. A new Halloween arena, THE HAUNTED HOUSE, joins MIX.

## How a drop works
- 1 s after FIGHT a warning starts on a random floor spot: a pale circle where the bolt will kill, a ring closing in, a column of light, crackling arcs, the power-up's picture and name over it, and a big 3, 2, 1.
- After 2.5 s the bench test's lightning bolt strikes there (a flash, a shock ring, the screen shakes). Anyone within 80 px dies (cause: lightning) and the electricity runs through the body for 1.8 s.
- The orb then floats there, bobbing, with a moving picture inside and its name under it. Touch it and it is yours. Nobody takes it in 10 s: it blinks and fades.
- 3 s after it is taken (or gone) the next warning starts. One orb at a time.
- Who took what: a pop over them with its name and what it does (everyone sees it), chips under their card on the HUD, badges over their head. On your own screen a banner in the middle: the picture, the name, what it does.

## The regular power-ups (they last the whole match)
| Power-up | What it does |
|---|---|
| FIRE / BOMB / POISON / SMOKE ARROWS | every arrow you shoot is that kind; the newest one you take wins, and it stacks with the rest |
| SHIELD | the next arrow that hits you bounces off (no blast, no cloud); stomps still kill |
| INFINITE ARROWS | your quiver never runs out |
| FLYING | no falling: the stick (W/S or up/down) floats you any way; people only (bots walk their routes) |
| BIG ARROWS | your arrows are 3 times as big and reach further; their blast, fire and clouds cover 3 times the area |

## The BIG power-ups (Mario Kart's help from the back)
- At most one a round. It only comes when a person has fewer points than the leader. The chance is 30% of a drop, and 15% more for each extra point behind.
- It lands 150 to 520 px from the person furthest behind, never where its bolt would hit anyone. Only a person behind the leader can take it: the leader and the bots pass through it.

| BIG power-up | What it does |
|---|---|
| GHOST CATS | the user's daughter's idea: 3 pale glowing cats float through walls after the nearest players and kill on touch. A dodge into a cat catches it. |
| RAIN | everyone else runs at 55% speed for 8 s; your umbrella keeps you dry and bounces off anyone who stomps you |
| INVISIBLE | 12 s of blinking: 3 s gone, 1 s seen. Bots cannot see you; your own screen shows you faint. |
| FREEZE | everyone else is frozen in ice for 2.5 s (they still fall, and can still be hit) |
| SPIKED SHIELD | 20 s: arrows bounce off, the shield comes back 3 s after each one; spikes on top kill anyone who stomps you |
| SUPER SPEED | you run 1.6 times as fast for 10 s |

ROOT was left out: FREEZE does the same job and is easier to see.

## Smoke
- The smoke cloud is 25% wider: 288 px, was 230.
- Standing in smoke, you cannot see any other player: their bodies are hidden, a grey veil covers the screen, and a line says so. Bots inside smoke see nobody.

## THE HAUNTED HOUSE
- A new map: a house cut open, with the roof and chimney, an attic in two rooms, two floors and the hall. There is a dead tree on the left, a crypt and a balcony on the right, and tombstones in the yard.
- The arena probe passes every rule. 200 bot rounds: the median round is 10.7 s and no bot got stuck. Bot routes were written for it; the other arenas' routes are unchanged.
- The Halloween look, on every look:
  - a purple night, a big orange moon and bats;
  - the house's dark shape with flickering windows;
  - jack-o'-lanterns instead of torches, cobwebs and a purple mist.
- It is in the lobby's ARENA list and in MIX.
- The host page's ARENA now reads MIX, KEEP, BRIDGES, HAUNTED. Its POWER switch now turns the power-ups on and off.

## Changes (where)
- **`sim/sim.gd`, `sim/proto.gd`:**
  - the drop cycle, the strike, pickups, the regular and BIG power-ups, ghost cats, rain, shields, and the stomp rules (spikes, umbrella);
  - the snapshot's new power-up part (older readers stop before it), the BIG arrow bit, and the power-up fields in `write_fighter_state`, so prediction follows flying, rain and freeze.
- **`sim/brain.gd`:** bots go for orbs (35/55/75% by level), step away from a warning, see nobody inside smoke, and never see someone invisible.
- **`server/game_server.gd`:**
  - POWER-UPS is on by default (`--no-powerups` turns it off); the lobby's and the host's power switch control it;
  - kept power-ups are cleared for a new match and for a new person in a seat;
  - `[POWER]` lines, and `power` lines in `v2_server.jsonl`;
  - the test knob `--pu-gallery`.
- **Page:** `client/powers_view.gd` (new), `client/theme_view.gd` (new), plus `world.gd`, `hud.gd`, `net.gd`, `present.gd`, `fx.gd`, `lobby.gd`, `deathcam.gd` and `game_client.gd`. The test knob `bolt_s=` holds each lightning bolt on screen for pictures.
- **Tools:** `bench/tools/power_probe.gd` (new), `tools/v2_power_shots.py` (new), and the BIG arrow bit in `tools/v2_synth.py`.
- **Research:** `docs/reference/dynamic-ideas.md` (a helper's research: more ways to make matches dynamic, and holiday themes).
- **Fixed after the first deploy (same build number, redeployed before anyone loaded it):**
  - an invisible fighter leaves no dust, sparks or pops on the others' screens;
  - the deathcam's replay never hides anyone for smoke (the montage makes the winner "you");
  - once a round is decided, nothing from the power-ups hurts anyone: the cats go and a warning never strikes, as the purple walls melt (a bolt or a cat could catch the winner in the 1.5 s before the point);
  - a dodge catches a ghost cat from farther, so dodging into one stops it.
- **The night report** (`tools/v2_night_report.py`) has a POWER-UPS part (what was taken, by whom, the BIG ones) and the new causes of death.
