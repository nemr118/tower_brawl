# v0.2.0-p4.19 - Second Helpings

**Date:** 2026-11-24 · **Phase:** the polish before Thanksgiving. The user, after p4.18: "Let's continue polishing before we deploy. [...] I want to create a fun, balanced, silly game for all the kids to enjoy at thanksgiving. Make it as polished as possible. It should look app store quality with the UI, menus, splash screens, etc."; "Yes, fix all bugs. Run a soak if you think that'll help catch more bugs"; "winner is safe, but can we do something silly to show the winner would have died?" · **Harness:** the proofs below

> The 12 bugs from the review are fixed. The round's winner is safe, and a late hit bounces off as a drumstick with a PHEW. The game has sound. Every screen around the match is redone: the loading screen, the first tap, SETTINGS, HOW TO PLAY, the name cards, the phone's sticks and buttons. The bots are called GRAVY and STUFFING, there is a GOBBLE button, and your own bomb no longer knocks you out.

Three helpers worked in their own copies (the bugs, the sound, the page around the game) while the lobby, the match's look, the silly things and the balance were done here; all merged and checked together.

## The 12 bugs ([[game-review-2026-11]])
| Bug | Now |
|---|---|
| 1. A dropped phone lost its seat after 8 s | The seat is held 60 s in a match (20 s in the lobby). The page takes its own seat back by itself: the same seat, colour and points, no tap on JOIN |
| 2. After a freeze a page's clock ran far ahead for good | The ping the clock uses is capped at 300 ms, and the server's margin byte is clamped |
| 3. A freed seat's fighter could win the round | Its fighter goes out of the round at once (a normal death, cause 12 "left the game"); the point never goes to an empty seat |
| 4. The round's winner could still be killed in the 1.5 s before the point | **The winner is safe** (`sim.gd safe`; every death goes through `_kill`). A hit that would have killed says `E_PHEW` (45); the page's own copy is told (`OWN_SAFE`) so it stays in step |
| 5. The server never noticed a silent phone | 3 s of nothing is a drop; a known token takes its own seat over on a new socket, never a second seat |
| 6. After a server restart an open page played without prediction | The prediction and the last inputs start over with a new socket |
| 7. A thumb already on the glass when the sticks appear was ignored | It works from its first move; a cancelled touch lowers the bow and fires nothing |
| 8. SKIP worked from the replay's first frame, where right thumbs play | SKIP wakes up 2 s in and sits at the top in the middle. On a PC the space bar (the JUMP key) no longer skips; Esc and Enter do |
| 9. The winner's screen got what the replay left of 12 s | The server adds the replay's length: the winner's screen always gets 8 s |
| 10. Four lists in the page only grew | Capped (the newest 20 000 each) |
| 11. Every lobby said "ONE MORE STEP" after a Wi-Fi blip | Only when the socket has never opened. After a drop: "The connection dropped. Joining again by itself…" |
| 12. A socket the server could not send to kept its seat "on" | It counts as a drop |

The chaos soak found one more: a client's junk made a script error in two lobby handlers (the message was dropped, nothing else). Fixed.

## The look (the user: "app store quality")
- **The page around the game** (`bench/web/shell.html`, a helper): the loading screen fills the screen at every shape with the season's picture, a gold bar with real progress, tips for kids, the version in a corner, and a fade into the lobby. A big TAP TO PLAY button. A drawn phone that turns, with three iPhone steps. One friendly card with TRY AGAIN for every failure. A painted app icon in every size (`tools/v2_page_art.py`), a fuller `manifest.json`.
- **SETTINGS** (`client/lobby_settings.gd`): one button in place of LOOK, CONTROLS and SEASON in the corners. Every choice shows and the one in use is lit: LOOK, CONTROLS (phones), SOUND, and for everyone SEASON.
- **In a match** (`client/hud.gd`, polish "hud2"): the name cards in the lobby's style (the seat's colour, the fighter's head, the name in the title's letters, gold pips, a painted crown on the leader). Your power-ups are pills with a picture and a word; the others' are pictures only. The power-up card and the notes are pills. The arena's labels use the lobby's bold letters.
- **HOW TO PLAY** (polish "howto2"): the real fighters run, shoot, dodge and catch.
- **A phone's sticks and buttons** (`client/touch_sticks.gd`, polish "sticks2"): pads with a double rim, knobs with a shine, DODGE (orange) and JUMP (green) as buttons with a lip, see-through at rest. The long hints go once the player has jumped and shot a few times.
- WHO ARE YOU? wears the lobby's dress. The look note says "Picture set to LOW to stay smooth. You can change it in SETTINGS."

Each piece has its switch in `client/polish.gd` (`?polish_off=hud2,howto2,settings,sticks2,cheers` brings the old look back for a picture).

## Sound (a helper)
- 37 cartoon sounds made from plain maths (`tools/make_sfx.py`, no recordings): a bow's twang, a bonk and a squeak, a boing, a whoosh, gobbles, a pie's splat and a wah-wah, a lid's clang and ta-da, 3-2-1 and FIGHT, a sad trombone for a draw, a kazoo fanfare for the match, a slide whistle for PHEW.
- `client/sfx.gd`: 8 voices, your own actions a little louder, 3 new sounds a frame at most.
- ON for a PC and THE BIG SCREEN, OFF for a phone until SETTINGS turns it on (8 phones in one room, and iPhones are short of memory). With sound off nothing is loaded.
- A browser plays no sound before one tap: the page wakes it on the first tap anywhere; the TV lobby says "Click this screen once to turn the sound on".
- An Android phone buzzes for a moment when you are knocked out, land a shot, take a pie or a power-up (an iPhone's browser cannot).

## Silly things
- **PHEW** (`client/powers_view.gd`): what hit the winner after the call bounces off as a drumstick (a rubber chicken out of the Harvest season), TOO LATE!, and PHEW! in a bubble over the winner with drops of sweat.
- **The bots have names** by the season (`proto.gd BOT_NAMES`): GRAVY, STUFFING, YAMS, BISCUIT, DRUMSTICK, CRANBERRY, CORNBREAD, WISHBONE. "YAMS WINS THE ROUND".
- **A silly line under each round's winner** (polish "cheers"): PASS THE GRAVY!, SAVE ROOM FOR PIE!, STUFFED!...
- **A taunt:** a GOBBLE button on a phone, G or T on a PC. A speech bubble and a gobble at your seat's own pitch, for everyone. A bot gloats after 2 knockouts in 5.

## Balance (`bench/tools/balance_probe.gd`, and October's logs)
| What the numbers said | The change |
|---|---|
| On 24 and 25 October 40 of the 60 times a person died in a blast, it was their own bomb. A bomb's taker won about 20% of rounds; the other power-ups' takers about 50% | **Your own blast throws you far; it does not knock you out** (`sim.gd self_harm`). With bots a bomb's taker goes from 14% to 23% (any power-up: 28%), and draws from 5% to 1% |
| A pie's shooter was creamed by its own pie, and the cream landed on someone else once in 40 rounds | Your own pie does not cream you; the cream reaches 300 px (it was 230). A pie's taker: 18% to 24% |
| A 2-fighter round lasted 4.4 s (bots); two could start side by side | With 2 to 4 fighters the starts are far apart (`far_starts`) |
| A fighter who starts with the catch-up SHIELD wins 37% of 4-fighter rounds (a fair share: 25%) | Left as it is: a real help, not a win |
| The start spots: on THE BIG TABLE the best wins 15.6% and the worst 7.9% of 8-bot rounds (fair: 12.4%) | Left as it is: the spots are shuffled every round, so nobody keeps a bad one |

**To watch at the family check:** among bots THE BIG TURKEY's wearer wins 45% of 8-fighter rounds and 60% of 4-fighter rounds (its lunge makes no difference to that; it is the two hits). October's people were weak in the costume, so it is left alone. The night's log now says the costume's own win rate.

## A comfort
The lobby's settings (the bots, their level, the arena, power-ups, the season) are kept over a restart (`<out>/lobby_settings.json`; a command-line option wins). October's server came up with 7 bots every time, and the family plays with 0 or 1.

- When `index.wasm` failed to download, the old page sat on its loading bar for good. Now the card with TRY AGAIN comes.
- Godot's robot stood in for the Home Screen icon on an iPhone. Now it is ours.
- The picture tools left their browser profiles in `/tmp` (92 of them, 3.7 GB of memory). Removed, and the tools clean up now.
