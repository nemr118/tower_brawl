# v0.1.5 - Bot Routes

**Date:** 2026-09-10 · **Phase:** bot routes: the bots find their way up and down the tower, and two guards end a standoff · **Harness:** 25/25 with --restart-each, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.1.5.json](../harness_report_v0.1.5.json)

> The bots could not find their way up or down the tower. Their brain was written for the old arena, where high ground is reached by falling through the bottom seam; the tower has a ledge under almost every column, so a bot whose target stood straight above or below it stood still or jumped on the spot, and a round could run for ten hours. Now the bots read the tower as a map of places to stand and the moves between them, and two safety nets end any standoff that is left.

## Why every soak stalled
- **The laptop logs (2026-09-08 to 2026-09-10).** Matches 28, 33, 36, 39 and 44 all ended on an idle reset after hours, and the run live on 2026-09-10 had stalled too: three chasers stacked in one column at (387, 82), (392, 582) and (405, about 780), frozen for 20 minutes, the lowest one jumping on the spot. The overnight run of 2026-09-08 had a rusher and a griefer pinned against the left wall for ten hours ([[bot-soak-2026-09-08]]).
- **Cause 1, no way down or up.** `_steer_to` looked for an empty column to fall through (`_nearest_drop_x`). In the tower every column has a ledge under it, so there was never one: a target below meant "stand still", a target more than one jump above meant "jump on the spot" (88 477 jumps in one log). The brain never used down + jump.
- **Cause 2, the griefer's "seam" mood.** It steered for "the outermost platform on the far side", which in the tower is a wall piece at x -48: it held `left` into the wall for hours.
- **Cause 3, nothing ends a standoff.** Rounds have no clock (backlog 28), and a sniper keeping its distance or a turtle holding its ground never closes in.

## Changes
- **`scripts/bot_nav.gd` (new): the tower as a route map.** Built from the layout table (`arena_layouts.gd`) at load: 25 places to stand (ledge, bump and floor tops) and the moves between them. A **jump** reaches a surface up to `JUMP_RISE` 75 px higher: straight up through a one-way ledge, or from the end of a surface to a ledge beside it (a gap up to `JUMP_GAP` 56 px; up to `JUMP_GAP_DASH` 96 px with a dash at the apex, "need_dash"). A **drop** goes down through a one-way ledge. A **walk-off** walks off a free end; the floor hole comes out of the ceiling hole. Falls are planned with `player.gd`'s own numbers: `FALL_GRAVITY` up to the `MAX_FALL_SPEED` 600 cap, and air steering that starts a reaction delay late (`REACT` 0.25 s). A landing must be reachable and nothing higher may catch the fall on the way (three columns under the path; a fall through the floor hole is checked in two halves). `next_move(from, x, to, penalty)` is a shortest-route search that returns the first move.
- **`scripts/bot_brain.gd`: routes.** `_steer_to` follows the route when the target stands on another surface: walk to the move's take-off stretch (the position predicted over the reaction delay), then press. In the air `_air_steer` heads for the landing surface's inner stretch. A move that lands anywhere else, or a press that never leaves the floor, is a **miss**: that move costs `NAV_MISS_COST` 150 more for `NAV_MISS_FORGET_S` 60 s, so the next route goes another way. Roam and retreat targets are only places to stand now (they used to include walls and the ceiling). The griefer's "seam" mood is gone.
- **The drop-through, the bot way.** Down first; the jump only after the fighter has stood on the ledge for `DROP_STEADY_FRAMES` 3 frames with down held (`_physics_process` step 4c). See "Found on the way".
- **A wide jump dashes only when it has to.** The dash waits for the apex (a dash ignores gravity, `player.gd` sets `velocity = dash_dir * DASH_SPEED`) and fires only if the ledge is still more than `LEAP_NEED_PX` 28 px away; before, it overshot near ledges and ran off the far end.
- **Two stall guards.** **Hunt:** no kill anywhere for `HUNT_AFTER_S` 20 s (heard through `Global.net_player_died` and `net_new_round`) and every persona but the rusher plays the chaser until the next kill. **Detour:** steering that wants to move but has not left its surface or got 24 px along it for `STUCK_S` 4 s heads for a random place to stand for `DETOUR_S` 3 s. Both print a `🧭 [BotNav]` line.
- **The status line and the card.** `🧠 [Bot ...]` ends in `| nav surf= steps= misses= stucks= hunt=` (after the old fields, so the harness regex still matches); the bot card's `nav` gains `steps`, `misses`, `stucks`, and its `state` shows `hunt` or `detour`.
- **`tools/nav_probe.gd` (new).** The real tower bodies, one real fighter, one brain told where to go (`goal_override`), and trips between surfaces: first the six spots where the soaks deadlocked, then random ones. `--only=N` traces one trip frame by frame; `--drop-test` checks the drop-through by hand. Runs at `--fixed-fps 60`, about 8 simulated minutes in 4 s.

## Found on the way (reported, not fixed)
- **Down and jump pressed in the same frame jump instead of dropping through** (backlog 31). `nav_probe.gd --drop-test`: with both pressed in one frame the duck changes the collision shape, `is_on_floor()` is false on the frame the drop check needs, and a plain jump fires (vy -430); with down held 10 frames first, the fighter drops. Also: while ducked at rest `_on_ledge()` read false for 3 frames in a row. For people: a keyboard S + W pressed together jumps; on twin sticks a quick flick straight past the down rim sends the down axis and the jump tap in the same `_twin_move` call. A slow push (duck first, then the rim) works.
- **A bot added right after `tbbot clear` never gets a seat** (backlog 32). The server holds a leaving player's seat 8 s (the rejoin grace), so bots started in that window get `[JOIN] rejected ... all 4 seats are taken` and `--autojoin` does not ask again: four spectators, no match (seen on the PC, 18:02:39). Wait 10 s between `clear` and `add`; the fix is a retry in the autojoin, or `tbbot add` waiting for a free seat.
