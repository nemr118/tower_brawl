# v0.2.0-p4.13 - The Boiler Room

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Phase:** the second gathering night. The user asked for a polished nightmare boiler room ("walking on pipes or industrial grating, dynamic background that has areas that shoot fire out of a furnace, killing nearby people, pressure pipes that bulge then release steam... Lean into the scary"), then a bed and lockers for the passages; candies "separate from the normal orb spawns. Make corpses drop candies and occasional random spawns"; a lobby that is less "plain and uninspiring"; more detail on the assets; ComfyUI to make pictures; and a full screen for the iPhones. Every look change was shown to the user first (BEFORE/AFTER pictures) and only what they picked is in. · **Harness:** the proofs below

> THE BOILER ROOM is here: furnaces that blast fire, pipes that blow steam, a swinging hook, the lights going out with a tall shadow in the furnace glow, and a nightmare surge. TRICK OR TREAT candies now pop out of fallen players and fall from the sky, with a big moment when opened. The lobby is new, the arenas have far more detail, the power-ups and awards have pictures made with ComfyUI, and iPhones can play full screen from the Home Screen.

## THE BOILER ROOM (a helper's build; an original homage: no name, face or hat, the repo is public)
- **The room:** riveted steel, grating catwalks, pipe walkways striped red and green, the great boiler in the middle (a safety valve on top, the firebox under it), a water tank, hanging pipes, brick vaults with claw marks.
- **Furnaces (3):** the left and right walls and the boiler's firebox. A 1.75 s warning (flames lick out, the walkway glows red, sparks), then 1 s of fire that kills (cause "a furnace"). One at a time; each rests 8 to 13 s.
- **Pressure pipes (4):** two out of the boiler's sides, two floor vents. A 1.25 s bulge (swells red, the gauge climbs, a hiss), then 1.5 s of steam that knocks fighters (no kill), blows bodies along, bends arrows and blows stuck ones loose. A floor vent throws you up onto the catwalk above.
- **The hook:** its chain rattles, then it swings across the top walkway twice and knocks people off.
- **Lights out** (every 18 to 26 s): the bulbs flicker, then 3 s of dark with only the furnaces glowing, and a tall thin shadow with knife fingers stands in one's light (look only).
- **The nightmare surge** (every 25 to 32 s): the room pulses red like a heartbeat, a giant shadow rises behind the boiler, then every furnace fires at once.
- **The bed and the lockers (the user's):** fall into a bed over a floor gap and the sheet rises, pale hands hold you 0.3 s and pull you through; you drop out of the ceiling with feathers. Walk into a side locker: its door swings open and slams, and the other side's door bursts open as you come out.
- Every hazard warns first and comes no closer than 71 px to a spawn spot; the first warning 5 s after FIGHT. The bots step away from warnings (`boiler.gd flee()`).
- It is in the ARENA list and in MIX.

## Changes
- **Candies (TRICK OR TREAT):**
  - no longer an orb. A body drops a candy half the time; one falls from the sky every 15 to 25 s; at most 3 at once;
  - a candy bounces onto a floor, wiggles with a glow and a pulsing ring, goes after 10 s; whoever touches it first opens it (bots do not go looking for candies);
  - opening one: wrapper confetti and a big candy-striped badge with what came out (the treat, PUMPKIN HEAD, BACKWARDS, TINY or GHOSTFACE);
  - a treat is a BIG power-up 1 time in 6 only for someone behind the leader (the BIG rule);
  - GHOSTFACE now comes about once every 3 rounds; a new award, SWEET TOOTH.
- **The lobby (a helper's overhaul):** a real title (a jack-o'-lantern O and a HALLOWEEN NIGHT ribbon in October), 8 seat cards with each fighter in its colour, game-style buttons, mist, embers and bats, a 3-2-1 countdown that punches in, and a winner's screen with a podium, rays, confetti and award cards. Fonts: Noto Sans Condensed (SIL OFL 1.1).
- **ComfyUI pictures (made on this PC, Z-Image Turbo and FLUX.2 klein):**
  - the loading picture: painted archers on the battlements under the moon, with the logo;
  - the 17 power-up pictures (orbs, badges, your power-up card), on darker discs;
  - a picture on each of the 46 awards on the winner's screen.
- **The look (a helper's polish, each piece behind a switch in `client/polish.gd`):** THE HAUNTED HOUSE (lit windows, a moon with a witch, jack-o'-lanterns, spiders, graves, mist), THE KEEP and THE BRIDGES (a new backdrop with banners, depth, moss, chains, iron sconces), new arrows, light shafts with dust (the old ones never drew).
- **iPhones:** Share, then "Add to Home Screen": the game opens with no bars. The game's connection goes through the page's own address (`serve_game.py /v2ws`), so one certificate is enough; the page falls back to port 8091 if that fails. A small tip shows on iPhones.
- **The ROG app** (Super+Space, "Tower Brawl Server") opens the firewall to whatever network the laptop is on (one password box).
- **Small fixes:** the ghost's tip ends at the next round; the page shell cannot throw on a quick reload.
