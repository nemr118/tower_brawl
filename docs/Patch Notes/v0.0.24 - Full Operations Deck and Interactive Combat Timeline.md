# v0.0.24 - Full Operations Deck and Interactive Combat Timeline

**Date:** 2026-09-04 · **Phase:** before Phase 3c: the deck as a tool for the replay work · **Harness:** 19/19 with `--restart-each`, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.24.json](../harness_report_v0.0.24.json)

> The terminal deck (`tbdash`) now reads the keyboard and the mouse: you can pan and zoom the fight strip, click a second to see what happened in it, filter the log, pause and save a snapshot. The strip shows every kill at 1 s per column with the weapon and the colours of the two fighters, and the bot arena shows every bot, not just the brains.

## The problems
1. **The bot arena never showed.** `bot_cards()` in `tools/watch_server.py` kept only cards with `kind == "brain"`. In the suite, `fleet` runs with `--godot 2` and no `--ai`, so its Godot clients join with `"bot": "headless"` and the protocol bots with `"bot": "protocol"`. Both kinds were thrown away, and the harness `bots[]` list only fills from a `🧠 [Bot` log line, which only a brain prints. The panel had data and hid it.
2. **The fight strip was too coarse.** One column was 5 s, so kills stacked into one tick and the order of a fight was lost. A whole match did not fit either way, and there was no way to look back.
3. **Nothing on the deck could be touched.** `rich` only draws. Every question ("what happened at 1:00?") meant reading `server.log`.

## The fix, piece by piece
- **Input layer, new file `tools/deck_input.py`.** A thread reads stdin in raw mode (`termios`, `tty`, `select`, standard library). The deck prints `ESC[?1000h` + `ESC[?1006h`, so the terminal reports the mouse as `ESC[<b;x;yM`: button 64 wheel up, 65 wheel down, +4 Shift, +8 Alt, +16 Ctrl. Events out: `("key", name)`, `("wheel", step, ctrl, shift, x, y)`, `("click", button, x, y)`. Mouse off again on exit and on Ctrl+C. `python tools/deck_input.py` runs the parser self-test. `--no-mouse` keeps the keyboard only.
- **Keys and mouse.** Wheel pans the strip. Ctrl+wheel or Shift+wheel zooms around the column under the pointer through the ladder 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 30 s per column (`ZOOM_LADDER`). `←` `→` pan, `+` `-` zoom, `Home` match start, `End` or `f` back to live and follow, click a column for the inspect line, `Esc` clears it, `1`-`9` hide or show a log tag (`KILL ROUND MATCH JOIN LEAVE NAME LOCK CONN NET`), wheel over the events panel scrolls it, `p` pause, `s` saves `deck_snapshot_<HH-MM-SS>.txt` in the repo root (gitignored), `q` quit. `--zoom 5` starts at 5 s per column. Checked in `foot`: Ctrl+wheel reaches the deck.
- **The strip** (`build_strip`). One column = one bin, 1 s by default; kill times already have 0.1 s resolution (`_match_t`). The `kills/1s` row is the exact count per column (`▁` = 1, `▂` = 2 ...). Lane cells: one kill = the weapon glyph in the victim's colour (`➶` Arrow, `✦` Firebolt, `✧` Kunai, `❋` Thorns, `⚔` Melee, `▼` Goomba Stomp; `⬇` would draw two cells wide in emoji fonts), one death = `✕` in the killer's colour, kill plus death = `◈`, two or more events in one column = the digit (green kills only, red deaths only, magenta mixed). Seat colours: P1 blue, P2 red, P3 green, P4 yellow, also on the lane labels, which read `P2 Godot4 ♛4 42/24` (crowns, kills/deaths). Every second round has a `grey11` background band. Round start `┃`, round end `┫`, the winner's `♛` one column after the closing kill (that kill is the winner's own, so the crown cannot sit on it), match end `║`. Round row: `R2 21s ♛ P2`. Time axis: `┴` ticks with `m:ss`, labels at least 8 columns apart, `▶ live` on the right while following (`▶ end` for a finished match). Columns after "now" stay blank, so a young match fills in from the left. The panel opens the second a match starts; a match reset shows as a fresh empty strip with `R1`. Title: kills, round, `clock 2:40`, `kills/min`, `(last match, P2 won)`, and `view 0:00-3:49 (End = live)` when panned.
- **Inspect line.** Under the axis, for the clicked column: `1:00  Godot2 (P4) killed Andrew (P1) with Firebolt · round 1 ended, P4 Godot2 won`. Falls (`killer == victim` or 0) read `fell`. The axis shows `▲` and the column is highlighted.
- **Server stamps** (`serve_game.py`): `match_timeline["rounds"]` entries are now `[round, start_s, winner, end_s]` (`_check_round_end` writes `end_s`); `match_timeline["ended_at"]` and `["winner"]` at match over; `match_timeline["names"]` (`_stamp_names`, at match start, every round start and every kill) so the deck keeps the right names after a seat changes hands. All three appear under `match.timeline` in `status.json`. Old 3-field rounds still draw.
- **Bot arena** accepts every kind. Columns `Kind` (`brain`, `headless`, `protocol`), `Persona`, `Diff`, `State`, `K/D` (filled from the seat's kill and death counts when the bot did not send `combat.kills`), the brain numbers, `Age` (seconds since the card was updated) and `Up`. A brain with only its join card shows `no card yet` until its first `bot_status` (5 s). A column that is `-` on every row is left out, so a headless-only run stays narrow. Title: `BOT ARENA  2 brains, 1 headless client, 1 protocol bot`.
- **Height budget.** `render_rich` counts the lines of each panel. When the picture would not fit, the events panel goes first, then the traffic panel. Header, harness, seats, fight and arena are never cropped (they need about 35 lines with a live harness and 4 brains).
- **Sparklines from the deck's own memory.** The deck keeps the last 60 status reads: a `Trend` column next to the ping (lowest to highest ping in the window, shown on terminals 120 columns or wider once 3 samples exist) and two 60 s `KB/s` curves in the traffic panel with the peak.
- **Harness ETA.** The deck reads the newest `docs/harness_report_*.json` for the seconds each scenario took last time. `ETA 4m 10s` in the harness title; a running scenario at more than twice its last time (and at least 10 s over) reads `SLOW` in yellow. A run with a different `--duration` is flagged on purpose.
- **Alert line.** A red one-liner at the very top for 10 s when a new event is an `ERROR` line, a `[NET]` stall, or a harness-gate demotion / `join_locked`; several alerts show `(+2 more)`. Events already on screen when the deck starts do not fire.
- **`--once`** now prints one colour picture too (`--once --plain` for text). The `tbdash` alias in `~/.bashrc` runs the deck from any folder.

- 300 s brain fleet (`--godot 4 --ai chaser,sniper,turtle,rusher --latency-ms 120 --jitter-ms 40`): passed, 108 kills, 10 rounds, 4 bot cards from the first minute. Watched live on `tbdash` in `foot` (screenshots 00:04:16 and 00:08:44): Ctrl+wheel zoom, bands, colours, crown, inspect line, arena, curves all confirmed.
