# v0.2.0-p4.22 - Christmas Eve

**Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Phase:** the Christmas update. The user asked for a Christmas theme as big as Halloween's and the Harvest's, picked every piece of the list, KRAMPUS for the costume, THE ROOFTOPS and SANTA'S WORKSHOP for the arenas, and said go to the picture batch. Built by Fable 5.2 with four helpers; when Fable's usage limit stopped them all at 17:00, Opus 5.5 finished the work from their saved copies (the user: "Can opus finish this?"). · **Harness:** the proofs below

> The game has a Christmas season, all of December. Presents float down on parachutes, Santa's sleigh drops the power-ups, a reindeer trots in that you can ride, snowballs pack people into snowmen, and you can turn into KRAMPUS. Two new arenas: THE ROOFTOPS on Christmas Eve, and SANTA'S WORKSHOP with a toy train to jump. The castle arenas are under snow, everyone wears a winter hat they pick, and the lobby counts the sleeps to Christmas.

## New rules
- **SNOWBALLS** (`PU_SNOWBALL`, an arrow kind for the match, about 11% of the season's drops): a direct hit knocks out, as any arrow; where it lands it bursts, and everyone within 190 px is packed into a snowman for 1.5 s (frozen). A dodge, the shooter, a teammate and the round's winner are safe. Among bots its holder wins 10.3% of 8-fighter rounds (plain arrows 12.7%, PIE 13.7%): fun, not strong.
- **The reindeer** (the season's pet): it trots in 2 to 4 s into about 45% of rounds (80% on THE ROOFTOPS). Touch it and you ride it: 5 s of flying (FLYING's rule), bells jingling, then it flies off. A knockout ends the ride. KRAMPUS can ride it too.
- **Two new silly rounds:** SECRET SANTA! (everyone opens a present at the start: a power-up for this round, or a trick) and SLIP AND SLIDE! (the floor is ice this round: a slide to a stop is 11 times as long; a slab of ice and skid marks under each fighter).
- **Ten awards** (counted in the season only): NAUGHTY LIST, NICE LIST, SNOWBALL CHAMP, LUMP OF COAL, SLEIGH RIDE, UNWRAPPED, FRUITCAKE, HORNS AND BELLS, IN THE SACK, KRAMPUS CATCHER.

## Two new arenas (each built by a helper in its own copy, then merged)
- **THE ROOFTOPS** (layout 7, `client/rooftops_view.gd`): Christmas Eve, a clock tower between two alleys, snowy roofs at different heights, chimneys, strings of lights, the sleigh across the moon. Every way up is a jump of 4 rows or less. A gust of snow sweeps across (the barn's rule). `tools/rooftops_probe.gd`.
- **SANTA'S WORKSHOP** (layout 8, `client/workshop_view.gd`, `sim/workshop.gd`, three pictures from `tools/make_workshop_art.py`): a tree of four ledge tiers with the star on top, candy-cane posts, rooms of toys, a fireplace. The floor is the toy train's track: a 2 s whistle, a red glow and arrows on the track, then the train crosses in 2.25 s and knocks out whoever it touches (a new death cause, 13). A held jump clears it; every ledge is safe; the winner is safe. Among bots it is 0.5% of knockouts (EASY 1.1%, HARD 0.1%): a surprise, not the main danger. `tools/workshop_probe.gd`.
- **THE KEEP and THE BRIDGES** in the season (`client/snow_view.gd`): snow on every floor and ledge (a bright edge that also shows where to stand), icicles, wreaths round the torches, strings of lights that twinkle and flash on a knockout, falling snow, a winter sky.
- In the season MIX is THE KEEP, THE BRIDGES, THE ROOFTOPS and SANTA'S WORKSHOP. The picker shows nine cards.

## Hats (a helper; `client/hats.gd`, `server/hat_book.gd`)
- Six hats drawn in code, in each seat's colour, only in the Christmas season (polish switch "hats"): a Santa hat, an elf hat with a bell, antlers, earmuffs, a pom beanie, a snowman's top hat with holly.
- They sit on the hood in every pose. The tail swings after a run and springs back, the hat dips on a landing and tips in a roll. A knockout pops it off: it flies, bounces once and lies beside the body. TINY wears a tiny hat; an invisible fighter's hat hides with it; a costume or a trick's form wears none. A fighter packed into a snowman wears its hat on the snowman's head, so you can tell whose snowman it is.
- On the lobby cards, the match's seat cards, THE BIG SCREEN, the winner's podium and the painted hero.
- **The pick:** a HAT button on your own card gives the next hat (a pop and a jingle). A tap on the rest of the card still hops your fighter (or changes sides in TEAMS). The server checks the pick (your own seat, in the lobby, in the season) and keeps it by name in `known_hats.json` beside `known_names.json`, so the same name gets its hat back next time. A new person and each bot start with a hat by seat.

## The lobby's dress (a helper; `client/lobby_xmas.gd`)
- A Christmas palette (a winter night, pine green buttons, a gold START, CHRISTMAS BRAWL); December by date, `?dress=christmas`.
- **The title:** snow on every letter, a wreath for the O of TOWER, sparkles. The ribbon counts down by the server's date: 12 SLEEPS TO CHRISTMAS, 1 SLEEP TO CHRISTMAS, MERRY CHRISTMAS! on the 25th.
- **The lobby:** snow falling at three depths, strings of lights that twinkle, the sleigh crossing every 26 s. Snow piles up on each seat card over a minute and falls off in a puff when the card reacts.
- **The six panels** (SETTINGS, a match's MENU, TONIGHT'S CHAMPIONS, WHO ARE YOU?, PICK AN ARENA, HOW TO PLAY): lights along the top, snow behind. SEASON has CHRISTMAS. HOW TO PLAY says to ride the reindeer.
- **PICK AN ARENA:** nine cards in rows of 5 and 4, the new arenas' painted cards, the season's MIX card.
- The winner's screen: a great hall with a tree and a fire, snow and gold sparkles; the ten award pictures; snow on the round banners; the loading page in December (its picture, colours and tips).

## In a match: the look, the motion, the sounds
- One ComfyUI batch, 136 pictures, picked by eye (`tools/comfy/run_christmas.py`, `make_assets.py christmas`): the badges, KRAMPUS, the reindeer, the snowman, the presents, the sleigh, the wreath, the winner's hall, the winter sky, the arena cards, the loading picture. `client/christmas_view.gd` draws the season's things in a match.
- **New motion:** the sleigh flying over a drop and on after it, the giant present falling and bursting open, the reindeer trotting and galloping with sparkles, KRAMPUS's swish and his sack's kicking boots, the parachute swaying, white powder at a jump and a landing, footprints in the snow, breath puffs from anyone standing still out in the cold, the lights flashing on a knockout, BRRR! and SQUASH!
- **Sounds:** 12 new, made from maths like the others (`tools/make_sfx.py`): sleigh bells, a jingle, a present unwrapped, a snowball's poof, packed in snow, glass breaking, KRAMPUS (a growl and his chain), his sticks, the giant present's thud, HO HO HO, the train's whistle, its chug.
- **Songs:** three Christmas tunes are written for ACE-Step (`tools/comfy/run_music.py lobby_xmas match_xmas winner_xmas`), not made yet: a GPU run that waits for the user's go. The page plays a season's own take when there is one (`client/music.gd`).

## Measured, not changed: KRAMPUS's strength
Among bots the costume's wearer wins 47.4% of 8-fighter rounds (a fair share 12.5%). Two levers were tried (`tools/balance_probe.gd --mask-once=1`, `--mask-down=180`; 300 rounds a line on THE KEEP and THE BRIDGES): getting up from one knock-down only gave 43.2%, lying 3 s gave 41.1%. Anyone who takes any power-up wins 30.5%. Neither lever is the cause, so the rule is as it was; both stay as switches (`sim.gd mask_once`, `mask_down`). The next thing to try is the swing's reach and lunge, after a real night.

## Test knobs and tools
- The server: `--season christmas`, `--xmas-gallery` (every Christmas look at FIGHT), `--train-demo` (the train stands in the middle), `--slowmo N` (N times fewer ticks a second, for films of a match).
- The page: `?slowmatch=N` (its clock N times slower, with the server's `--slowmo N`), `?sleeps=N`, `?pile_s=N` (the cards' snow fills in N s), `?dress=christmas`, `?hatsheet`, `?hatsheet=move`.
- `tools/v2_motion_clip.py --script match --secs N` (a film of a match), `--script hats`, `hatmove`, `dress`.
- **A fix for every film:** Godot gives one frame at most 8 physics steps of time, so on the slow test page every animation played 3 to 6 times too slow in a film. The p4.21 menu film the user was sent had this flaw: the real menus are quicker than that film showed. With `?slowmo` a frame now gets its whole time.
- New probes: `christmas_probe.gd`, `rooftops_probe.gd`, `workshop_probe.gd`, `hat_probe.gd`. `motion_probe.gd`'s tap on your own card no longer races the card's own idle hop (it failed 1 run in 31 since p4.21; a helper found it).
- The night report and the soak tool know the Christmas names and arenas 7 and 8.

## Found on the way (reported, not fixed)
1. TONIGHT'S CHAMPIONS cuts nine-letter names: "FRUITCAKE (a bot" loses its bracket; CORNBREAD and CRANBERRY too.
2. Award lines with 1 and a plural: "opened 1 presents" (UNWRAPPED), "1 tricks out of presents" (LUMP OF COAL); others have the same pattern.
3. CAT WHISPERER and CAT FOOD say "ghost cats" in every season.
4. A name that starts with "player" (PlayerOne) does not keep its hat (`hat_book.gd _key`).
5. The earmuffs are the weakest hat at a phone's size; on the yellow seat their band blends into the fighter.
6. In the lobby the sleigh crosses behind the title: Santa peeks between the letters for 3 s in every 26 s (judged charming, left in). On a 16:9 PC the winner's hall's fire is behind the awards list.
7. The PC froze hard at 18:42 (the log ends with no shutdown) as the Quest stream started a session; the game's jobs then were headless, on the processor.
