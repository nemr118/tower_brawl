# v0.0.33 - Shift Tumble Kept

**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Phase:** v0.1.0 list, bug build 6: backlog 23 closed (the shift tumble stays; the fleet gate ignores it) · **Harness:** 23/23 with --restart-each, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.33.json](../harness_report_v0.0.33.json) · heavy fleet run: [seed 13](../harness_report_v0.0.33_fleet_seed13.json)

> The free fall during the arena shift is gameplay, not a bug. The bot brain no longer counts a bottom wrap while the stage turns, so `fleet.bot-fall-loop` only trips on a real fall loop. Two files changed: `scripts/bot_brain.gd` and `tools/chaos_bots.py`. The game itself is unchanged.

## The design call (backlog 23)
- **The call: keep the tumble.** When the shift starts, a fighter standing on a platform drops through the soft stage, falls out of the bottom, comes back in at the top, and does that three or four times before the new layout locks. That is the feel we want. No change to `arena.gd`, `player.gd` or the packets.
- **The problem was the gate, not the game.** `_track_wraps` in `bot_brain.gd` counts every bottom wrap and the fleet gate fails a brain at 4 wraps without a landing (`fleet.bot-fall-loop`). The gate was written for a brain stuck falling through the seam columns (backlog 8). During a 2.5 s turn a standing fighter reaches exactly 4 wraps, so the gate tripped on the tumble.

## Changes
- **`scripts/bot_brain.gd` `_track_wraps`.** Each physics frame it reads `arena.is_arena_rotating` from the fighter's parent. A bottom wrap during the turn is counted in a new stat, `shift_wraps`, instead of `wraps`; it does not add to `_loop_len` and does not start the landing clock. When a turn begins, `_loop_len` goes to 0 and `_wrap_at` to -1, so a wrap just before the shift cannot chain into the tumble. A wrap after the stage locks, while the fighter is still falling, counts as a normal wrap again.
- **The `🧠 [Bot]` line** now ends its nav part with `shift=N` (after `max_loop=`). The `bot_status` packet's `nav` dict carries `shift_wraps` too, so the deck's `harness_state.json` has it.
- **`tools/chaos_bots.py`.** `BOT_LINE` reads the new field (optional, so logs from older builds still parse), the fleet note prints `shift wraps N`, and the brain card in `harness_state.json` carries `shift_wraps`. The gate threshold (`max_loop >= 4`) is unchanged.
