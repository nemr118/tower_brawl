# v0.1.3 - The Tower

**Date:** 2026-09-08 · **Phase:** the tower arena, build 1 · **Harness:** 25/25 with `--restart-each`, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.1.3.json](../harness_report_v0.1.3.json) · the design: [[arena-tower]] · the sheet: [[Playtest — Master Validation Suite v0.1]] §6

> The arena is a tower now: one screen wide, three screens tall, hard walls just outside the screen, a hole in the floor and one in the ceiling, two side passages, one-way ledges every 70 px. A camera follows you up and down. You can duck (a shot at head height passes over), look below or above by holding, and see a marker for a fighter off your screen. The arena shift is off. The fight is slower on purpose (the user's call: "a brawl, but tactical"). The scene file was edited for the first time; rule 1 changed to allow it, with a load check.

## The user's picture (2026-09-08)
Hard outer walls with details, a hole at the bottom and the top so a fighter runs out of the bottom and comes back in up top, the same through the sides, an inside much taller than wide, a view that shifts down when you hold duck and up when you hold the aim up, the width just outside the screen and the height three screens, no arena flip for now, themes and traps later. Towerfall Ascension as the reference. Plus: slow the combat down, longer cooldowns, a bigger play area, so the dash and the teleport have a moment to be used. Every number that came out of that is in [[arena-tower]].

## Changes
- **The layout table** (`scripts/arena_layouts.gd`, new). A layout is a name, a size and a list of rectangles (`wall`, `bump`, `ledge`, with `spawn` on four ledges). `arena.gd` `_build_layout()` makes a `StaticBody2D` with a `CollisionShape2D` and a drawn slab per piece at load, under `$Platforms` (the node keeps its name, so the tape, the replay and the bot brain read it as before). Walls and bumps are solid on layer 1; ledges are one-way on layer 6 (`player.gd LEDGE_LAYER`). The tower: 640 x 1080, walls 48 px outside the screen, holes at x 256..384, passages at y 380..428 and 800..848, fifteen ledge rows, wall bumps at three heights on each side.
- **The scene** (`scenes/arena.tscn`). The eight platform bodies and their five shapes are gone. New: `Cam` (a `Camera2D`, limits 0..640 x 0..1080, smoothing 8/s), `Sky` (a `CanvasLayer` at -1 holding the background, so it does not scroll), `Markers` (a `CanvasLayer`). The pause overlay and the join button now go under `$HUD` in code. `player.tscn` is untouched.
- **The load check** (`tools/check_scripts.gd`): every `.tscn` under `scenes/` is loaded and instantiated after the scripts compile, so a broken scene fails the bump. `CLAUDE.md` rule 1 rewritten: scene edits allowed, run the check, never binary files.
- **The seams** (`player.gd`, the four projectile scripts, `bot_brain.gd`, `chaos_bots.py`). The rule keeps its shape with the tower's numbers (`arena_w` / `arena_h`, statics on `player.gd` that the arena sets): x past -12 comes back at 650 (only possible inside a passage), y past 1096 comes back at -10 (the floor hole), y under -16 while rising comes back at 1090 (the ceiling hole). The puppet ring's y seam is 1106. New `MAX_FALL_SPEED` 600: a three-screen drop would reach 1860 px/s, 93 px a packet, past the 80 px teleport threshold.
- **The camera** (`arena.gd` `_process`, `_camera_target`): my fighter plus `look_offset_y`; a spectator sees the middle of the living fighters; a replay follows the closing kill's victim (`replay_player.focus_point()`); a dead fighter's view stays where it fell. The VHS overlay moved onto its own `CanvasLayer` (screen space); the red ring goes through the camera's transform.
- **Duck** (`player.gd` `_set_duck`): down on the floor with no sideways push. No walking while ducked. The hitbox is 14 x 12 at (0, 2), the bottom half. The bit goes out in `sync_pos` as `FLAG_DUCK` 0x40, so every screen shrinks the puppet's shape too (hits are reported by whoever sees them); the tape keeps it as bit 256 (64 and 128 are the tape's bubble and dead bits); the replay's ghosts duck. Down + jump on a ledge drops through it (`DROP_THROUGH_S` 0.3, the ledge layer ignored meanwhile).
- **Look** (`player.gd` `_update_look`): duck for 1.5 s and the view eases 200 px down; aim within 30 degrees of straight up for 1.5 s (the mouse, or the phone stick straight up inside its aim ring) and it eases 200 px up; back in about 0.3 s when the hold ends.
- **The markers** (`arena.gd` `_draw_markers`): a fighter above or below the view gets a 22 px bubble with its class icon and a small arrow at the top or bottom edge, at its real x. Never for yourself, never for the dead.
- **The arena shift is off** (`arena.gd` `ARENA_SHIFT_ENABLED = false`): the host's 15 s powerup timer is not started. The relay, `arena_flips`, the tape's `rot` and `flips`, the replay's turn and the bots' `shift_wraps` all stay and read zero. Backlog 9 and 24 close as "does not apply any more". Spawns: round starts on the four spawn ledges (`ArenaLayouts.spawn_spots`), a mid-round respawn drops in through the ceiling hole (x 300..340, y -10) in the bubble and lands on the ledge under it.
- **The pacing table** ([[arena-tower]] §7): run 220 to 170, accel and friction down with it, the dash 480 x 0.16 (the same 77 px) with a 1.2 s cooldown (was 0.65), attacks 0.45 / 0.5 / 0.5 / 0.5 and 0.6 / 0.35 s (ranger, knight, mage, druid thorn and bear, rogue), specials 1.5 / 1.2 / 2.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 s, the mage and rogue recharges 1.8 and 1.2 s, shots 560 / 460 / 600 / 480 px/s with longer kunai and firebolt lives. Jump and gravity unchanged: the rows are built on the 80 px jump. The bot brain reads `SPEED` from the fighter now instead of a copy.
