# v0.2.0-p4.15 - Climb Out

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Phase:** after the second gathering night. The user: "It went well overall. I think the worst thing was the movement. With the wall climbs, ledge grabs, dashes, and movement on a virtual joystick, it was hard to get the character to do exactly what you wanted. In the lower left corner of the haunted house, theres a pit. You have to wall jump and dash to get out. Its really hard to do that repeatedly with intention." And: "The iphones had a bug where the screen would turn white and they would have to refresh the page to play again. It happened ever 2-3 matches." · **Harness:** the proofs below

> Wall jumps are much easier now. Jump next to a wall and you jump off it, whichever way the stick points. A slide down a wall starts slow, so there is time to jump. On a phone, pump the thumb or swing it over the top to jump again. The iPhone white screen came from two leaks that grew every match. Both are fixed, and the page comes back by itself if a phone ever takes its drawing away.

## Why the moves were hard (what we found)
- Our wall jump needed the stick held TOWARD the wall at the press. On the phone stick that is a thin slice: the thumb between 45 degrees (the jump line) and about 68 degrees (where left or right stops counting), on the wall's side. TowerFall does not ask for that: any jump with a wall within 2 of its pixels (9 of ours) jumps off it.
- With the thumb already up, the stick only jumped again when the thumb dipped under the 45 degree line. Pumping it, or swinging it over the top to the other side (what a kid does to change walls), did nothing.
- A slide down a wall went at full speed at once. TowerFall starts it slow and speeds it up over about 2 s.
- A jump during a dash did nothing next to a wall. In TowerFall it jumps off the wall (early in the dash, a big jump).
- A ledge grab caught a fighter only up to 18 px under the hang. TowerFall: up to 7 of its pixels (31 of ours).

## Changes
- **The wall moves** (`sim/sim.gd`, `tf_moves`; for everyone, the bots too; TowerFall's own rules):
  - a jump with a wall within 9 px jumps off it, the stick any way (not with a floor within 22 px below: that jump is the ground's);
  - a slide down a wall starts at 2.25 px a tick and speeds up by 0.045 a tick to 7.2;
  - a jump during a dash next to a wall jumps off it: in the dash's first 8 ticks the big one (up and away at the dash's speed, and the dash goes on), later a plain one;
  - a ledge grab catches a fighter up to 37 px under the hang (was 18);
  - a jump pressed during a dash waits 5 ticks, as on foot (it used to wait to the dash's end).
- **The phone stick** (`client/touch_sticks.gd`): with the thumb up, two more ways to jump again: pump it (down a fifth of the stick's reach, then up a little) or swing it over the top to the other side. `?stick=old` gives the p4.14 stick, to compare.
- **The bots' route map** (`sim/routes.json`) rebuilt with the new moves.
- **The iPhone white screen** (a helper found it):
  - the KILL! pop in the match's replay drew its letters at a new size every frame. A font keeps a 2 MB sheet of letters for every size it has drawn (on the GPU too): up to 95 sheets in one replay at an iPhone's size. Now it draws one size and shrinks it (`client/deathcam.gd`). It looks the same (BEFORE/AFTER pictures, 0% to 95% of the pop);
  - the web export's GL layer gave every GL object a new number and never reused one, so its tables grew by megabytes a minute (the game makes about 200 GL objects a frame). `tools/v2_build.sh` now swaps in a version that reuses free numbers. The build stops if Godot's text ever changes;
  - a safety net (`bench/web/shell.html`): if the phone takes the game's drawing away (a lost WebGL context) or the engine stops, the page reloads itself with your name and takes your seat again (at most once a minute).
- **The phone report** now carries the stick (which one, its jumps, dodges from the stick's rim and from the DODGE button) and the page's memory (`mem_mb`, `tex_mb`). `tools/v2_night_report.py` prints them and flags a phone whose memory grew 150 MB.
- **New probe:** `bench/tools/pit_probe.gd` (THE HAUNTED HOUSE's pit, old and new moves, old and new stick).
