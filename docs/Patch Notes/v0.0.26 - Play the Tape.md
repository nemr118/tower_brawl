# v0.0.26 - Play the Tape

**Date:** 2026-09-04 · **Phase:** v0.1.0 groundwork: the VHS replay plays the frozen tape back · **Harness:** 21/21 with `--restart-each`, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.26.json](../harness_report_v0.0.26.json)

> When a round ends on a kill, every screen now plays its own frozen tape back as a VHS replay: a quick rewind, the last 2.5 seconds at full speed, the kill in slow motion with a flash, the fall, then a fade to black. The server makes room for it with a 6.5 s round gap. Spectators see it too. The harness checks one `📼 [Replay]` line per playback.

## When it plays
- **The server decides.** `serve_game.py` `_check_round_end` adds `"replay": true` to `round_end` when the round had a winner and a death landed in the last `REPLAY_KILL_WINDOW = 1.5` s (from `last_death`). For those rounds `_next_round_later` waits `REPLAY_ROUND_DELAY = 6.5` s instead of `NEXT_ROUND_DELAY = 2.6` s. A draw or a forfeit win has nothing to show and keeps 2.6 s. A won match keeps its banner and waits `MATCH_END_DELAY = 7.0` s (was 6.0) so the last round's replay ends before `return_to_lobby`.
- **The client follows the tape.** `global.gd` passes the flag on: `net_round_end(winner_id, scores, round_num, match_over, replay)`. `arena.gd` `_on_round_end_sync` remembers it (`_replay_due`) and the time (`_round_end_msec`). The tape freezes about 1.0 s later (the 60-frame tail, v0.0.25); at that moment `_record_tape_frame` calls `_start_replay`. If the tape is not frozen 1.5 s after `round_end` (`REPLAY_WAIT_S`, a hidden tab whose ticks stalled) the replay is skipped with `not-frozen`; a tape with nothing on it skips with `empty-tape`; no closing stamp skips with `no-closing-stamp`. `_start_round` calls `_stop_replay_now`, so `new_round` cuts anything still running and the line is printed before `tape.clear`.
- **Why grow the gap:** the tape is 6 s and the old gap was 2.6 s. A corner picture in the 1.5 s start bubble only fits a sped-up clip, and a replay on a key makes people die while watching. The cost is 3.9 s more per won round.

## What is played and how
- **New file `scripts/replay_player.gd`** (`ReplayPlayer`, a `Node2D` that `arena.gd` adds in `_ready`, `z_index = 20`). It plays 211 frames around the closing kill `K`: `◄◄ REW` from K+60 back to K-150 at 10x (about 0.4 s), `► PLAY` K-150 to K-30 at 1x (2.0 s), `► SLOW` K-30 to K+30 at 0.5x with a white flash on frame K (2.0 s), `► PLAY` K+30 to K+60, the fall (0.5 s), `■ STOP` on the last frame with a 0.2 s fade to black. About 5.05 s. Stepping runs on the render clock in `_process`; this is local tape, not network state (rule 3 untouched). A screen that runs slow still shows the right frames at the right time; the `drawn` count says how many it skipped.
- **The live world pauses.** Every live fighter and projectile gets `process_mode = PROCESS_MODE_DISABLED` and `visible = false` (the bot brain under a fighter pauses with it). Nothing is put back by hand: `_start_round` respawns everyone and clears the projectiles. When the replay ends on its own (a won match) the old `process_mode` and visibility come back.
- **Ghost fighters** are real `player.tscn` instances with a new `is_ghost` flag in `player.gd`: `_ready` turns physics off, disables the collision shape and both collision masks, keeps the fighter out of the `players` group (arrows and bot brains never see it) and returns early. `_draw` is shared with the live fighter; the only differences are the floor test (`ghost_on_floor` from `FLAG_FLOOR` instead of `is_on_floor()`) and the ammo row, which a ghost skips because the tape holds no ammo counts. Per frame the ghost gets `global_position`, `aim_direction`, `is_facing_right`, `is_dashing`, `is_shielding`, `is_bear_form`, `is_egg`, `is_bubble` and `ghost_on_floor` from the frame flags, `velocity` from the position delta so the legs run, `dash_dir` from the movement, `anim_time` from the frame's `msec`. `FLAG_DEAD` hides the ghost, so a replayed death looks like the real one. Your own ghost keeps the pale yellow name tag.
- **Ghost projectiles** are the four projectile scenes, pooled per list index and weapon id (cap 32), taken out of the `projectiles` group, physics off, `monitoring` and `monitorable` off, `is_stuck` and `rotation` from the frame.
- **The spin is real:** `platforms_node.rotation` follows the frame's `rot`; at the end it goes back to `Global.arena_flips * PI`, the same value `_finish_spin_now` uses.
- **The VHS look** is drawn by an overlay child (`VhsOverlay`, `z_index = 30`, above the ghosts) with plain `draw_*` calls: a faint cool tint, scanlines every other row (16 % black), a pale tracking bar rolling down every 1.5 s, a ±2 px horizontal shake on about 5 % of frames (60 % while rewinding), a red pulsing ring around the victim during the slow part, a one-frame white flash on the kill frame, the fade to black at the end. Text: the label and a counter from the kill (`-2.5s` to `+1.0s`) bottom left, `REPLAY` with a blinking red dot bottom right (both in the void columns beside the seam; the top row belongs to the HUD panels), and the caption `Godot2 killed Andrew · Firebolt` with `ROUND 3` above the ground. The banner (`banner_label`) hides while the replay plays and comes back after, so the spectator's `SPECTATING...` text and the `WINS THE MATCH` text are not drawn over the tape.
- **Spectators** play the same way from their own tape: `arena.gd` records on every screen. Their cards are dropped by the server as before (no seat).

## What the replay tells the world
- **The `📼 [Replay]` log line**, one per replay, printed by `arena.gd` when it ends, is cut or is skipped (`replay_player.gd` `status_line`): `📼 [Replay] round=1 killer=4 victim=3 weapon=Firebolt from=869 to=1079 frames=211 drawn=211 dur_ms=5050 late_ms=987 cut=0 skipped=-`. `late_ms` = from `round_end` to the first replay frame; `cut=1` = the next round arrived first; `skipped=not-frozen|empty-tape|no-closing-stamp|too-short|cut-before-start` with zeros elsewhere (`cut-before-start`: the next round came before a due replay could start, the case of a screen whose ticks crawl).
- **The card.** `history_ring.gd` carries a `replay` dictionary and `status_card` sends it inside `history_status` as `replay: {playing, played, round, frames, drawn, dur_ms, late_ms, cut, skipped}` (null until a replay starts, cleared by `clear`). Sent once when the replay starts (`playing: true`) and once when it ends or is skipped. The server keeps it in `seats[].tape.replay` in `status.json` and logs `[TAPE] P3's screen played the round 1 replay: 211 frames in 5.0 s, started 1.0 s after the round end` (or `... skipped the round 2 replay: not-frozen`).
- **The deck (`tbdash`).** The `Tape` column reads `▶ R3` while a screen plays, `■ R3 ✓ ▶5.1s` after it played, `■ R3 ✓ ✗` when it skipped. The inspect line of a round-end column adds `· replay played on 2 screens (P3 P4), 5.1 s, started 1.0 s after the kill` (plus `, cut by the next round`, `· replay playing on P1`, `· replay skipped on P2 (not-frozen)`). The `[TAPE]` events stay on key `0`.

## The harness
- **New scenario `replay`** (21 in the suite, about 30 s): the `tape` scenario's set-up (two standing Godot clients, two bots with `shoot = False`, three scripted Firebolt kills end round 1). Checks: `replay.flag-missing` (`round_end.replay` not true), `replay.gap-short` / `replay.gap-long` (`new_round` outside 6.0 to 8.0 s after `round_end`), `replay.no-new-round`, then on both Godot logs `replay.missing`, `replay.double`, `replay.skipped`, `replay.wrong-kill`, `replay.short` (under 200 frames), `replay.frames-dropped` (under 90 % drawn), `replay.duration` (outside 4 500 to 6 000 ms), `replay.late` (over 1 500 ms), `replay.cut`, `replay.script-error`, `replay.no-card` (`status.json` `seats[].tape.replay.played` not true), plus the set-up findings (`clients-not-joined`, `no-round-end`, `wrong-winner`, `no-status`).
- **`replay_gate`** on every `fleet` client and the `lag` observer, next to `tape_gate`: every frozen tape with a closing kill (but the last, whose replay may still run) must have a `[Replay]` line (`<scen>.replay-missing`), not skipped (`.replay-skipped`), started within 1 500 ms (`.replay-late`), with 90 %+ of its frames drawn (`.replay-frames-dropped`) and, when not cut, 4 500 to 6 000 ms long (`.replay-duration`).
- **Schema and model:** `round_end` now requires `replay` (bool). The oracle remembers it (`Model.round_replay`) and the `timeout.next-round` watchdog allows `REPLAY_ROUND_DELAY + 2.0` s after such a round. The mirrored constants: `REPLAY_ROUND_DELAY = 6.5`, `MATCH_END_DELAY = 7.0`. The waits that assumed a 2.6 s gap (`ensure_lobby`, `smoke`, `spectator`, `simultaneous_leave`) use the long gap; `rounds` gets 60 s for its three rounds (was 45).
- **`GodotClient.replays()`** parses the `📼 [Replay]` lines (`REPLAY_LINE`).

## Checked by eye
A windowed Godot spectator was screenshotted during the `replay` scenario: the rewind, `► PLAY -2.3s`, the victim's bubble respawns replayed in place (hidden while dead), `► SLOW` with the red ring, the flash, the caption and the scanlines all drew; no script error or warning. The first pass put the labels on the top row, over the HUD panels; they moved to the bottom corners. A first spectator whose window was hidden ran at 8.5 fps (the compositor gives a hidden window no frames) and never froze its tape in time: that is the `not-frozen` path.

## Not in this build (v0.1.0 ideas)
- A match-end montage: the closing kill of every round of the match, one after the other, instead of the single last-round replay (the tape only keeps the current round, so the kill frames would have to be copied aside at each freeze).
- A "skip the replay" key, a longer tail, sound, a replay on demand for spectators.
- Ammo counts, the shooter's colour on arrows and the power-up sprite on the tape (the tape holds a spot for the power-up; the replay draws a ring there).
