# v0.0.19 - Who is who

**Date:** 2026-09-03 · **Phase:** Phase 3b step 5, playtest fixes · **Harness:** 18/18 (--restart-each)
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.19.json](../harness_report_v0.0.19.json)

> The second fix build after the family playtest: every fighter wears its name, no two players share a name, you can change your name in the lobby, and a round now starts with everyone standing on a platform.

## Changes

**Name tags** (`player.gd`)
- The problem: in the playtest nobody knew which fighter was theirs.
- Now every fighter has its name floating above its head. Your own tag is pale yellow. Everyone else's tag is white. Both have a black edge so they show on any background.
- The little squash animation scales the whole fighter, so the tag scales back the other way. The text never stretches.

**No two players with the same name** (`serve_game.py`, `character_select.gd`)
- The problem: two players typed the same name, and the top bar and the kill messages could not tell them apart.
- Now the server owns the names. If your name is taken, the server adds a number: `Tav`, then `Tav-2`, then `Tav-3`. Big and small letters count as the same name. Your letters are kept as you typed them. A 12-letter name is trimmed so the number still fits.
- This also works when you typed your name before you had a seat.
- The lobby card now shows the name the server gave you, so you see `Tav-2` too. Your saved name in the browser stays `Tav`.

**Change your name in the lobby** (`character_select.gd`)
- A small `CHANGE NAME` button sits under the join button. It opens the same name sheet as the first visit, with your current name filled in, a `SAVE NAME` button and a `CANCEL` button. While the sheet is open the class-cycling keys are paused.

**Round start on the ground** (`arena.gd`, `player.gd`)
- The problem: the v0.0.18 sky bubble is right for coming back after a death, but at the start of a round everyone floated down from the same strip of sky.
- Now, at round start, the game looks at the platforms as they are right now, flips and all. It finds the top edge of every platform that is on the screen and picks spots that are far apart: the leftmost first, then always the spot farthest from the ones already picked. Every screen has the same platforms, so every screen picks the same spots. No new network packet.
- Players get the spots in slot order and face the middle of the arena.
- Each fighter stands still inside a bubble for 1.5 seconds. Gravity is off. Your first key press pops the bubble, and that key press still counts. The bubble is a shield, so an early arrow flies back.
- Upright with four players: the left ledge, the right ledge, the top of the tower, the left ground. Upside down the two big ground blocks sit at the top edge and are skipped.
- A `🧭 [Spawn]` line in the console shows the picked spots at every round start.

**Sky bubble a bit higher** (`arena.gd`)
- After a death the fighter now floats in from `y = 40` instead of `y = 70`, above the top plank instead of level with it. Everything else about the sky bubble is unchanged.

**Test harness** (`tools/chaos_bots.py`)
- New scenario `dup_names`: three clients ask for the same name and must get `Tav`, `tav-2` and `Tav-3`; a 12-letter clash must come back trimmed. The suite now has 18 scenarios.

**Still in place**
- The `🪤 [JumpTrap]` console line stays. The jump bug did not show up in the v0.0.18 playtest.
