# v0.0.13 - Snapshot Interpolation

**Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Phase:** Phase 3b, step 2 of 5 · **Harness:** three 90 s fleets PASS (see Metrics)
**Report:** comparison against the [[v0.0.12 - Puppet Jitter Metric & Send Jitter]] baseline below

> Remote fighters are now drawn 100 ms behind the sender, between two real samples, with the seams unwrapped: puppet jitter drops from ~290 px/s to ~20 px/s and screen-wide glides are gone.

## Changes
### Protocol (client and harness)
- **The tick is a slot clock.** `player.gd` advances `net_tick` every 50 ms slot whether or not the slot is sent, so a receiver can place every sample in time and read a tick gap as "unchanged for that long" (idle suppression sends nothing when nothing changed). `tools/chaos_bots.py` bots do the same; the `oracle.movement-out-of-order` check is unchanged (ticks stay monotonic). The u16 wraps at 65536; the receiver's stale / restart rules from v0.0.8 still apply, and an exact duplicate tick is dropped.

### Client (`scripts/player.gd`, `scripts/global.gd`)
- **Snapshot ring per puppet** (16 samples: tick in unwrapped space, position, aim, flags, arrival time). The exponential lerp is gone.
- **Render clock.** Sender time is estimated as the newest sample's tick plus the time it has been sitting here (robust to idle suppression); the puppet renders `RENDER_DELAY_TICKS = 2` (100 ms) behind that. The clock free-runs at 20 ticks/s and is pulled to the estimate at 5 % per frame; a difference over 5 ticks resyncs outright.
- **Lookup.** The two samples bracketing the render clock are interpolated linearly. A tick gap over 2 is idle suppression by construction, so the state holds at the older sample and steps during the last tick. Past the newest sample the puppet holds (Step 3 adds extrapolation).
- **Seams.** The delta between two samples is unwrapped across the horizontal seam (640 px) and the bottom seam (386 px) before interpolating, and the result is wrapped back into the arena, so a crossing is a few-pixel step instead of a glide across the screen.
- **Flags at render time.** Facing, dash, shield, bear and egg come from the sample being rendered, so invulnerability windows and FX sit on the drawn position (observer-side hit checks read these flags). Aim is slerped between the two samples.
- **Telemetry:** `snaps` now means a teleport (over 80 px in one frame without crossing a seam); seam crossings are counted in a new `wraps` field. Respawn, rejoin and `reset_net_sequence` clear the ring.

### Bots
- **Turtle** no longer raises its shield while airborne over an empty column (the knight shield has no air control); closes PASSDOWN backlog 8, the `fleet.bot-fall-loop` flake.

The few remaining snaps are respawn teleports and clock resyncs; wraps are the bots' seam routes (they drop through the seam 20–40 times each per 150 s).
