# v0.0.15 - Harness Quality Gate & Spectator Count

**Date:** 2026-09-03 · **Phase:** Phase 3b, step 4 of 5 · **Harness:** 17/17
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.15.json](../harness_report_v0.0.15.json)

> The puppet-jitter numbers from steps 1–3 become pass/fail: every brain in a fleet run and a new Godot observer in the `lag` scenario must keep their puppets under the calibrated thresholds. No client code changed in this build.

## Changes
### Harness (`tools/chaos_bots.py`)
- **`puppet_gate`.** For one Godot client's aggregated `puppets=` telemetry: `*.puppet-jitter` when the mean exceeds `PUPPET_JITTER_STD = 45` px/s (`--jitter-ms` ≤ 60) or `PUPPET_JITTER_HEAVY = 100` px/s above that; `*.puppet-snaps` when teleport snaps exceed the deaths that client saw plus `PUPPET_SNAP_SLACK = 5` (respawns are teleports by design; seam wraps are counted separately and never gated); `*.puppet-telemetry-missing` when the field is absent. Calibration: v0.0.13/v0.0.14 fleets measured 18–31 px/s mean at 0–120 ± 40 ms and 73–81 at 120 ± 150 ms, against 234–316 on the old lerp, so the gates sit at roughly 1.5× the measured values and 3–6× below the lerp.
- **`fleet`** applies the gate to every client started with a persona (brains move like players; brainless clients stand still and measure nothing).
- **`lag`** now starts a headless Godot client with the chaser brain as the fourth player, so the scenario measures how a real client renders a 150 ± 50 ms, 10 %-loss peer; its puppets go through the standard gate. Runs 30 s (was 25) so the observer logs six NetStats intervals. Calibration run: mean 11.6 px/s, p95 median 52, 13 snaps on 13+ deaths.

### Server (`serve_game.py`)
- The `[STATS]` line reads `players=N spectators=M sockets=N+M`. The old `sockets=` was the spectator count under a misleading name.

## Metrics

|---|---|---|---|---|
| `lag`, Godot observer vs a 150 ± 50 ms + 10 % loss peer, 30 s | 11.6 | 52 | 13 / ≥ 13 | standard, PASS |
| fleet 4 bots, 120 ± 40 ms, 90 s | 26–31 | 97–115 | 0–4 / 38 | standard (45), PASS |
| fleet 4 bots, 120 ± 150 ms, 90 s | 72–89 | 355–460 | 3–4 / 38 | heavy (100), PASS; worst client 88.5, ~12 % headroom |

Observation: in the brainless default `fleet` (two idle Godot clients watching protocol bots) `extrap` reads ~55 %: the protocol bots' ticker is a sleep loop, so under load their slot clock falls behind wall time and the client's sender-time estimate runs ahead of their samples. The puppets are idle so nothing moves wrongly, and no gate applies without a brain; if it matters later, tick the bots from the wall clock (`tools/chaos_bots.py` `_ticker`).
