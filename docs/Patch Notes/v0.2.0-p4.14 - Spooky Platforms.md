# v0.2.0-p4.14 - Spooky Platforms

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Phase:** the second gathering night. The user: "Could we use comfyui to generate the haunted house and boiler room platforms? Add some detail to them." ComfyUI was tried first: plain textures came out flatter than the hand-made tiles, and a detail pass (img2img) changed the shapes players rely on (the furnace's round door and HOT sign, the red and green striped pipes). The user picked detail drawn in code instead, from BEFORE/AFTER pictures. · **Harness:** the proofs below

> THE HAUNTED HOUSE's platforms now have cobwebs in the corners, candles with flickering flames, little skulls, moss and hanging roots; THE BOILER ROOM's have rivet rows, rust drips, oil stains and hazard stripes. The shapes are exactly the same, so nothing in play changes.

## Changes
- **THE HAUNTED HOUSE** (`client/dress_view.gd`, switch `hh_dress`): a cobweb in every top inner corner; a candle on about one top in five with a flickering flame and its glow (on the glow layer); a little skull on about one top in six; purple-grey moss tufts; dark roots hanging under ceilings; a few faint cracks.
- **THE BOILER ROOM** (switch `boiler_dress`): a row of rivets along every steel top, rust drips running down from seams, oil stains under overhangs, short yellow-and-black hazard bands. The dressing is lifted over the boiler's own platform picture on that arena (`world.gd`).
- **The host page (Ignition):** the phone link now has a QR code under it (PLAY ON PHONES), from Ignition's own barcode component, so phones can scan it from the dashboard (the user: "I dont see the qr codes in the ignition app"). The links page with codes never opened as a window on the ROG.
- **Kept for another day:** `tools/comfy/run_textures.py` and `cut_textures.py` (seamless ComfyUI textures).
