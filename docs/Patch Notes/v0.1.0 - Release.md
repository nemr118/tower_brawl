# v0.1.0 - Release

**Date:** 2026-09-07 · **Phase:** the first minor release: v0.0.41 with a new number, signed off by the afternoon's free play on the laptop server · **Harness:** 24/24 with `--restart-each`, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.1.0.json](../harness_report_v0.1.0.json) · runbook: [[release-v0.1.0]] · sign-off: [[Playtest — Master Validation Suite]] (archived) · the laptop: [[laptop-server]]

> v0.1.0 is v0.0.41 with a new version number and nothing else: `GAME_VERSION` in `scripts/global.gd`, its mirror in `serve_game.py`, and the exported `build/web` files. It closes the v0.0.x line that began on 2026-09-02: the custom WebSocket relay, four classes plus the druid, the tape and the VHS replay, rejoin with a held seat, the bot personas, the 24-scenario harness, the optimisation cuts (tape card on change, relay bundles), the anchored arena floor, the device cards, and the laptop that runs it all for a family night.

## What is in it (since v0.0.36, the last page before the release run)
- **v0.0.37** every device mails its NetStats numbers to the server every 5 s; `tools/stats_table.py`; the sheet asks for clock times only ([[v0.0.37 - Client Stats Reporting]]).
- **v0.0.38** the platforms stay solid during the arena shift again, the v0.0.32 free fall reverted by decision ([[v0.0.38 - Solid Shift Restored]]).
- **v0.0.39** the ground slabs are anchored; the ledges, tower and apex turn above them ([[v0.0.39 - Anchored Ground]]).
- **v0.0.40** the laptop server kit: bots, deck controls, deploy and pull scripts, join / class / kill / leave records, match summaries ([[v0.0.40 - LAN Server Kit]]).
- **v0.0.41** the replay marks are drawn shapes; the deck's bot menu and info key ([[v0.0.41 - Drawn Replay Icons]]).

## Not in it, by decision
- **Optimisation Step C cut 1** (the phone main thread) moved to the v0.1.1 decision, against the Android export + PCK loader. The device cards give the numbers for either.
- **Backlog 9** (an outer ledge carries a fighter above the top edge mid-shift) and **24** (turning ledges sweep the anchored slab corners) stay open for the arena configurations.

## The sign-off
The planned family evening was cancelled. The afternoon's free play on the laptop server stands instead, read from its `client_stats.jsonl`: six matches between 13:33 and 14:01, three to five crowns; matches 5 and 6 were the user on the PC (Chrome) against a chaser bot, 7 and 9 rounds, lobby to crown to lobby. PC numbers over match 6: 240 fps drawn, 60 physics ticks, slowest frame 19.8 ms, 0 hitches, scripts 1.9 ms mean, rtt 4 ms, 16 packets in a second, puppet jitter 22 px/s, focus never lost. The arena shift and the bundles were judged in play (sections 4 and 5 of the sheet); sections 1 to 3 were closed without a play because their questions had moved on (numbers are automatic now; backlog 1, 6 and 13 are covered by the harness or are low, seen-once lines). The one thing that play found wrong, the replay marks, was fixed in v0.0.41. The sheet is archived with every verdict ticked.
