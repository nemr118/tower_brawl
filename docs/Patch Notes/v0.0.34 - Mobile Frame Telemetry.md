# v0.0.34 - Mobile Frame Telemetry

**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Phase:** optimisation Step C, build 1: the phone reports its own script milliseconds; one master playtest sheet · **Harness:** 23/23 with `--restart-each`, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.34.json](../harness_report_v0.0.34.json)

> The `📈 [NetStats]` line now ends its `fps` part with `proc=X.Xms phys_cpu=Y.Yms`, so a phone says how many milliseconds its scripts take. One file changed: `scripts/global.gd`. The game is unchanged. The three waiting playtest sheets were folded into one, [[Playtest — Master Validation Suite]], and the USB phone console is written up in [[Commands]].

## Why
Step B ([[optimisation_step_b_profiles]]) measured the PC only: about 1 ms of a 16.7 ms frame. The phone is the platform that matters and had no number. The cheapest next measurement was two Godot monitors on the line the sheets already read.

## Changes
- **`scripts/global.gd` `_ft_sample`** reads `Performance.get_monitor(Performance.TIME_PROCESS)` and `TIME_PHYSICS_PROCESS` every drawn frame and sums them. `_ft_summary` returns the mean in ms as `proc_ms` and `phys_cpu_ms`; `_ft_reset` clears the sums. The NetStats line prints them as `proc=` and `phys_cpu=` after `hitches=`; the `net_stats_updated` signal's `fps` dict carries both keys.
- **What the numbers mean.** Godot refreshes `TIME_PROCESS` and `TIME_PHYSICS_PROCESS` once a second with the slowest `_process` frame and the slowest `_physics_process` tick of that second (`Main::iteration` keeps `process_max`, not a mean). So `proc=` is "the worst script frame of each second, averaged over five seconds". The first line after the arena loads carries the load spike (`proc=28.2ms` next to `worst=132ms hitches=2` on the PC); the lines after it are the reading.
- **`docs/Commands.md`:** new block "Phone console over USB" (`adb devices`, Chromium `chrome://inspect/#devices`, Discover USB devices, inspect, filter `NetStats`), the NetStats field list updated. Trimmed to stay under 5 000 bytes (one fault-knob line and the `status.json` one-liner dropped).
- **One master sheet.** `Playtest Backlog 1 — Ranger Reload Quiver`, `Playtest Backlog 13 — PC Reload Input Probe` and `Playtest v0.0.32 — Free-Fall Arena Shift` were deleted and their scenarios, edge cases, tables and verdicts moved into [[Playtest — Master Validation Suite]] as sections 2, 3 and 4. Section 1 is new: the S25 Ultra over USB step by step, device fields, and frame tables (`draw= phys= worst= hitches= proc= phys_cpu=`) for the S25 Ultra, the Pixel and the iPad. Older patch pages still link the three old names; those links now point nowhere, the content is in the master sheet.
- **`docs/PASSDOWN.md`** points every human-validation item at the master sheet.

## Harness
- **Full suite:** 23/23 with `--restart-each`, no Minor. The suite does not read the `fps` part of NetStats, so the new fields could not change a verdict; the client logs show the line printing (`proc=28.2ms phys_cpu=0.6ms` on the first line after the arena load, headless PC).
