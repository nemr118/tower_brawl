# v0.0.12 - Puppet Jitter Metric & Send Jitter

**Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Phase:** Phase 3b, step 1 of 5 · **Harness:** fleet baselines, see Metrics
**Report:** baseline logs in `.harness_logs/` at the time of the run; the numbers are in this page and in PASSDOWN

> Measurement only: every remote fighter now reports how jerky it looks, the NetStats line carries the number, and the headless client can jitter its sends. Nothing about puppet motion changed yet; these are the numbers Step 2 has to beat.

## Changes
### Client
- **Puppet-jitter telemetry** (`scripts/player.gd` `_puppet_telemetry`, `scripts/global.gd` `puppet_sample`). Per remote fighter and rendered physics frame: the absolute change in rendered speed since the previous frame (px/s), a *snap* when the puppet moved more than 200 px in one frame, and a *stall* when no sample has arrived for 150 ms although the sender was moving. Aggregated per 5 s NetStats interval: `puppets=<count> jitter=<mean>/<p95>px/s snaps=<n> stall=<pct>% pn=<puppet-frames>`, also in the `net_stats_updated` signal under `puppets`. Snaps currently include legitimate seam crossings; Step 2 separates those from teleports.
- **`--jitter-ms=N`** on the headless send path next to `--latency-ms`: every packet gets latency ± jitter, with a monotonic due time so a later packet never overtakes an earlier one (the same model as the harness bots; TCP could not reorder either).

### Harness
- `--jitter-ms` reaches the Godot clients; the fleet scenario aggregates the `puppets=` field over the run (frame-weighted mean, median and max of the interval p95s, snap total, stall %) into a note per client. No assertion yet: thresholds come in Step 4 from the before/after numbers.

## Metrics (the Step 2 yardstick)
Mixed fleet chaser + sniper + turtle + rusher, 4 headless clients, 90 s, difficulty 0.7, **current exponential lerp (24/s)**. Each row is one client's view of its three puppets.

| Godot send latency ± jitter | jitter mean (px/s) | p95 median (px/s) | p95 max | snaps / 90 s | stall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 ms | 275 · 303 · 316 · 289 | 617 · 684 · 685 · 670 | 1245–2039 | 22–29 | ≤ 0.1 % |
| 120 ms | 268 · 316 · 305 · 279 | 623 · 697 · 700 · 674 | 1163–1902 | 16–23 | ≤ 0.1 % |
| 120 ± 40 ms | 250 · 277 · 289 · 234 | 620 · 685 · 722 · 645 | 1190–2040 | 9–18 | 0 % |

Reading: the jitter is the lerp's own shape (a speed kink on every 50 ms sample, then decay), not the network; latency and jitter at LAN scale do not move the number. The bots' own speed is 220 px/s, so a mean speed change of ~290 px/s per frame means the puppet's speed is essentially never steady. Snaps are almost all seam crossings (the bots wrap 20–40 times per 150 s each). Stalls do not occur on TCP with constant latency.

## Found, not fixed (outside the phase)
- **`fleet.bot-fall-loop` on the turtle** in the 0 ms run: 4 bottom wraps without landing (threshold 4). Same mechanism as the griefer fix in v0.0.11: the knight shield has no air control, and the turtle shields on inbound threats while falling. Fix candidate in `bot_brain.gd`: no air shield while over an empty column. The other two runs passed; the metric is unaffected.
