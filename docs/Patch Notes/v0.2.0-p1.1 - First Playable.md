# v0.2.0-p1.1 - First Playable

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Phase:** P1, the first playable: the benchmark grown into the new game (plan section 3, steps 1 to 8) · **Harness:** the P1 proofs on the PC (below); the relay harness does not apply to the new server
**Report:** the server's report lines, `bench_results/<date>/v2_server.jsonl` (no `harness_report` file for the new game yet)

> The new game plays: phones and the PC keyboard join a lobby, bots fill the empty seats, START, one-life rounds, points, a winner, back to the lobby. It is built from the benchmark in `bench/` (the plan: [[first-playable-plan]], the decision: [[Design Decisions v0.2]] D18). It is not on the laptop yet: the deploy waits for a go.

## Changes
- **1. Real buttons in the sim** (`bench/sim/sim.gd`, `proto.gd`). `apply_input(f, buttons, aim)`: left and right run; jump pressed is a jump (a 5-tick buffer, so a press just before landing still jumps), jump held is the higher jump (161 px; a tap 98 px); down held falls faster, and down + jump drops through a ledge (TowerFall's rule; the bench's "down alone drops" is gone); shoot held is aiming (you stand still on the ground, you face the aim), let go is the shot, snapped to an enemy within 162 px and 65° with nothing solid between (TowerFall's auto-lock, D5); an empty quiver gives `E_DRYFIRE`; dodge goes the way the stick points (8 ways, nothing = the way you face), on the ground a downward dodge is a faster slide, a jump in the first 12 ticks of a ground dodge cancels it, a second press ends it, a catch holds you still 10 ticks. The brain plays only a seat with no person (a bot, or a dropped person's seat). `respawn` off = one life; `power_arrows` off = plain arrows for all; `start_round(seats)`, `frozen`, `alive_count()`, `last_alive()`. The snapshot grew a match header (phase, round, ticks left, goal, winner) and two fighter bytes (points; flags2: bot, aiming, away). The benchmark still runs on the same sim (`sim_probe`: 0.18 ms a tick with 8 bots).
- **2. The server** (`bench/server/game_server.gd`, new; `bench_server.gd` stays for the benchmark). 8 seats. `hello {name, version, token}`: another version is turned away (`version_error`); a reload within 8 s gets the seat back by its token. `join`, `leave`, `start`, `bots`, `name` as JSON; `seats` to everyone on every change. START by anyone seated with 2 or more fighters → a 3 s countdown → the rounds. A round: the fighters stand 1.5 s (ROUND N), FIGHT, one life; the last one standing gets the point 1.5 s later (TowerFall's RoundEndCounter); nobody left = a draw at once; no end at 60 s = a draw (backlog 28's rule until the miasma). Points to win: 5, 4, 3 for 2, 3, 4 fighters, 3 for more (`--goal N` overrides: a test knob). The match end holds 7 s, then the lobby. Bots fill the empty seats (`--bots 7` by default; the lobby's - and + change it); a late joiner takes a free seat, or a bot's, and plays from the next round; a dropped person's fighter is played by the brain until they are back or 8 s pass. Inputs: every new client tick in a packet is applied in order, so a 1-tick press is never lost, and the held state is kept between packets. Two threads as in the benchmark (the network thread owns the sockets). Tagged lines on stdout (`[NET] [JOIN] [LEAVE] [LOBBY] [ROUND] [KILL] [MATCH]`), a report line every 5 s and `v2_server.jsonl`.
- **3. The client** (`bench/client/game_client.gd`, `lobby.gd`, `hud.gd`, `touch_sticks.gd`, `net.gd`; `world.gd` and `fx.gd` changed). The bench world at T2 (`?look=` stays a knob). The lobby: your name (saved), JOIN or LEAVE, the 8 seats with their colours, BOTS - +, START with the countdown, a status line, the controls' help. The HUD: a card per seat (colour, name, arrows, points towards the goal, a crown on the leader, a white frame on yours, grey when dead, "next round", "away"); ROUND N, FIGHT!, who wins the round, DRAW, who wins the match, DRAW IN N. Your own fighter from the newest snapshot, the others 50 ms behind; fighters matched by seat, so colours never shift. The twin sticks ported from v0.1.4 into window pixels: the left stick runs, up jumps (held up = the full jump), down holds down, down + rim drops, the rim elsewhere dodges that way; the right stick aims while held and fires when let go; a DODGE button at the right edge. Keyboard: A D run, W or space jump, S down, shift dodge, hold the mouse button to aim, let go to fire; an aim pointer while aiming. It reconnects every 2 s; a wrong version is said on the status line. `?bench` opens the old benchmark page.
- **4. Build and serve.** `tools/v2_build.sh`: the check (`bench/tools/check_scripts.gd`), the bump of `v0.2.0-p1.N` in `proto.gd`, the export preset "Web v2" to `build/web/v2/` with the phone shell (`bench/web/shell.html` links to `web/shell.html`), the pck named after the version, the patch page. The Python service serves `/v2/` with no change. `tools/deploy_laptop.sh` also sends `bench/` and `build/web/v2/` and installs `tools/towerbrawl-v2.service` (headless Godot, wss 8091, the cert pair, beside the old game): written, **not run**.
- **5. Tools.** `bench/tools/input_probe.gd` (23 sim rules offline), `bench/tools/play_probe.gd` (a headless person: the move proof checked against the same buttons on a local sim, a match watched), `tools/v2_synth.py` (fake players with a small brain: run at the nearest, shoot, fetch arrows, dodge, unstick), `tools/v2_shot.py` (Chromium pictures, `--phone`).
- **6. Docs.** [[Commands]] opens with the new game, PASSDOWN carries two games, `CLAUDE.md` has an orientation line, the sheet is [[Playtest v0.2.0-p1.1]].

## Found on the way (reported, not fixed)
- **Two left alive often stand off until the 60 s draw** (bots: 3 of 8 rounds; fakes: 5 of 8). Backlog 28's rule does its job; the miasma (D2, G4) is the real answer. Kids hunt each other, so the night tells.
- **The bots aim like the benchmark's**: a shot every 20 to 50 ticks with ±7° noise and no aim help. Fine for filling seats; a difficulty setting comes with G6.
- **The fallback font has no arrow glyph**: "←" drew a box in the lobby (now "(you)"). Symbols in the HUD need a font first.
- **The plan's jump number** (153 px) was the formula; the sim's 60 Hz step gives 161, and both ends agree.
- **[[Playtest v0.1.7]]** (the old game) is still open with no ticks, so two sheets are open now.
- **A phone must accept the certificate twice**: once for the page (`:8443`) and once for the game server (`:8091`, a websocket never asks). The lobby's "Connecting to the server…" line should say so after a few seconds (the next build).
- **Headless Chromium draws at 8 fps** (software rendering): the pictures are right, the ping shown is Chromium's, not the game's.
