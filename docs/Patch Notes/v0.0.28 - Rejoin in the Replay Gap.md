# v0.0.28 - Rejoin in the Replay Gap

**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Phase:** v0.1.0 list, bug build 1: backlog 13, the rejoin inside the replay gap · **Harness:** 22/22 with `--restart-each`, no Minor (new scenario `reload_in_gap`)
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.28.json](../harness_report_v0.0.28.json)

> A screen that reloads or drops inside the 6.5 s replay gap keeps its seat and its match. Two bugs from [[Playtest v0.0.26]] are fixed: the server ended the match while a dropped seat was still held, and a client whose auto-rejoin landed in the lobby stayed in the arena for good. The PC keyboard-after-reload part is not reproduced yet; this build carries a probe for it (`keys= focus=` at the end of every `NetStats` line) and a focus self-heal.

## What the playtest saw (2026-09-04)
- **Phone, hidden during the replay of the last round.** `server.log` 16:59:01 round 1 over, 16:59:06 P1 (the phone) disconnected, seat held 8 s, 16:59:08 `[MATCH] over, back to the lobby (only 1 player(s) left)`. Then the phone came back, asked for its seat, got `assign_id` with `match_state: LOBBY`, and stayed in the arena with a dead fighter on screen until a refresh.
- **PC, reload during the replay.** 16:52:54 round 2 over, 16:52:55 P2 disconnected and rejoined in the same second (`rejoined mid-match`), 16:53:00 round 3 started with [1, 2]. The server side was right. The player could aim with the mouse but not walk, and a second reload at 16:53:10 did not help.

## Changes
- **Server, `serve_game.py` `_next_round_later`:** a seat the server still holds (`pending_rejoin`) counts as present when the next round starts. `present = [p for p in global_playing_players if player_slots[p - 1] or p in pending_rejoin]`, and the `[ROUND]` line says `(seat held for [2], back within the grace or out)`. Before, a fighter that dropped inside the gap made the list one short and the match ended with "only 1 player(s) left" although the seat was held for `REJOIN_GRACE = 8` s. If the fighter never comes back, `_grace_expired` still removes it and `_check_round_end` ends the new round as a forfeit; the match then ends on the short 2.6 s gap as before.
- **Client, `scripts/global.gd`, the scene after `assign_id`:** the check `elif not _rejoin_pending: _ensure_scene_for_state(...)` never ran for an auto-rejoin, because `_rejoin_pending` was cleared six lines further down. It is now `elif type == "assign_id" or not _rejoin_pending:`. A client that comes back after the match ended (hidden phone, or the server restarted under it) goes to the lobby scene. A rejoin into a running match still reloads the arena as before.
- **Client, `scripts/global.gd`, the round number:** `Global.current_round` now follows `current_round` in the `assign_id` / `spectator_state` snapshot, `new_round` and `scene_transition` (it was stuck at 1). A rejoiner's arena starts at the real round: the banner, the tape (`tape.clear(round)`) and the spawn seed use it.
- **Keyboard probe and focus self-heal (the open PC part):** in the browser the key events are bound to the `<canvas>`, and a canvas only hears keys while it has the page focus. A normal join clicks JOIN and LOCK IN, a reload lands in the arena with no click at all. `Global._input` counts `InputEventKey` presses, `canvas_focused()` asks the page (`document.activeElement === canvas`), and every `📈 [NetStats]` line ends with ` | keys=<presses since the last line> focus=<1 or 0>` (`stats["keys"]`, `stats["focus"]` in `net_stats_updated`). `Global.focus_canvas()` asks for the focus when the arena starts (`arena.gd` `_ready`) and right after the auto-rejoin scene change. `web/shell.html` focuses the canvas once the engine is up, on every `pointerdown` anywhere on the page, and when the window gets the focus back. A headless client always says `focus=1`.
- **Test hook, `scripts/global.gd`:** `--reclaim=N` makes a headless client ask for seat N back the moment it connects (`request_join(N)`), like a browser after a page reload. `--autojoin` alone still asks for any seat.
- **Harness, `tools/chaos_bots.py`:** new scenario `reload_in_gap` (22 total): one protocol bot and one headless wanderer knight. Act 1: the wanderer drops 1.5 s into the gap and comes back 1 s later with `--reclaim`; it must get its seat back `(rejoined)`, `new_round` must come 6.5 s after `round_end`, and it must move in the new round (30+ px spread over 6 s of relayed `sync_pos`). Act 2: it drops and stays away past `new_round`; the match must not end, `new_round` must come on time, and it must rejoin the running round and move. Act 3: the server restarts under it; its reconnect lands in LOBBY and its next `NetStats` line must not say `Arena`. `GodotClient` grew `tag=` (own log file for a restarted client), `keep_class=` (a brain plays a given class), `start(reclaim=)`, `assigns()`, `scene()` and `bot_moves()`. A `dup.movement` / `dup.event` finding now names the sender and the packet.
- **Not fixed:** the PC keyboard after a reload. The headless reload in act 1 walks 400 to 650 px in the next round, so the fighter's state is fine after a rejoin; the difference on the PC is the browser keyboard. The next reload test on the PC shows `keys=` and `focus=` in the console: `focus=0` means the page focus is the cause (the self-heal should already cover it), `focus=1 keys=0` means the keys are lost somewhere else.
