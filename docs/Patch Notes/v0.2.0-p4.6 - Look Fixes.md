# v0.2.0-p4.6 - Look Fixes

**Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Phase:** P2's last part, a fix from the night. The user: "We had issues with the graphics settings not saving. They would only last a round, then revert back to low. Also, the effects from the different power arrows would disappear and be invisible but still be happening, just couldn't see them." · **Harness:** the proofs below
**Report:** headless Chromium on a phone (the page's console), `tools/v2_shot.py` pictures

> The look you pick now stays. The LOOK button has a new first choice, AUTO: it starts on what suits the phone and steps down only when play is really choppy. Pick LOW, NORMAL or HIGH and the game keeps it. Fire, poison and smoke from the power arrows now show on every look, LOW too.

## Why
- **The look.** The pick was kept on the phone, but the step-down was far too quick: more than 5% of the frames slower than 20 ms in 3 s stepped the look down. A few hitches (an explosion, an effect drawn for the first time) did that, twice in a round (HIGH to NORMAL to LOW), and it never stepped back up until the page reloaded.
- **The zones.** `world._render_zones` drew a zone only with particles or real lights, and LOW has neither, so fire, poison and smoke were not drawn at all on LOW. A step-down in a match also clears the zones on screen, so they vanished right then.

## Changes
- **1. LOOK: AUTO, LOW, NORMAL, HIGH** (`game_client.gd`: `PICKS`, `look_pick`, `_look_label`). A picked look is kept on the phone and never steps down. AUTO starts on the device's guess (an iPhone LOW, an Android phone NORMAL, the PC HIGH) and is the default; the lobby shows "LOOK: AUTO · NORMAL". A look picked before this build is kept as a pick.
- **2. AUTO steps down only when play is really choppy:** more than a quarter of the frames slower than 30 fps (33.4 ms) for 5 s (`SLOW_MS`, `SLOW_SHARE`, `STEP_S`; they were 20 ms, 5%, 3 s). The note at the bottom says so and how to keep a look.
- **3. The power arrows' zones on every look** (`world.gd`): the fire's glow always, and on LOW a bright band of fire on the floor; the poison cloud always; on LOW a soft grey smoke cloud with a dense middle that hides who is in it. Particles and real lights come on top where the look has them.
- **4. Blasts, fire bursts and poison bursts flash on LOW too** (`fx.gd`: `flash(..., always)`).
