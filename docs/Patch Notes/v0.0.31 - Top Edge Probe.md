# v0.0.31 - Top Edge Probe

**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Phase:** v0.1.0 list, bug build 4: backlog 9, the fighter above the top edge (cause found) · **Harness:** 23/23 with --restart-each, no Minor
**Report:** [harness_report_v0.0.31.json](../harness_report_v0.0.31.json) · fleet runs: [seed 42](../harness_report_v0.0.31_fleet_seed42.json), [seed 7](../harness_report_v0.0.31_fleet_seed7.json), [seed 13](../harness_report_v0.0.31_fleet_seed13.json)

> A probe line names the thing a fighter touches while it is above the top edge. Three heavy fleet runs answered backlog 9: the arena shift carries the fighter out of the top of the screen. The outer platforms swing 29 to 36 px above the top edge while the stage turns, and a fighter standing on one rides along. No game rule changed in this build; the fix is a design call (below).

## The cause (backlog 9)
- **The arena shift is a half turn of `Platforms` around the middle of the screen, `(320, 180)`.** The screen is 360 px tall, so anything farther than 180 px from the middle leaves the screen while it turns. `GroundLeft` and `GroundRight` sit at local `(∓140, 155)`: 209 px from the middle, so their centres pass 29 px above the top edge, and the far corner of a 160 x 50 slab (284 px out) reaches `y = -104`. `LedgeLeft` and `LedgeRight` sit at `(∓210, 50)`: 216 px out, centres 36 px above the edge, corners at `y = -91`. `LedgeHighLeft` / `LedgeHighRight` (155 px out) and the centre tower stay on the screen.
- **A fighter standing on a ledge or a ground slab when the shift starts rides it.** `move_and_slide()` pushes the fighter out of the moving slab every tick, the tilted face still counts as a floor (the normal stays inside 45° of up for the first third of the turn), so `is_on_floor()` is true and the feet-on-the-ground bit goes out in `sync_pos`. The spin is `TRANS_SINE` / `EASE_IN_OUT` over 2.5 s: at the peak the ledge moves 7 px a tick, and the push along the tilted normal adds to that (the old note saw 17 px a tick). The fighter is out of view for about a second, then the face tilts past 45° and it falls back in. It was never stuck for good; the old report caught the ride.
- **The second way in: the bottom wrap.** `player.gd` `_check_screen_wrap` puts a fighter that fell out of the bottom at `y = -10`. During a shift a ground slab is passing right there (`GroundLeft` at `(263, -21)` 122° into the turn, `(430, 3)` 170° in). The fighter lands on the slab in the sky and rides it down, or gets shoved by it.
- **Why the old numbers fit.** `x ≈ 181–190, y ≈ -36..-57` is `LedgeRight` early in a turn (its centre at `(187, 10)` 38° in, the fighter's middle 8 px above the face, the face on its way up to `y ≈ -42`). `x ≈ 393` is a ground slab near the end of a turn. "Three things touching it" was three slide contacts with the same body, and "no platform up there" was true at rest: the platforms are only up there while the stage turns.
- **A `.tscn` change cannot fix this.** Every platform more than 180 px from the pivot leaves the screen during the turn; the ground slabs are 209 px out and have to stay at the bottom. Moving the ledges inside 180 px would put them on the centre tower.
- **The fix is a design call, backlog 9 stays open with these options:** (A) `arena.gd` `_activate_rotation`: set `collision_layer = 0` on every `StaticBody2D` under `Platforms` while `is_arena_rotating`, restore it after (fighters fall through the turning stage and land on the new layout; the ride is gone); (B) keep the ride and treat a fighter carried above `y = -60` during a turn like a bottom fall: wrap it to the bottom (`_check_screen_wrap`, plus the puppet threshold in `_wrap_into_arena`); (C) keep it as it is: one second out of view per shift, puppets draw it correctly since v0.0.17. A is the smallest sure fix.

## Changes
- **`scripts/player.gd` `_probe_top_edge` (stays in the build).** Called from `_check_screen_wrap`, so it runs right after every local `move_and_slide()`. When a local fighter is above `TOP_EDGE_PROBE_Y = -20` and touches something (a slide contact, or `is_on_floor()`), it prints one line and then stays quiet for 1 s. Nothing runs below the line, so normal play pays nothing. The line: `🧗 [TopEdge] P1 pos=(175, -20) vel=(220, 0) dy=-4.5 floor=true floor_normal=(0.62, -0.78) contacts=3 flips=6 rot=938.5 dash=false shield=false egg=false bubble=false | Platforms/LedgeRight(StaticBody2D shape=0 at=(187, 10) rot=-141.5 normal=(0.62, -0.78) depth=1.0 point=(166, -14)) ; ...`. `dy` is the move since the last physics tick (`-412` = a bottom wrap), `flips` is `Global.arena_flips`, `rot` is the live angle of `Platforms` in degrees (a multiple of 180 = at rest). After the bar, one entry per slide contact: the collider's parent and name, its class, the shape index, where it is, its angle, the contact normal, the overlap depth and the contact point.
- **`tools/chaos_bots.py` `GodotClient.top_edges()` and `top_edge_note()`.** The fleet scenario parses the lines (`TOPEDGE_LINE`) and adds one note per client: the count, the `y` range, how many had the feet on the floor and moved up, the flip counts, the collider names ranked, and the first line in full. A note, not a failure: the line is the diagnosis.
- **Three heavy fleet runs** (`--godot 4 --ai chaser,sniper,turtle,rusher --latency-ms 120 --jitter-ms 150`), seeds 42 (90 s), 7 and 13 (120 s each), 330 s in all, 4 lines, every one with the stage mid-turn:

- **Seen on the way, not touched (out of scope):** the seed 13 run failed on `fleet.bot-fall-loop` (`Godot2 (sniper): 4 bottom wraps without landing`, a brain falling through the seam columns four times in a row at 120 ± 150 ms), plus two `precondition.*` findings that came from chaining the runs on one server without `--restart-server`. The seed 42 and seed 7 runs passed.
