# v0.2.0-p4.3 - See Yourself

**Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Phase:** P4, see yourself (the user's note, playing p4.1 on a phone at a family gathering: "The characters need more lighting or more highlights. It's hard to tell which character you're controlling. Need a way to both make characters more visible and highlight your character"; the user picked the marker: a ring under your feet) · **Harness:** page pictures at phone size, before and after
**Report:** `tools/v2_shot.py --phone --extra "look=t1"` (and t2, t3) against a server with 7 bots

> Every fighter is bright now, in its true colour, with an outline in that colour, on every look. Your own fighter has a white outline and a glowing ring under its feet in your colour. The ring starts big at each round's start and shrinks in 3 s. It is gone while you are down.

## Changes
- **1. The fighters are never darkened.** They moved from the lit world, which the dark layer turns down to about a third, to the never-darkened layer of the arrows and the purple walls (`world.gd`: `bodies`, the first thing there, so arrows, clouds and the walls still draw over them). Before, a fighter was only bright next to a torch.
- **2. An outline on every look** (`shaders/fighter.gdshader`, now on LOW and NORMAL too, not only HIGH). A dark edge pixel, then 2 pixels of the fighter's own colour, a bit lighter. Yours is white and 3 pixels.
- **3. The ring under your feet** (`world.gd`: `ring`, `ring_glow`, `set_me`): a ring and a soft glow in your colour at your fighter's feet, 1.6 times as big when the round begins, back to normal over 3 s, hidden while you are down. The game client tells the arena your seat and the phase each frame (`world.set_me(my_seat, phase)`).
- **4. A fix in the fighter shader.** In Godot 4, `COLOR` comes into `fragment()` already multiplied by the texture. The shader multiplied by it again, so every outline pixel got the texture's alpha 0 (HIGH's dark outline never showed, since P1) and the fighters' colours came out squared, so darker. It now multiplies by the sprite's own tint, carried from `vertex()`.
- **5. Gone:** the HIGH look's two-step light on the fighters (they are no longer lit), and their normal-map texture (`FIGHTER_N`).
