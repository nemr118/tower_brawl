# v0.2.0-p4.8 - Deathcam

**Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Phase:** before the next play night. The user: "bring the deathcam back: an accelerating montage of the kills the match winner performed with a climax of a slow motion final kill for the match win. VHS style replay like we did in the last version of tower brawl." Then: only at the match's end, no red ring; "a nice little pop as each kill happens"; "a skip button, so they can skip the montage for everyone and go back to the lobby". · **Harness:** the proofs below
**Report:** `tools/v2_deathcam_check.py`

> When a match ends, every screen plays the winner's kills like an old tape. The earlier kills speed up one after another, then the kill that won the match plays in slow motion. The camera punches in on each kill with a flash and a gold KILL!, and pushes in close on the last one. Anyone seated can press SKIP, and everyone goes back to the lobby.

## Changes
- **1. Who killed whom** (`sim/proto.gd` `E_KILL`, `sim/sim.gd`): a new event beside each death: a = the killer (255: the arena), b = the one who died. Snapshots carry it; the page records it.
- **2. The recorder** (`client/deathcam.gd`): every screen keeps the last 3 s of what the arena draws (its views, 20 a second, with their events); each kill keeps a clip, 3 s before and 0.8 s after, up to 6 per killer; a new match clears them.
- **3. The montage** at the match's end: the winner's kills, oldest first: up to 5 earlier ones (1.6 s of run-up each) from normal speed to 3.4 times, then the last: 2.2 s in time, slow (0.3 times) from 0.6 s before the kill to 0.3 s after, the rest a little slow, a fade. Playback follows each frame's own time, so a slow phone's clips play right. The winner wears the white outline and the ring while it plays.
- **4. The pop on each kill:** the camera punches in 25% toward the kill and eases back (`world.set_view_zoom`), a flash, and a gold KILL!; on the last kill the camera pushes in to 1.5 times and holds through the slow motion, a bigger flash, FINAL KILL.
- **5. The VHS look** (the old game's replay, `scripts/replay_player.gd`): a cool tint, scanlines, a rolling tracking bar, tape specks, PLAY or SLOW with the speed (PLAY x2.6) and a counter to the kill, REPLAY with a blinking dot, KILL 3 OF 6, "NAME WINS THE MATCH" over the first moments, MATCH POINT and "BOT 4 got BOT 7". No red ring.
- **6. SKIP** for everyone: a seated player's SKIP button (or Esc, space, enter on a PC) sends the JSON `skip`; the server ends the match's end at once ("[MATCH] seat 0 'Cam' skipped the montage: back to the lobby") and every screen goes back to the lobby.
- **7. The match's end lasts 12 s** (it was 7), so the montage fits; the winner's screen shows after it. A winner with no kill: no montage, the winner's screen as before.
