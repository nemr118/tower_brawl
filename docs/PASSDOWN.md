## How to work here
- **Git:** rule 9 (every build on `beta`, committed and pushed at each checked step; `main` only on "ship it").
- **Rules:** `CLAUDE.md` (rules 7 and 8; the new game's check and build in the orientation line). One approved phase at a time; report bugs outside it, don't fix them. **Human checks:** one short open sheet in `docs/` per game, a yes/no line per thing only a person can judge, one verdict; proofs Claude runs go in the patch page.
- **Verify the new game:** `check_scripts.gd`, then `input_probe.gd`, `power_probe.gd`, `arena_probe.gd` (the arena rules; `--rounds=200`, `--png=DIR`; `--write-routes` after an arena or move change), `bot_probe.gd` (`--level=...`, `--catch`, `--reach`, `--kid`), then a match: a server with `--no-tls --port 8095 --bots 7`, `play_probe.gd --start --bots=7 --watch=300` (`v2_synth.py --n 7 --bots 0` for 8 sockets; `--goal 1` for a quick end). `--lag=60 --jitter=30` on any probe (late inputs); `--predict` (the corrections); the catch test: [[Commands]]. On the laptop the service is `--quiet`: the tick is in `~/tower_brawl/bench_results/<date>/v2_server.jsonl`. Stop a test server by its PID (`$!`); `pkill -f` with its arguments kills your own shell too. The old game: `tools/check_scripts.gd -- --no-net` and the harness (25/25 with `--restart-each`).
- **Serve:** the Python service (`systemctl --user restart towerbrawl`) serves both pages; the new server runs by hand on the PC (the service file is for the laptop). Laptop: [[laptop-server]]. Deck: `tbdash` (the old game only).

## Architecture now
- **The new game (`bench/`, its own Godot project).** `sim/sim.gd`: the rules with no nodes (TowerFall's numbers x4.5, whole-pixel movers on a 30 px grid, arrows, stomps, one life, the purple walls, p4.9's power-ups; `apply_input` for a person and, through `sim/brain.gd`, a bot (OLD = p3.1's brain, EASY, NORMAL, HARD); `sim/routes.gd` + `routes.json`: the route map; `write_snapshot`, `write_fighter_state` for `T_OWN`; `predicting`, `fly_arrows` for the client's copies). `sim/proto.gd`: `VERSION`, the packets, the buttons, the phases, the events, the power-up kinds. `sim/level.gd`: the arenas, 1920 x 1080 with wrap (`LAYOUTS`: 0 the bench, the tests' default; 1 THE KEEP, 2 THE BRIDGES, 3 THE HAUNTED HOUSE (theme halloween) as text maps; `PLAY`; `stands`, `one_drop`); `sim.set_arena`. `server/game_server.gd`: seats, the lobby, the phases, points, bots fill, an input queue per seat, a network thread, wss 8091; round, match and power lines in `v2_server.jsonl`, the players' numbers in `v2_stats.json`; its options: [[Commands]]. `client/`: `game_client.gd` (the page, its options, keys, mouse aim, the look), `net.gd` (snapshots, the predicted clock `ticks_now`, the draw delay, `lag`), `predict.gd` (your fighter), `present.gd` (the arrows on your tick), `world.gd` (the look T1 to T3, `set_arena`; the fighters in the threats layer, outlined, your ring: `set_me`) + `powers_view.gd` (the power-ups, every look), `ghostface.gd`, `theme_view.gd`, `edges_view.gd`, `dress_view.gd`, `polish.gd` (a switch per look piece; `?polish_off=`), `boiler_view.gd` (THE BOILER ROOM, layout 4; `sim/boiler.gd` its rules), `lobby*.gd` + `winner_view.gd` + `award_art.gd` (p4.13), `fx.gd`, `hud.gd`, `lobby.gd`, `touch_sticks.gd`; `bench_client.gd` is the old benchmark page (`?bench`).
