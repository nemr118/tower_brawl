## Architecture in one screen
- `serve_game.py`: authoritative lobby/match state (slots, playing/waiting, alive, stocks, scores, rounds, 5-crown match end), relay for everything else. Harness gate (v0.0.23): `_harness_tick` reads `harness_state.json`, `harness_gate` dict, `join_locked` / `harness_status` packets, `_demote_humans`, `_release_seat`. Tagged log lines and a `status.json` picture once a second (`_build_status` / `status_loop`, v0.0.21) for `tools/watch_server.py`; since v0.0.22 it also carries kills, deaths, `rtt_ms` and the bot card per seat, and the match's kill timeline (`match_kd`, `match_timeline`, `player_bots`); since v0.0.24 the timeline has round end times, `ended_at`, `winner` and `names`; since v0.0.25 `player_died` carries `weapon` and `seats[].tape` holds the screen's `history_status` card (`player_tapes`, `[TAPE]` log lines); since v0.0.26 `round_end` carries `replay` and the gap after a round won on a kill is `REPLAY_ROUND_DELAY = 6.5` s (`MATCH_END_DELAY = 7.0`), the card's `replay` part is logged as `[TAPE] ... played the round N replay`. One reader thread per socket + one `ClientConn` writer thread with a bounded queue (movement dropped first when backed up; stalled peers dropped after ~5 s of a non-shrinking kernel backlog). TCP keepalive instead of app-level timeouts (hidden tabs keep their seat). Frame cap 4 KB, relay cap 1 KB. Sender stamped server-side. Client token gates slot reclaim; 8 s rejoin grace mid-match (since v0.0.28 a held seat counts as present when the next round starts, `_next_round_later`); 1.5 s forfeit grace on explicit leave; observer-authoritative deaths deduped per 1.5 s.
- `scripts/global.gd`: the only network code on the client. JSON for events, **binary for movement**: `sync_pos` 11 B `[type, sender, tick u16 (20 Hz slot clock), x*10 s16, y*10 s16, aim 0.1° u16, flags]` (flag bits: facing, dash, shield, bear, egg, feet-on-the-ground), `spawn_projectile` 9 B. Layout mirrored in `serve_game.py BIN_TYPES` and `tools/chaos_bots.py`. Signals out to `arena.gd` / `character_select.gd` (both connect from a table and disconnect in `_exit_tree`). Auto-rejoin after a reconnect, scene correction to the server state, combat state (ammo, tick) persisted for reloads. Frame-time telemetry (v0.0.27): counts drawn pictures and physics ticks between `NetStats` reports (`_ft_*`), printed as `fps draw= phys= worst= hitches=`. Keyboard probe (v0.0.28): `_input` counts key presses, `canvas_focused()` / `focus_canvas()` talk to the page, printed as `keys= focus=`; `current_round` follows the server since v0.0.28.
- `scripts/player.gd`: local sim + 20 Hz send with idle suppression on a slot-clock tick; remote puppets render from a 16-sample snapshot ring 100 ms behind the sender (`_render_snapshots`: seam-unwrapped, flags at render time, ≤ 200 ms extrapolation past the newest sample, 80 px teleport snap; Phase 3b); per-frame puppet-jitter telemetry into `Global.puppet_sample`; deaths reported by whoever saw the hit. `_physics_process` order since v0.0.29: shared timers, puppet branch (early return), local egg branch, local controls.
- `scripts/history_ring.gd` (v0.0.25, Phase 3c): the tape. 360 reused frame slots, one per physics tick; `next_frame` / `projectile_slot` / `commit` to record, `stamp` on a kill, `close_round` for the 60-frame tail and the freeze, `clear` per round, `status_card` (the `history_status` packet, with the `replay` part since v0.0.26) and `status_line` (the `📼 [Tape]` log line). `arena.gd` drives it (`_record_tape_frame`, `_tape_report`).
- `scripts/replay_player.gd` (v0.0.26): the VHS replay. `start(ring, platforms, live, round_end_msec)` pauses the live nodes and plays 211 frames around the closing kill (rewind, play, slow motion with a flash, the fall, stop); `stop(cut)` frees the ghosts, restores the live nodes and the platform angle and emits `finished(result)`; `status_line(ring, result)` is the `📼 [Replay]` line. Ghosts are `player.tscn` with `is_ghost` (see `player.gd` `_ready`) and the projectile scenes out of their group. `arena.gd` drives it (`_start_replay`, `_skip_replay`, `_stop_replay_now`, `_on_replay_finished`).
- `scripts/harness_ticker.gd`: the small test ticker panel (v0.0.23). Listens to `Global.net_harness_status`, hides when `active` is false. Lobby makes a 3-line one, arena a 1-line one.
- `scripts/bot_brain.gd`: AI opponent attached by `arena.gd` to the local fighter under `--ai=<persona>`. Perceives from the scene tree, acts with `Input.action_press/release` (runs before the fighter in the same physics frame via `process_physics_priority = -10`), aims with a synthetic `InputEventMouseMotion` (verified: 0.0° error). 10 Hz decisions through a reaction-delay queue, gaussian aim error, seeded RNG. Navigation: wrap-aware steering, rises beyond one jump use a jump + upward dash at the apex or a drop through the bottom seam, and an airborne bot over an empty column air-steers to its landing platform (`_landing_move`). Shared: inbound-projectile threat test, lead-shot solver, evade by dash. Personas: `wanderer`, `chaser` (knight), `sniper` (mage), `turtle` (knight), `rusher` (rogue), `griefer` (druid: seam drops and hops, powerup camping, dashes into projectiles, shield spam, bear toggles).
- `web/shell.html`: the web page that holds the game. Godot fills in its `$GODOT_*` blanks at export time (`html/custom_html_shell` in `export_presets.cfg`; `bump_build.sh` stops if the exported page lacks the helpers). It adds three phone helpers on top of the game: a "tap to play full screen" sheet (fill the screen, then ask the phone to turn sideways), a corner button to get full screen back, and a style-only "turn your phone sideways" sign.
- `tools/watch_server.py` + `tools/deck_input.py`: the operations deck (v0.0.21 dashboard, v0.0.22 deck, v0.0.24 keys and mouse). Reads the two JSON files only; `Deck` holds the view (zoom, pan, selection, filters, alerts, 60 s of ping and traffic), `build_strip` makes the fight strip (`tape_inspect` adds the tape state of a round-end column), `tape_text` fills the `Tape` seat column, `render_rich` / `render_plain` draw it with a height budget.
- `tools/chaos_bots.py`: protocol oracle + 23 scenarios + fuzz + fault injection + soak + `GodotClient` fleet runner + `StatePublisher` (`harness_state.json`, v0.0.22) + `ctx.warn` for MINOR results (parses the `🧠 [Bot` line: `fleet.bot-idle`, `fleet.bot-fall-loop`, `fleet.bot-missing`, `fleet.no-kills`, `fleet.no-round-end` at 150 s+; parses `puppets=`: `puppet_gate` → `fleet.puppet-jitter` / `fleet.puppet-snaps`, also on the `lag` scenario's Godot observer; `tape_gate` → `fleet.tape-missing` / `fleet.tape-fps`, and the `tape` scenario with `GodotClient.tapes()`, v0.0.25; `replay_gate` → `fleet.replay-missing / -skipped / -late / -frames-dropped / -duration`, and the `replay` scenario with `GodotClient.replays()`, v0.0.26; the `reload_in_gap` scenario with `GodotClient(tag=, keep_class=)`, `start(reclaim=)`, `assigns()`, `scene()`, `bot_moves()`, v0.0.28; the `ranger_rejoin` scenario with `GodotClient.quivers()` and `Bot.double_shot_ok`, v0.0.30).

15. ~~**A wanderer brain with its default class sends the same `spawn_projectile` twice within 20 ms.**~~ **Explained in v0.0.30:** `player.gd` reads `attack` and `special` in the same physics frame (`_physics_process`, the two `is_action_just_pressed` checks), and a ranger's attack and special both fire an arrow from `global_position + aim_dir * 18` along `aim_direction`, so the two 9 B packets are identical. The wanderer sets both in one decision now and then. A legit double shot (two arrows, two costs, one spot), not a relay bug. The harness counts it where a ranger brain plays (`Bot.double_shot_ok`, `ranger_rejoin`); `reload_in_gap` keeps its knight. Whether one frame should ever fire two overlapping arrows is a class-kit question (later-list item 3).
16. **The stomp-egg has no author over the network (found 2026-09-05 while fixing backlog 10):** only a head stomp turns a shielding druid into an egg, and a stomp is only detected on the stomper's client (`_check_head_stomp` runs in the local branch of `player.gd`). `take_hit` on a shielding druid sets `is_egg` on that node and returns before sending anything (no `player_died`, no other packet). So the druid's own client never enters egg form, and since v0.0.29 the puppet's egg flag follows the druid's own packets, which never say egg: the egg form is invisible in a networked match. Fix idea, a design call first: a small `player_hit` event from the observer (`{type, attacker, victim, weapon}`), relayed by the server like `player_died`; the victim's own client runs `take_hit` on itself (shield → egg, or death) and every other screen ignores it. Same path could report melee and stomp hits on non-shielding fighters, which today go through `player_died` from the attacker's screen.
