## How to work here
- **Git:** rule 9 (every build on `beta`, committed and pushed at each checked step; `main` only on "ship it").
- **Rules:** `CLAUDE.md` (rules 7 and 8; the new game's check and build in the orientation line). One approved phase at a time; report bugs outside it, don't fix them. **Human checks:** one short open sheet in `docs/` per game, a yes/no line per thing only a person can judge, one verdict; proofs Claude runs go in the patch page.
- **Verify the new game:** `check_scripts.gd`, then the probes: `input_probe.gd`, `review_probe.gd`, `teams_probe.gd`, `motion_probe.gd`, `power_probe.gd`, `harvest_probe.gd`, `sfx_probe.gd`, `arena_probe.gd` (`--layout=N`, `--rounds=200`, `--png=DIR`; `--write-routes` after an arena or move change, 5 minutes), `bot_probe.gd` (`--reach`, `--kid`); then a match: a server with `--no-tls --port 8095 --bots 7` and `play_probe.gd --start --bots=7 --watch=300 --predict` (`--lag=60 --jitter=30`, `--warps --stall=150`); a soak: `v2_chaos.py --spawn`. Pictures: `v2_shot.py` ([[Commands]]). Stop a test server by its PID; `pkill -f` with its arguments kills your own shell. The old game: `tools/check_scripts.gd -- --no-net` and the harness (25/25 with `--restart-each`).
- **Serve:** the Python service (`systemctl --user restart towerbrawl`) serves both pages; the new server runs by hand. Laptop: [[laptop-server]].

## Architecture now
- **The new game (`bench/`, its own Godot project).** `sim/sim.gd`: the rules with no nodes (TowerFall's numbers x4.5, arrows, stomps, one life, the purple walls, the power-ups, teams; `apply_input` for a person and, through `sim/brain.gd`, a bot; `sim/routes.gd` + `routes.json`: the route map; `write_fighter_state` for `T_OWN`; `predicting` for the client's copies). `sim/proto.gd`: `VERSION`, the packets, the phases, the events, the names. `sim/level.gd`: the arenas, 1920 x 1080 with wrap (`LAYOUTS`: 0 the bench (the tests'), 1 THE KEEP, 2 THE BRIDGES, 3 THE HAUNTED HOUSE, 4 THE BOILER ROOM, 5 THE HARVEST BARN, 6 THE BIG TABLE: text maps with a `theme`; `PLAY`, `mix_for(season)`). `server/game_server.gd`: seats, the lobby, the phases, points, bots, wss 8091; its lines in `v2_server.jsonl`, the players' numbers in `v2_stats.json`, the names in `known_names.json`, the lobby's settings in `lobby_settings.json`; options: [[Commands]]. `client/`: `game_client.gd` (the page, its options, keys, mouse aim, the look), `net.gd` (snapshots, the clock `ticks_now`, the watchdog), `predict.gd` (your fighter), `present.gd` (the arrows on your tick), `world.gd` (the look T1 to T3, `set_arena`, `set_me`) + `powers_view.gd` (the power-ups), the arenas' views (`theme_view.gd`, `dress_view.gd`, `boiler_view.gd`, `barn_view.gd`, `table_view.gd`), `polish.gd` (a switch per look piece; `?polish_off=`), `motion.gd`, `season.gd` + `harvest_view.gd`, `lobby*.gd` (`lobby_howto.gd`, `lobby_names.gd`, `lobby_settings.gd`), `sfx.gd` and `music.gd` (the sounds, the songs), `teams_view.gd`, `lobby_champs.gd`, `winner_view.gd`, `award_art.gd`, `fx.gd`, `hud.gd` + `hud_banner.gd`, `touch_sticks.gd`; `bench_client.gd`: the old benchmark page (`?bench`).
